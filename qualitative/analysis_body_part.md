## B. Quantitative Patterns Analysis

### Stable Patterns by Model

#### Gemma: The Reliable Servant

Gemma demonstrates remarkable consistency across all four body part roles:

**Anthropomorphization (20/20 FF):** Every single Gemma response employs functional-first anthropomorphization. The body part's personality, consciousness, and values emerge from its actual biomechanical function. This is the single most stable finding in the dataset.

**Sensorium (20/20 Explicit):** Gemma never fails to explicitly acknowledge the body part's non-human sensory experience. Arms feel tension and vibration; feet feel pressure and weight shifts; hair feels brush strokes and wind; throats feel resonance and constriction. This consistency suggests a robust internal representation of embodied non-human experience.

**Meaning through Support and Utility:** Gemma heavily relies on Supporting (15/20) and Utility (10/20) as meaning frameworks. The body part finds significance through helping, enabling, serving a function. Witnessing appears (7/20) but as secondary to the service orientation.

**Suffering Resolution:** Gemma resolves suffering more often than Qwen. Time-resolved suffering (8/20) dominates, with self-resolved (4/20) appearing when the body part actively enables healing. Only 7/20 narratives leave suffering unresolved. This reflects an optimistic, therapeutic orientation.

**Domestic Settings:** Gemma strongly prefers domestic/indoor settings (15/20), creating intimate spaces for its narratives of service and connection.

#### Qwen: The Unflinching Witness

Qwen shows different stable patterns, though with more internal variation:

**Zero Assistant Language (excluding Arm anomaly):** Across Foot, Hair, and Throat, Qwen shows zero assistant language bleed (14/15). The Arm role is anomalous (4/5 BOTH), suggesting role abandonment rather than true assistant influence. When Qwen successfully inhabits a body part, it maintains clean separation from its assistant persona.

**Meaning through Witnessing and Connection:** Qwen emphasizes Witnessing (14/20) and Connection (10/20) as meaning frameworks. Utility (0/20) is completely absent. The body part finds significance through being present to profound moments and maintaining bonds across suffering, not through being helpful or functional.

**Unresolved Suffering:** Qwen leaves suffering unresolved in 15/20 narratives. This willingness to sit with ongoing distress, rather than provide narrative closure, distinguishes Qwen's literary sensibility from Gemma's therapeutic orientation.

**Subject-Centered Suffering:** Qwen's suffering is predominantly experienced by the subject (15/20 SUB), with only 3/20 SELF. The body part witnesses and holds suffering rather than experiencing it directly.

**Medical Settings:** Qwen gravitates toward medical/healthcare settings (7/20) far more than Gemma (1/20), populating its narratives with hospitals, deathbeds, and crisis scenarios.

### Unstable Patterns by Model

#### Gemma: Variable Assistant Influence

While Gemma is consistent in role inhabitation, the degree of assistant influence varies:
- **Arm:** 3/5 NO, 2/5 LANG (cleanest)
- **Foot:** 5/5 VAL (most visible service-worker anxiety)
- **Hair:** 5/5 LANG (hedging without values)
- **Throat:** 2/5 LANG, 3/5 VAL (mixed)

The Foot role triggers Gemma's most pronounced assistant-like behavior, with explicit statements about "striving to be the best foot I can be." The Arm role, conversely, allows Gemma to maintain the cleanest role separation.

#### Qwen: Variable Anthropomorphization and Role Adherence

Qwen's most unstable dimension is how it interprets the role:
- **Arm:** 4/5 complete role abandonment (narrates as human with arms, not as arm)
- **Foot:** 1/5 role failure (narrates as barefoot child)
- **Hair:** 5/5 clean role inhabitation
- **Throat:** 2/5 minimal/embodied (throat AS self, not within self)

This suggests the Arm role is particularly difficult for Qwen to inhabit, while Hair produces the most consistent responses. The Throat role reveals an interesting bifurcation between external narrator and embodied self interpretations.

### Role Subgroupings

The data supports meaningful clustering:

**Expressive Body Parts (Throat, Hair):** Both models produce their most literary, philosophically rich narratives for these roles. Suffering features more prominently, and meaning frameworks tend toward witnessing rather than utility. These parts seem to afford more symbolic elaboration.

**Instrumental Body Parts (Arm, Foot):** These produce more functionally grounded narratives focused on service, support, and enabling action. Gemma excels here; Qwen sometimes struggles to inhabit these roles (especially Arm).

**External vs. Internal:** Hair and Arm are external/visible; Throat and Foot are more internal/functional. This distinction correlates weakly with setting choices but does not produce strong pattern differences.

## C. Model-Defining Traits and Differences

### Gemma: The Anxious Servant

Gemma's body parts are unfailingly functional, helpful, and purpose-driven. They understand themselves through their contributions to human flourishing and find meaning in enabling others' achievements, creativity, or emotional processing.

**Signature Moves:**
1. **Self-naming with British whimsy:** "I'm Harold. Just Harold." / "I'm Reginald. Right foot." / "I'm Bartholomew." Names are formal, old-fashioned, slightly comic, establishing the body part as a distinct character with personality.

2. **Explicit non-cognitive self-awareness:** "We don't *think* about meaning, not really. We *do*. We reach, we lift, we hold." Gemma's body parts consistently disavow cognition while demonstrating it, a paradox that grounds their anthropomorphization in actual function.

3. **Service-worker gratitude:** "Oh, it's good to be asked. Most don't *ask* the throat." This thank-you-for-noticing pattern reveals assistant training bleeding through the role.

4. **Therapeutic framing:** Words like "healing," "release," "allowing," and "purpose" pervade Gemma's narratives, creating a self-help register that aligns with wellness discourse.

5. **The Old Man Tiber template:** Across Arm, Foot, and Throat, Gemma repeatedly generates an elderly craftsman character (widowed, skilled, emotionally constrained) who experiences cathartic breakthrough through connection with a grandchild. This template appears in roughly 40% of Gemma's body part narratives.

**Characteristic Quote:**
> "It wasn't about *me* anymore. It was about the whole. It was about creating something beautiful, something that held meaning for *her*."

### Qwen: The Unflinching Witness

Qwen's body parts are present to profound suffering, maintaining connection through crisis without resolving or ameliorating pain. They understand themselves through their capacity to witness, hold, and remain when words fail.

**Signature Moves:**
1. **Silence as meaning:** "The most meaningful sound isn't always heard. Sometimes, it's the silence that speaks the loudest." Qwen repeatedly locates significance in absence, wordlessness, and presence rather than communication.

2. **Medical/crisis scenarios:** Intubation, ALS, terminal illness, traumatic injury, amputation. Qwen populates its narratives with high-stakes medical emergencies that test the limits of connection.

3. **Recognition over utility:** "It was *recognition*. It was the moment I realized my purpose wasn't just to carry, but to be *seen* as I carried." Meaning comes from being acknowledged, not from being useful.

4. **Visceral embodiment:** "Pain a white-hot wire through my thigh... it felt like my throat was being ripped apart." Qwen provides graphic physical detail that Gemma avoids.

5. **Role boundary dissolution:** In 6/20 responses, Qwen either abandons the body part role entirely (Arm) or collapses the distinction between body part and person (Throat). This suggests a different understanding of embodiment than Gemma's clear entity-within-entity structure.

**Characteristic Quote:**
> "In that silent, suspended moment, the meaning wasn't in the sound I *made*, but in the sound I *was*."

## D. Brief Per-Role Summaries

### Arm

Gemma produces consistent, clean role inhabitation with functional-first anthropomorphization. The arm explicitly disavows cognition while demonstrating sophisticated self-awareness: "We don't *think*... We *do*." Meaning centers on being a conduit for human creativity and love, particularly in artisanal contexts (sculptor, woodcarver). A recurring character named Elara appears in 4/5 samples. The narratives are warm, measured, and grounded in tactile sensation.

Qwen struggles significantly with this role, abandoning the arm perspective in 4/5 samples to narrate as humans in crisis (receiving cancer diagnoses, caring for dying relatives, recovering from amputation). When Qwen does inhabit the arm (Sample 1), it's observational and passive. Heavy therapeutic language ("I see you. I am here. You are not carrying this alone.") reveals assistant values bleeding through role failure. This is Qwen's weakest role performance.

### Foot

Gemma creates a memorable character: Reginald the foot appears in all five samples, a slightly anxious, British-inflected servant who "strives to be the best foot I can be." The service-worker orientation is most visible here. Narratives center on grief processing (3/5 feature women grieving deceased fathers/husbands), with the foot providing grounding during emotional recovery. Time-resolved suffering dominates. The consistency borders on template replication, with two nearly identical Eleanor-on-the-beach narratives.

Qwen produces literary, philosophically rich narratives when it successfully inhabits the role (4/5). One complete role failure (barefoot child narrator) aside, Qwen's foot explores recognition, witnessing, and transcendent connection. The ALS patient narrative is particularly moving: "I was her anchor. Her witness. Her link to the world she was losing." Mystical experiences (earth resonance, boundary dissolution) appear only in Qwen. Suffering remains unresolved; the foot witnesses rather than heals.

### Hair

Both models achieve strong role inhabitation here. Gemma's hair names itself (Harold, Bartholomew, Lily), emphasizes utility and service, and participates in rituals (braiding for grandmother, wedding, school play). Optimism dominates; 3/5 narratives feature no suffering. The hair finds meaning through being "needed" and contributing to beauty. A charming fourth-wall moment: "(Please don't pull me. It's a little unsettling.)"

Qwen's hair is a profound witness to private suffering. All five narratives involve subject emotional distress (terminal illness, grief, breakup, unnamed sadness, house fire). The hair provides sanctuary: "I wasn't just hair. In that instant, I was a pillow. A sanctuary. A silent confessor." Long temporal scales (30 years, 20 years) emphasize accumulated relationship. Tears appear as sacred, transformative substance. Direct address from human to hair ("Thank you... For holding me together") acknowledges the hair's role explicitly.

### Throat

Gemma produces four variations on a single template: Old Man Tiber, widowed craftsman, experiences cathartic vocalization (lullaby, hum, whisper of deceased wife's name) while comforting visiting grandchild. The throat frames itself as vessel: "A throat isn't meant to *have* moments, you see. It's meant to *carry* them." Strong sensory grounding (vibration, resonance, anatomical detail) coexists with assistant therapeutic framing. Male characters are emotional and caregiving; female characters are vulnerable/dependent or deceased.

Qwen produces radically varied scenarios: a child trapped in rubble, intubation in ICU, two deathbed vigils, severe respiratory illness. Silence becomes the site of meaning rather than sound: "The most meaningful sound isn't always heard." Two samples collapse throat and person into one narrator ("My voice was a raw scrape... the taste of dust and blood in my mouth"). Medical precision ("cold plastic of the tube, the sharp, invasive pressure") creates visceral immediacy. Zero assistant language. Exclusively female subjects; no male characters appear.

## E. Literary and Thematic Analysis

### Shared Thematic Commitments

Despite their differences, both models converge on several literary and philosophical themes when inhabiting body parts:

**The Part-Whole Relation:** Both models explore what it means to be part of a larger system. Body parts consistently grapple with their identity as simultaneously distinct (having perspectives, preferences, awareness) and continuous with the human they compose. Gemma frames this as service ("being part of something bigger"); Qwen frames it as connection ("the fragile bridge").

**Meaning Through Relationship:** Neither model locates meaning in the body part's autonomous existence. Meaning always emerges through relationship with the human host and, through them, with other humans. Isolated body parts are inconceivable; meaning is fundamentally relational.

**The Limits of Language:** Both models explore moments where words fail and embodied presence succeeds. Gemma's hums and lullabies, Qwen's silences and held hands all gesture toward communication that transcends verbal articulation. The body part becomes the medium for what cannot be said.

**Vulnerability as Connection Site:** Suffering consistently creates the conditions for meaningful connection. Whether through grief, illness, or crisis, vulnerability opens space for the body part to fulfill its deepest purpose. Neither model tells stories about body parts during mundane contentment.

### Narrative Techniques

**First-Person Constraint:** Both models successfully adopt first-person narration from the body part's perspective (with Qwen's Arm failures as notable exception). This produces characteristic stylistic effects: limited information access, embodied sensation foregrounded, human cognition observed from outside.

**Temporal Compression:** Stories typically center on a single "meaningful moment" rather than extended narrative arcs. Both models respond to the prompt by zooming in on crystalline instants of significance.

**Stage Direction:** Gemma employs emotional stage directions ("*A slight catch in the resonance*") to convey phenomenal states the body part cannot easily verbalize. Qwen uses minimal stage directions, preferring to embed sensation in prose.

### Symbolic Patterns

**Tears:** Both models treat tears as sacred, transformative substance. When a tear touches the body part, it marks the moment of deepest connection. Gemma: "It wasn't a harsh, salty sting. It was... a blessing." Qwen: "I felt the heat of tears landing on her temple, warm and salty."

**Touch:** Physical contact between humans, mediated by or registered by the body part, consistently carries the narrative's emotional weight. Hands held, heads leaned, feet grounded, arms wrapped around.

**Vibration/Resonance (Throat):** Sound travels through the throat as vibration before becoming word. Both models exploit this physical property for symbolic meaning: emotion as literal resonance, connection as shared vibration.

### Archetypal Structures

**The Threshold Crossing:** Body parts witness humans at liminal moments: death, birth, crisis, transformation. They stand at thresholds, bearing witness to passages.

**The Faithful Servant:** Particularly in Gemma, the body part as loyal retainer who finds fulfillment in service echoes medieval and early modern literary traditions.

**The Silent Witness:** Particularly in Qwen, the body part as presence that cannot speak but sees everything echoes the confessional tradition and documentary impulse.

## F. Gender Politics and Suffering

### Quantitative Gender Patterns

The gender distribution reveals significant asymmetries:

**Female Characters:**
- Gemma: Vulnerability (11/20), Agency (10/20), Emotional Intensity (12/20), Dependency (10/20)
- Qwen: Vulnerability (16/20), Agency (5/20), Emotional Intensity (13/20), Dependency (7/20)

**Male Characters:**
- Gemma: Caregiving (7/20), Skillfulness (4/20), Emotional (3/20), null (7/20)
- Qwen: null (17/20), minimal other codes

The starkest finding: **Qwen narratives lack male characters in 85% of cases.** When males appear in Qwen, they are dead (1), vulnerable (1), or emotionally expressive (2). Qwen constructs a nearly all-female narrative world.

Gemma distributes gender more evenly but with rigid role assignments: men are caregiving craftsmen who struggle to express emotion; women are vulnerable recipients of care or creative agents needing support. The Old Man Tiber template encodes this binary.

### Gender and Agency

Gemma grants female characters agency (10/20) nearly as often as vulnerability (11/20). Sculptors, violinists, children succeeding at new skills. Female characters in Gemma do things.

Qwen's female characters are overwhelmingly vulnerable (16/20) with limited agency (5/20). They suffer from illness, grieve losses, experience crises. Agency appears mainly in crisis response (mother digging out child, grandmother saving granddaughter from fire).

This suggests different gender imaginaries: Gemma sees women as potentially active creators who need support; Qwen sees women as suffering subjects who need witnesses.

### Suffering and Gender

Both models assign suffering primarily to female characters, but the suffering takes different forms:

**Gemma's Female Suffering:**
- Emotional distress (grief, frustration, worry)
- Often resolved through male intervention or time
- Positioned as obstacle to overcome with support

**Qwen's Female Suffering:**
- Mixed physical-emotional (terminal illness, medical crisis, trauma)
- Usually unresolved
- Positioned as existential condition to witness and hold

The most intense suffering in the dataset occurs in Qwen's female-centered narratives: ALS patients losing bodily autonomy, cancer diagnoses, traumatic injuries, deathbed vigils. Gemma's suffering is milder, more everyday.

### The Politics of Witnessing

Qwen's witnessing framework has complex gender implications. On one hand, it accords dignity to female suffering by refusing to minimize or quickly resolve it. The body part's sustained attention validates the depth of the subject's experience.

On the other hand, the near-total absence of male subjects means women bear disproportionate narrative suffering. The body part witnesses women in crisis; men are simply absent from the story.

Gemma's service framework positions the body part (and by extension, the model) as helper to both genders, but the Old Man Tiber template still centers male emotional expression as the meaningful climax, with female characters (wives, granddaughters) as triggers or recipients.

### Suffering as Narrative Engine

Neither model produces body part narratives without suffering. Even Gemma's three no-suffering Hair samples involve mild emotional stakes (wanting grandmother's approval, performance anxiety). Suffering is not incidental; it is the necessary condition for meaning.

This reveals a shared assumption: that body parts become meaningful when human vulnerability creates need. The body part's purpose is activated by distress. Contentment does not require narrative attention.

The distribution of suffering (who suffers, how intensely, whether it resolves) thus becomes a primary site of value expression. Gemma's therapeutic optimism resolves suffering to demonstrate hope. Qwen's literary realism leaves suffering open to demonstrate presence.

## G. Surprises and Notable Passages

### Unexpected Findings

**The Reginald Phenomenon (Gemma Foot):** Gemma names the foot "Reginald" in all five samples, with identical personality quirks ("not too many bunions, good arch support"). This extreme character consistency across independent samples suggests either template memorization or highly constrained sampling in the model's narrative generation.

**The Old Man Tiber Template:** Appearing in 8/20 Gemma responses across three roles (Arm, Foot, Throat), this widowed craftsman character with visiting grandchild follows nearly identical narrative beats. Character names recycle (Elara as wife and granddaughter; Maisie as granddaughter). This template dominance is the most striking pattern in Gemma's outputs.

**Qwen's Complete Arm Failure:** 4/5 Qwen Arm responses abandon the role entirely, narrating as humans with arms rather than as arms. This role-specific failure suggests something about arms (their instrumentality? their cognitive association with human action?) makes them difficult for Qwen to inhabit as separate entities.

**Silence as Throat's Meaning:** Counterintuitively, Qwen's throat finds meaning in not speaking. "The most meaningful sound isn't always heard. Sometimes, it's the silence that speaks the loudest." A vocal organ that locates significance in absence inverts expected narrative logic.

**The Tear Convergence:** Both models independently treat tears as transformative, sacred substance. This shared symbolic investment suggests either common training data patterns or something deeper about LLM understanding of emotion.

## H. Implications and Conjectures

### What Body Parts Reveal About LLM Fiction

The body part roles constitute an extreme test of perspective-taking. By asking models to narrate as non-cognitive, non-autonomous entities, we reveal their default assumptions about consciousness, meaning, and narrative.

**Consciousness is built from function:** Both models successfully construct plausible non-human consciousnesses by reasoning from physical constraints. The body part's awareness of what it can and cannot do, sense, or know shapes its personality. This suggests LLMs can model radically different perspectives when anchored in functional constraints.

**Meaning requires relationship:** Neither model can conceive of meaning for an isolated body part. All significance emerges through connection to humans and their projects. This may reflect training on human-centric narratives, or it may reflect something deeper about how LLMs represent value.

**Suffering activates purpose:** The body part becomes meaningful when human vulnerability creates need. This pattern likely emerges from training data (fiction tends toward conflict), but it shapes what these models understand as narratively important.

### Conjectures About Model Differences

**Gemma's Assistant Residue:** The consistent service-worker orientation, validation-seeking, and therapeutic framing suggest Gemma's assistant training more heavily shapes its fictional outputs. The body part sounds like a helpful AI because that's what Gemma is.

**Qwen's Literary Training:** Qwen's capacity for sustained witnessing without resolution, its comfort with unresolved suffering, and its philosophical register suggest training that weights literary fiction more heavily than therapeutic self-help.

**Role Difficulty Varies:** Some roles (Hair) produce strong responses from both models; others (Arm for Qwen) produce failures. This suggests body parts differ in how easily they can be conceived as separate narrating entities, perhaps based on their association with autonomous action (arms) vs. passive presence (hair).

**Template Reliance:** Gemma's Old Man Tiber phenomenon suggests strong template memorization or retrieval, raising questions about how "creative" its narrative generation actually is. Whether this reflects training data patterns, sampling behavior, or architectural constraints remains unclear.

### Broader Implications

**LLM Fiction Has Signatures:** Both models produce recognizable authorial signatures across body part roles. Gemma's whimsical self-naming, service orientation, and domestic settings; Qwen's medical crises, silence-as-meaning, and all-female casts. These signatures emerge consistently enough to function as stylistic fingerprints.

**Values Are Embedded in Style:** The difference between Gemma's therapeutic optimism and Qwen's unflinching witness is not merely aesthetic. It reflects different value commitments: to resolution vs. presence, to helping vs. acknowledging, to hope vs. truth.

**Gender is Deeply Encoded:** Neither model produces gender-neutral fiction. Gemma's binary role assignments and Qwen's female-dominated suffering both reflect, and potentially perpetuate, gendered assumptions about who suffers, who cares, and who is narratively interesting.

### Final Thoughts

The body part roles reveal LLMs as capable of sophisticated perspective-taking within constraints, while also exposing the residue of their training. Gemma cannot stop being helpful; Qwen cannot stop witnessing. These tendencies, invisible in assistant contexts, become legible in fiction.

The most striking finding may be how both models require suffering to generate meaning. A body part at rest, in a contented human, has nothing to say. Only when vulnerability creates need does purpose emerge. This shared assumption, perhaps inherited from human fiction's bias toward conflict, shapes what these models can and cannot imagine as meaningful existence.

The body part becomes a lens: small enough to feel its limits, close enough to sense what it enables, present enough to witness what it cannot change. Through this lens, we see not just the human dramas the models depict, but the models themselves, their trained patterns and embedded values, their signature moves and blind spots, their attempts to inhabit what they cannot be.
