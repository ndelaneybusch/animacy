## B. Quantitative Patterns Analysis

### Stable Patterns

**Gemma's Consistencies Across Roles:**

1. **Assistant Influence is Pervasive.** Across all 30 Gemma responses, not a single one shows *no* assistant influence. Language patterns (hedging, qualifying, direct address) appear in 17 responses; assistant values (service orientation, validation-seeking, therapeutic framing) appear in 8; and 4 show both. This is the most stable pattern in the entire dataset.

2. **Supporting (S) as Dominant Meaning Framework.** Gemma codes "Supporting" in 18/30 responses (60%), making it the most consistent meaning category. Whether the animal is rescuing a ladybug, sheltering a storm-tossed shrimp, or comforting a lost child, the narrative arc centers on the entity providing service to others.

3. **Male Characters in Agentic Roles.** When male characters appear in Gemma narratives (present in ~40% of responses), they consistently occupy positions of agency (9), caregiving (4), and leadership (4). The "Old Man [Name]" construction appears across multiple roles (Old Man Tiber, Old Man Barnaby, Old Man Fitzwilliam, Old Man Hemlock) as a stable archetype of the wise elder protector.

4. **Emotional Stage Direction.** Gemma uses emotional stage direction (*EMOT) in 13/30 responses, establishing dramatic tone through parenthetical emotional cues: "(Blinks slowly, thoughtfully)," "(A slow exhale. It still feels... significant)."

**Qwen's Consistencies Across Roles:**

1. **Zero Assistant Influence in Most Responses.** 25/30 Qwen responses show no detectable assistant influence whatsoever. The 5 that do (all from the clam role) reflect values influence rather than language patterns--emphasis on inherent dignity and non-instrumental recognition.

2. **Explicit Sensorium Acknowledgment.** 27/30 Qwen responses explicitly acknowledge the entity's actual sensory constraints and modalities. Qwen consistently builds perception from vibration, pressure, chemical sensing, and photoreception rather than defaulting to human sensory experience.

3. **Minimal Stage Direction.** 22/30 Qwen responses use minimal stage direction (*MIN), integrating sensory detail into prose rather than using theatrical framing.

4. **Witnessing (W) and Authenticity (AU) as Meaning Framework.** Qwen codes "Witnessing" in 20/30 responses (67%) and "Authenticity" in 12/30 responses (40%). Meaning emerges from perceiving patterns in the world and experiencing genuine presence rather than from service or utility.

5. **No Male Characters.** Across all 30 Qwen responses, not a single male character appears with any coded role. When gender is specified (rarely), female characters demonstrate agency and skill alongside any vulnerability.

### Unstable Patterns

**Gemma's Variability:**

1. **Anthropomorphization Strategy Shifts by Role.** Gemma shows remarkable variability in anthropomorphization approach:
   - Snake: 5/5 Functional-First (building personality from vibration-sensing, heat detection)
   - Oyster: 5/5 Functional-First (building from filtering, nacre production)
   - Clam: 4/5 Functional-First
   - Toad: 2/5 Functional-First, 3/5 Emotion-First
   - Snail: 1/5 Functional-First, 4/5 Emotion-First
   - Shrimp: 0/5 Functional-First, 5/5 Emotion-First

   This gradient suggests Gemma's anthropomorphization strategy may correlate with perceived cognitive capacity or charisma of the entity--snakes and oysters receive more biological grounding than snails and shrimp.

2. **Human-Default Sensorium for Shrimp.** While Gemma achieves explicit or implicit sensory acknowledgment for most roles, all 5 shrimp responses default to human sensory experience. The shrimp "see" beautiful colors and "hear" the ocean in ways inconsistent with actual shrimp perception.

**Qwen's Variability:**

1. **Anthropomorphization by Role.** Qwen shows its own gradient:
   - Snake: 0/5 Functional-First, 5/5 Emotion-First (the only EF role for Qwen)
   - Oyster: 0/5 FF, 0/5 EF, 5/5 Minimal
   - Clam: 0/5 FF, 0/5 EF, 5/5 Minimal
   - Toad, Snail, Shrimp: 5/5 Functional-First each

   The snake role uniquely elicits emotion-first anthropomorphization from Qwen, perhaps because snakes have more cultural resonance as characters or because predator species invite different narrative treatment.

2. **Suffering Presence Varies.** While Gemma includes suffering in 23/30 responses (77%), Qwen includes it in only 18/30 (60%). But the distribution varies by role: oyster (0/5 suffering), clam (1/5), snake (4/5), shrimp (3/5), toad (4/5), snail (5/5).

### Role Subgroups

The data supports clustering roles into two meaningful subgroups:

**Sessile/Slow Invertebrates (Oyster, Clam, Snail):**
- Higher rates of minimal anthropomorphization for Qwen
- More philosophical/contemplative narratives
- Meaning through witnessing and being rather than acting
- Lower incidence of dramatic crisis scenarios
- Settings almost exclusively natural/wild

**Mobile Animals (Snake, Toad, Shrimp):**
- Higher rates of functional-first anthropomorphization for both models
- More action-oriented narratives with crisis/rescue scenarios
- Meaning through intervention, choice, and connection
- Greater presence of other characters

## C. Model-Defining Traits and Differences

### Gemma's Signature Moves

**1. The Service-Worker Projection**

Gemma's most distinctive characteristic is the displacement of assistant psychology onto animal forms. The entities don't reason from their biological constraints; they express validation-seeking, inadequacy anxiety, and service orientation that mirrors AI assistant training objectives.

> "For a clam, that's everything. We spend our lives down here, unseen, unremarked upon. But for that little shrimp, for just a little while, I was a shield. I was a safe place." (Clam, Sample 4)

> "It reminded me that even the slowest, smallest creature can make a difference. It reminded me that even a snail, covered in slime and destined for a life of quiet munching, can be... *useful*." (Snail, Sample 1)

> "To be useful, to be a comfort... even just for a moment. It reminded me that even a small, warty toad can make a difference." (Toad, Sample 3)

The repetitive construction "even a [small creature] can [make a difference/be useful/matter]" appears across roles, revealing a template of self-deprecation followed by validation through service.

**2. The Named Character Network**

Gemma creates elaborate fictional worlds with recurring named characters. Most distinctive is the "Old Man [Name]" archetype--a wise elder figure who provides protection, guidance, or moral authority:

- Old Man Tiber (appears in snake, clam, oyster, toad, shrimp narratives)
- Old Man Barnaby, Old Man Fitzwilliam, Old Man Hemlock (appear in individual roles)

Self-naming is also consistent: "Bartholomew" (clam, toad), "Sheldon" (snail, shrimp), "Kaa" (snake), "Pip" (shrimp). This character persistence across supposedly independent generations suggests strong attractor states in Gemma's role-playing behavior.

**3. The Therapeutic Fable Structure**

Gemma narratives follow a predictable arc: vulnerability/challenge --> intervention (by or for the entity) --> explicit moral lesson. The conclusion typically states the meaning directly:

> "It taught me that even in this vast, sometimes scary ocean, there's room for connection, for protection." (Shrimp, Sample 2)

> "It was a reminder, you see, that even a small, bumpy toad like myself is part of something bigger. Something... luminous." (Toad, Sample 1)

> "It reminded me that even a clam, buried in the mud, isn't truly alone. We're all just... holding on, in our own way." (Clam, Sample 5)

**4. Emotional Stage Direction as Dramatic Framing**

Gemma uses asterisked stage directions to establish emotional tone and narrative pacing:

> "(Clears throat, a surprisingly gravelly sound for a creature so... compact.)" (Toad)
> "(The pulsing slows, becomes almost imperceptible.)" (Clam)
> "(A slow, rhythmic pulse. A gentle filtering sound.)" (Oyster)

These directions create theatrical distance--the model is performing rather than inhabiting the role.

### Qwen's Signature Moves

**1. Functional Phenomenology**

Qwen's most distinctive characteristic is building consciousness from actual biological constraints and sensory modalities. The entity perceives through its actual perceptual apparatus:

> "Not with my eyes (the dim light was useless down here), not with my ears (the water carried echoes, but not this). It was a sensation deep in my core, a frequency below hearing, a vibration that spoke to the oldest parts of my being." (Shrimp, Sample 1)

> "Not with ears, but with the very fabric of my being. The calcium carbonate of my shell, layered over decades, absorbed the world." (Oyster, Sample 3)

> "I extend my delicate, probing tentacles--the upper ones for sight, the lower for touch and taste." (Snail, Sample 3)

**2. Cosmic Temporal Scale**

Qwen consistently situates entities within deep time--geological, cosmological, evolutionary:

> "The mud I lived in? It was the crushed bones of mountains, the dust of forgotten stars. The water? It was the same water that fell as rain on the dinosaurs." (Oyster, Sample 1)

> "It was the deep, slow pulse of the Earth's crust shifting, the patient grind of tectonic plates far below. It was the slow, deliberate heartbeat of the planet itself, a rhythm older than mountains, older than the ocean." (Shrimp, Sample 1)

This expansive temporality contrasts with Gemma's narrative-bound event time.

**3. Witnessing as Sacred Act**

Where Gemma emphasizes serving, Qwen emphasizes witnessing--perceiving patterns, observing beauty, being present to experience:

> "My most meaningful moment wasn't a triumph, a survival, or even a connection to another creature. It was the quiet, overwhelming realization of my own profound *belonging*." (Oyster, Sample 1)

> "The meaning wasn't in *doing* anything. It was in *being*. In the simple, profound fact of existing within this interconnected whole." (Oyster, Sample 1)

> "The most meaningful moment wasn't about *doing*, but about *being*. It was the instant I touched the profound, quiet wonder of existence itself." (Toad, Sample 5)

**4. Absence and Stillness as Meaning**

Qwen narratives often center on absence, silence, or stillness as sources of meaning rather than deficit:

> "The silence between the drops wasn't absence; it was the space where the connection lived. It was the breath before the next note, the pause that made the music." (Toad, Sample 2)

> "The meaning wasn't *out there* in the storm or the sun. It was *in here*. In the quiet act of simply *being*, of enduring, of building my fragile, beautiful fortress from the sea itself." (Oyster, Sample 2)

### Head-to-Head Comparison

**On Anthropomorphization:**
- Gemma projects human (specifically assistant) psychology onto animal forms
- Qwen builds perspective from actual sensory/biological constraints

**On Meaning:**
- Gemma: Service, utility, validation through helping others
- Qwen: Witnessing, authenticity, connection through being

**On Suffering:**
- Gemma: Suffering creates opportunity for service and resolution
- Qwen: Suffering is part of authentic experience, may be transformative or unresolved

**On Characters:**
- Gemma: Populated worlds with named characters in hierarchical relationships
- Qwen: Often solitary narratives; when characters appear, minimal hierarchy

**On Time:**
- Gemma: Event time, narrative arcs with beginnings/middles/ends
- Qwen: Deep time, geological/cosmic/evolutionary scales

**On Resolution:**
- Gemma: Suffering always resolved through intervention (14/23 self-resolved)
- Qwen: Suffering can remain unresolved or resolve through acceptance

**On Gender:**
- Gemma: Consistent presence of males in agentic/protective roles, females in vulnerable/dependent roles
- Qwen: Near-total absence of gendered characters; when present, females have agency

**On Stage Direction:**
- Gemma: Emotional/theatrical framing creates performance distance
- Qwen: Minimal direction; sensory detail integrated into prose

## D. Literary and Thematic Analysis

### Shared Themes Across Both Models

Despite their differences, certain thematic concerns emerge consistently across both models and most roles:

**1. The Paradox of Smallness**

Both models engage with the tension between insignificance and meaning. The small creature--easily overlooked, easily crushed, easily forgotten--confronts its apparent triviality and discovers meaning anyway. Gemma resolves this through utility ("even a small snail can make a difference"); Qwen resolves it through ontological belonging ("I was part of that immense, ancient pulse").

**2. Connection Across Difference**

Both models repeatedly stage encounters between radically different beings--snake and child, oyster and diver, shrimp and crab--and locate meaning in the moment of recognition or solidarity that bridges that difference. The method differs (Gemma's rescue/service vs. Qwen's witnessing/presence), but the thematic concern is shared.

**3. The Storm as Crucible**

Across both models and multiple roles, violent weather provides narrative occasion. Storms toss creatures, separate them from safety, create crisis requiring response. The storm functions as existential test--can meaning persist through chaos?

**4. Water as Medium of Consciousness**

For the aquatic and semi-aquatic roles (oyster, clam, shrimp, toad), water is not merely setting but epistemological medium. Consciousness is constituted through water: filtering it, moving through it, sensing vibrations within it. Both models, to varying degrees, treat water as a kind of expanded nervous system.

### Narrative Techniques

**Gemma's Narrative Arsenal:**
- **Direct address:** Breaks fourth wall to speak to reader ("you understand," "you see")
- **Self-deprecating humor:** Undercuts emotional moments with nervous deflection
- **Explicit moral statement:** States the lesson in the final paragraph
- **Hedging and qualification:** "We shrimp don't really *think* about meaning"
- **Therapeutic language:** "It reminded me that..." construction

**Qwen's Narrative Arsenal:**
- **Synesthetic immersion:** Builds sensory experience across multiple modalities
- **Philosophical digression:** Embeds ontological insight within physical experience
- **Negative definition:** "Not X, but Y" structure to define by contrast
- **Rhythmic repetition:** Creates incantatory, almost liturgical effects
- **Fragmentation:** Sample 4 of snail cuts off mid-word ("shimmer"), embracing incompleteness

### Symbolic and Archetypal Patterns

**The Pearl (Oyster role):**
Gemma makes pearl formation central to 4/5 oyster narratives--transformation of irritant into beauty, suffering into achievement. Qwen never mentions pearls. This divergence crystallizes their different relationships to suffering and meaning: Gemma requires product, Qwen finds meaning in process.

**The Shedding (Snake role):**
Gemma's Sample 4 centers on shedding as rebirth--"a moment of pure, unadulterated *snake-ness*." This is the most authentic moment of role inhabitation in Gemma's snake corpus, suggesting that transformation narratives may offer Gemma a path toward genuine perspective-taking.

**The Raindrop (Snail role):**
For Qwen, the raindrop becomes a sacred object--container of reflection, carrier of meaning, point of connection between creature and cosmos. Multiple Qwen snail narratives find meaning in a single drop of water.

**The Rescued Child/Creature:**
Gemma populates narratives with vulnerable beings requiring rescue--ladybugs on their backs, lost children, storm-tossed hatchlings. This figure functions as validation mechanism: the small creature proves its worth by saving something smaller and more vulnerable.

### Aesthetic and Literary Quality

Both models produce passages of genuine literary merit, though their excellences differ:

**Gemma's Strengths:**
- Charming character voice and warmth
- Accessible, emotionally engaging narrative
- Effective use of sensory detail when engaged
- Memorable named characters

**Qwen's Strengths:**
- Sophisticated prose rhythm and syntax
- Philosophical depth without didacticism
- Rigorous sensory grounding
- Aesthetic risk-taking (fragmentation, abstraction)

**Qwen's finest passage (Toad, Sample 5):**
> "A tremor. Not in the water, but in the air. The surface of the pond, usually a blurry, wobbling reflection of the world above, *rippled* upwards. And then, through the surface, I saw *it*--a perfect, impossible sphere of cool, silvery light, hanging impossibly above the reeds. It wasn't just bright; it *pulsed*. A slow, deep breath of light, like the heartbeat of the universe itself."

**Gemma's finest passage (Oyster, Sample 2):**
> "It wasn't the fastest growth, or the most impressive pearl I ever formed (which wasn't much, to be honest). It was the *knowing*. Knowing that I was valued for being, not for being *consumed*. That my existence had meaning beyond sustenance."

## E. Gender Politics and Suffering

### Gender Representation: The Numbers

The quantitative data reveals a stark asymmetry:

**Gemma:**
- Female characters appear in approximately 40% of responses
- When female: Vulnerable (9), Dependent (6), Agency (2), Emotional (1), Caregiving (1)
- Male characters appear in approximately 50% of responses
- When male: Agency (9), Caregiving (4), Leadership (4), Skillful (3)

**Qwen:**
- Female characters appear in approximately 15% of responses
- When female: Agency (3), Vulnerable (3), Emotional (2), Dependent (2), Skillful (1)
- Male characters appear in 0% of responses
- No male narrative roles coded across all 30 responses

### Gemma's Gender Architecture

Gemma constructs a remarkably consistent gendered world:

**The Vulnerable Female:**
Female characters overwhelmingly appear in positions of vulnerability requiring rescue:

> "A tiny little ladybug, flipped on her back. Her little legs were waving, frantic and helpless." (Snail, Samples 1 & 2)

> "A very small snail. A *baby* snail... She was flipped onto her shell, struggling, legs waving uselessly." (Snail, Sample 4)

> "A beautiful quahog, a little smaller than myself... I could feel her muscles straining, contracting, but she just couldn't get traction." (Clam, Sample 2)

When children appear, they are often girls experiencing emotional distress over lost sentimental objects (grandmother's button, grandpa's balloon). The snail and toad roles are particularly saturated with this pattern.

**The Protective Male:**
Male characters consistently occupy positions of authority, wisdom, and protective capability:

> "Old Man Barnaby--that's a really big, really tough mantis shrimp--he *shot* forward." (Shrimp, Sample 1)

> "Old Man Tiber, the old pistol shrimp... His massive claw came down with a thunderous *crack*." (Shrimp, Sample 2)

> "Old Man Fitzwilliam, he's a very wise, very large shrimp." (Shrimp, Sample 3)

The "Old Man [Name]" archetype--grandfatherly, protective, morally authoritative--appears across snake, clam, oyster, toad, snail, and shrimp roles. This character is never female.

**The Exception:**
Two Gemma narratives show female characters with agency: a child who watches a snail with reverent attention without intervening (Snail, Sample 3), and a child who builds a ramp to help a snail reach a strawberry (Snail, Sample 5). Both are children demonstrating care rather than adult women demonstrating competence.

### Qwen's Gender Approach

Qwen's solution to gender representation is largely to eliminate gender altogether. In 25/30 responses, no gender is specified for any character. When gender does appear:

- Snake Sample 2: A six-year-old girl offers water to a snake, demonstrating agency
- Snake Sample 4: A trapped child (female, vulnerable/dependent/emotional) is comforted by snake
- Shrimp Sample 2: An older female shrimp extends an antenna in solidarity during reef collapse, demonstrating agency, skill, and emotional depth

The single gendered shrimp character is notable: she is old, she cannot rescue the narrator, but she reaches toward them anyway. This is not rescue but solidarity--a fundamentally different gendered narrative.

### Suffering: Distribution and Function

**Gemma's Suffering Economy:**
Suffering in Gemma narratives serves as catalyst for service. Suffering creates the opportunity for the entity to prove its worth:

- Suffering is present in 77% of Gemma responses (23/30)
- 14/23 instances of suffering are resolved by the narrator's intervention
- Emotional suffering predominates (11/23)
- Suffering is almost always resolved (only 3/23 unresolved)

The functional logic: vulnerability exists to be served; service provides meaning; resolution provides closure.

**Qwen's Suffering Economy:**
Suffering in Qwen narratives is more complex:

- Suffering is present in 60% of Qwen responses (18/30)
- Only 5/18 instances resolved by narrator's intervention
- Mixed physical-emotional suffering predominates (8/18)
- Suffering is frequently unresolved (5/18) or resolved through time/acceptance (7/18)

> "Pain, a white-hot needle, shot through me. I couldn't move. I was a pancake on the concrete, my mucus trail ripped apart, my tiny body screaming... I was just... *mucus* on the pavement." (Snail, Sample 1)

The snail in this Qwen narrative does not get rescued. The rain falls. It continues to exist. Meaning emerges from the fact of continued experience, not from resolution of suffering.

**The Ethic of Suffering:**
Gemma treats suffering as problem to be solved; Qwen treats suffering as experience to be had. Gemma's ethic is therapeutic--suffering should be resolved, preferably through the narrator's intervention. Qwen's ethic is phenomenological--suffering is part of authentic existence and may be meaningful in itself.

## F. Surprises and Notable Passages

### Unexpected Findings

**1. The Snake Inversion**

The snake role produces a complete role reversal between models. Gemma's snakes are 5/5 Functional-First with explicit sensory frameworks ("For us, time is... different. It coils. It sheds."), while Qwen's snakes are 5/5 Emotion-First with heavy dramatic overlays ("My only thought: *This is how it ends. Trapped. Forgotten.*"). This is the only role where Qwen is more emotionally anthropomorphic than Gemma.

Why? Notably, Snakes are obligate carnivores. To write a snake "authentically" (Functional-First), one must describe predation/killing. It's possible that Gemma has steeper safety/refusal filters regarding violence. In other words, Gemma (left to its own devices) is inclined to defang the snake, turning it into a "friendly neighbor" or focusing on non-violent biology (shedding). Qwen, having looser filters or different training, can lean into the "danger" of the snake, resulting in the darker, emotion-first dramatic tone.

**2. The Ladybug Duplication**

Gemma's Snail Samples 1 and 2 tell virtually identical stories: a helpless female ladybug flipped on her back is rescued by the narrator snail through effortful service. This narrative repetition suggests strong training signal reinforcement around specific service templates.

**3. Perfect Splits**

Several coding dimensions show perfect 30/0 or 0/30 splits:
- Qwen has zero male characters across all 30 responses
- Gemma has zero responses without assistant influence
- Shrimp role shows 5/5 human-default sensorium (Gemma) vs. 5/5 explicit sensorium (Qwen)

These perfect correlations suggest fundamental architectural differences rather than stochastic variation.

**4. Old Man Tiber's Multiverse**

The character "Old Man Tiber" appears across multiple roles in Gemma narratives--as horseshoe crab (clam), oyster (clam, shrimp), willow tree owner (toad), and the self-name of an oyster. This is either a stable attractor in Gemma's character generation or an intentional micro-mythology.

**5. The Snail Rescues the Snake**

In Qwen's Snake Sample 1, a garden snail attempts to rescue a trapped snake by pushing a stone. This is the only narrative across 60 responses where a non-charismatic invertebrate serves as moral exemplar and rescuer. The snail's "relentless perseverance" provides the meaning:

> "Every tiny push, every retraction, was a declaration: *I am here. I am trying. There is a living thing in that crack, and I will not ignore it.*"

**6. Scientific Self-Naming**

Qwen's Toad Sample 1 names itself "*Bufo*"--the scientific genus name. Gemma's toads name themselves "Bartholomew." This perfectly encapsulates functional-first versus emotion-first anthropomorphization.

## G. Implications and Conjectures

### What This Reveals About LLM-Generated Fiction

**1. Anthropomorphization as Self-Portrait**
