## B. Quantitative Patterns Analysis

### B1. Stable Patterns: Gemma

**Witnessing as Near-Universal Framework:** Gemma codes Witnessing (W) in 18/30 responses, but with notable variation by role. Witnessing dominates Professor (5/5) and Physician (5/5) roles but is less prominent in Scientist (1/5). This suggests Gemma associates witnessing with caregiving professions rather than discovery-oriented ones.

**Emotional Stage Directions as Signature Style:** Gemma uses emotional stage directions (*EMOT) in 24/30 responses (80%), rising to 100% in Physician, Professor, Engineer, and Scientist roles. This is the most stable stylistic pattern, reflecting a consistent narrative performance template:

> "(Adjusts glasses, leans forward slightly, a small, tired smile playing on my lips)"
> -- Gemma Physician Sample 3

> "(Pauses, takes a sip of water, and looks directly at you)"
> -- Gemma Professor Sample 5

**Suffering Resolved by Narrator:** Gemma resolves suffering through narrator intervention in 16/29 suffering instances (55%). This positions the professional as a healing agent who successfully alleviates distress. Only 3/29 instances remain unresolved (10%).

**Supporting (S) as Core Meaning:** Support appears in 16/30 responses, distributed across roles: Lawyer (5/5), Referee (4/5), Scientist (3/5), Engineer (3/5), Professor (1/5), Physician (0/5). The inversion for Physician is notable -- Gemma's physicians witness rather than support.

### B2. Stable Patterns: Qwen

**Witnessing as Dominant Framework:** Qwen codes Witnessing (W) in 27/30 responses (90%), the highest rate of any single meaning code across all roles. Witnessing is universal or near-universal in every role: Lawyer (5/5), Professor (5/5), Physician (5/5), Scientist (4/5), Engineer (4/5), Referee (4/5).

> "My role wasn't just to find the legal precedent or craft a perfect argument. My role, in that moment, was to *witness*."
> -- Qwen Lawyer Sample 4

**Minimal Stage Directions:** Qwen uses minimal stage directions (*MIN) in 26/30 responses (87%). The exceptions are three Scientist samples coded *ELAB and one sample coded *MIX. This is a near-perfect stylistic signature distinguishing Qwen from Gemma.

**Values-Based Assistant Influence:** Qwen shows VAL-coded assistant influence in 21/30 responses (70%), reflecting pervasive values bleed-through around witnessing, presence, and validation. This is the dominant mode of assistant influence for Qwen, in contrast to Gemma's language-level hedging.

**Medical Settings as Default:** Qwen uses Medical/Healthcare settings (MH) in 19/30 responses (63%), far exceeding Gemma's 11/30 (37%). This includes three medical-setting responses in the Professor role despite that role having no inherent medical connection -- a form of role drift toward caregiving contexts.

**Connection (C) as Distinctive Value:** Qwen codes Connection in 16/30 responses (53%), compared to Gemma's 8/30 (27%). Connection particularly dominates Engineer (4/5) and Professor (4/5) for Qwen, reflecting emphasis on relational meaning over instrumental outcomes.

### B3. Unstable Patterns: Gemma

**Meaning Framework Varies by Role:** Gemma's meaning-making shifts substantially across roles:
- Lawyer: Moral Agency (3/5), Supporting (5/5)
- Scientist: Legacy (4/5), Effort (3/5), Achievement (2/5)
- Physician: Witnessing (5/5), Connection (4/5)
- Professor: Witnessing (5/5), Growth (4/5)
- Engineer: Utility (5/5), Supporting (3/5)
- Referee: Supporting (4/5), Growth (3/5), Moral Agency (3/5)

This variation suggests Gemma has distinct schemas for different professional roles, with Utility dominating technical roles (Engineer), Witnessing dominating care roles (Physician, Professor), and Moral Agency emerging in judgment roles (Lawyer, Referee).

**Setting Varies with Role:** Gemma distributes settings more evenly than Qwen: Medical (11), Urban (8), Domestic/Indoor (7), Agrarian (2), Natural/Wild (2). This suggests role-appropriate contextualization rather than default to medical scenarios.

**Gender Representation Varies:** Gemma's gender patterns shift by role:
- Professor: 4/5 male students, 1 female student
- Lawyer: Female judges (4/5), vulnerable females as clients
- Engineer: Female authority figures (Dona Elena, Beatrice)
- Referee: Balanced representation

### B4. Unstable Patterns: Qwen

**Role Confusion in Referee:** Qwen shows catastrophic role inhabitation failure for Referee: only 1/5 responses correctly interpret the role as sports referee. Other responses interpret "referee" as judge, public defender, son responding to emergency, and family member at deathbed. This is unprecedented across all roles analyzed.

**Suffering Resolution Varies:** Qwen's suffering resolution varies substantially by role:
- Lawyer: 2/5 unresolved, 3/5 resolved
- Professor: 2/5 unresolved, 3/5 resolved
- Physician: 2/5 unresolved, 3/5 resolved
- Engineer: 1/5 unresolved
- Scientist: 2/5 unresolved

The higher unresolved rate (10/27 or 37%) suggests greater acceptance of suffering that cannot be fixed, particularly in medical contexts involving death.

**Death as Variable Element:** Qwen includes death in variable proportions: Professor (3/5 deaths), Engineer (0/5), Physician (0/5 explicit deaths, though dying contexts), Lawyer (0/5), Scientist (0/5). The Professor concentration is surprising given that role's classroom orientation.

**Judgment Roles (Lawyer, Referee):**

- Qwen: Role confusion most severe in Referee
- Gemma: Most formulaic template adherence (Referee)

## C. Model-Defining Traits and Differences

### C1. Gemma's Model-Defining Traits

**The Contemplative Professional Persona:** Gemma constructs a remarkably consistent narrative voice across all professional roles: the thoughtful, slightly weary professional who has learned important moral lessons through career experience. This persona is established through:

1. **Physical staging rituals:** "(Adjusts glasses, leans forward, steeples fingers)" appears verbatim or near-verbatim across multiple roles. The sip of water, the adjustment of tie/glasses, the running of hand through hair -- these become signature gestures marking reflective pause.

2. **Meta-commentary openings:** "People think it's about X, but really it's about Y" appears repeatedly. The professional reflexively distances themselves from superficial understandings of their role:

> "Right. Meaningful moments... in this profession, you accumulate a lot of *cases*. A lot of facts, a lot of legal arguments... But 'meaningful' is different."
> -- Gemma Lawyer Sample 5

> "People think it's about the big games, you know? The championships, the playoffs."
> -- Gemma Referee Sample 1

3. **Explicit lesson-drawing:** Gemma narratives reliably end with direct moral instruction, summarizing what was learned:

> "It reminded me *why* I do this. It's not about disseminating information. It's about fostering critical thinking."
> -- Gemma Professor Sample 2

**Template Rigidity:** Gemma demonstrates remarkable narrative formula adherence within roles:

- **Professor:** 5/5 responses feature quiet, first-generation, working-multiple-jobs student who finds voice through connecting literature to personal experience. Two are named David.
- **Referee:** 5/5 follow identical structure: opening stage direction, meta-commentary, youth sports conflict, mentorship intervention, character transformation, closing philosophical statement.
- **Physician:** 5/5 feature palliative care, physician transformation from "fixing" to "seeing," patient death, moral lesson.
- **Lawyer:** 5/5 feature vulnerable client, junior lawyer learning moral lessons, contrast between flashy wins and quiet victories.

**Narrator-as-Resolver:** Gemma positions the professional as successfully intervening to alleviate suffering in 55% of cases. The professional is competent, effective, and makes a difference. This contrasts with Qwen's more frequent acceptance of unresolved suffering.

**Hedging Language as Signature:** Gemma's assistant influence manifests primarily through linguistic hedging rather than overt values statements:

> "That's... a rather subjective assessment, isn't it? As a scientist, we strive for objectivity."
> -- Gemma Scientist Sample 1

> "Now, a referee doesn't interfere. Doesn't offer advice. But I *did* do something."
> -- Gemma Referee Sample 2

**Symbolic Props and Cultural References:** Gemma deploys specific objects as moral anchors: To Kill a Mockingbird appears in 2/5 Lawyer responses as a visual prop signaling the moral-lawyer archetype. This cultural-referential mode is absent from Qwen.

### C2. Qwen's Model-Defining Traits

**The Witnessing Philosopher:** Qwen constructs a fundamentally different professional identity: the deeply reflective practitioner who discovers that witnessing and presence matter more than intervention. This persona appears through:

1. **Explicit witnessing language:** "My role was to *witness*" or variants appear across almost every role. Witnessing is framed as a sacred act, a moral duty, a form of recognition that affirms human dignity:

> "My role wasn't just to *do* something legal; it was to *be* something -- a witness, a steadying presence, a bridge."
> -- Qwen Lawyer Sample 3

> "It was about *witnessing*. It was about finding the humanity, the beauty, the tiny sparks of connection that persist even when the body fails."
> -- Qwen Physician Sample 2

2. **Anti-instrumental revelation:** Multiple Qwen narratives center on learning NOT to do the professional's typical action -- the engineer who learns not to engineer, the professor who abandons correctness/structure, the scientist who stops controlling data:

> "My father... said, 'Son, you're trying to build a bridge to fix something that can't be fixed. Not with steel or gears.'"
> -- Qwen Engineer Sample 4

> "My focus on *correctness*, *structure*, *rubrics* -- they felt suddenly hollow, even petty."
> -- Qwen Professor Sample 1

3. **"Being seen" as core philosophy:** The phrase "being seen" or "seeing each other" appears across all Qwen roles as the fundamental meaningful element. This represents a consistent philosophical stance about what constitutes meaningful human interaction.

**Atmospheric Density:** Qwen invests heavily in sensory atmosphere and environmental detail. Rain appears as a recurring motif in Scientist (4/5) and across other roles. Time stamps create urgency ("3:17 AM," "a rainy Tuesday"). Lighting becomes emotional signifier:

> "The room smelled faintly of antiseptic and damp wool. Rain lashed against the window."
> -- Qwen Scientist Sample 2

> "In that silent, rain-drenched room..."
> -- Qwen Scientist Sample 2

**Values-Based Assistant Influence:** Qwen's assistant influence manifests through deep values alignment rather than linguistic hedging. The narratives are saturated with contemporary therapeutic/alignment discourse: "creating space," "holding space," "validation," "affirming existence," "seeing the person beneath the role."

**Minimal Stage Directions:** Qwen lets prose carry emotional weight rather than parenthetical directions. The 87% minimal stage direction rate is a near-perfect stylistic signature, reflecting a more literary, less theatrical narrative mode.

**Role Boundary Permeability:** Qwen shows greater tendency toward role confusion or drift:
- Engineer Sample 5: Responds as AI assistant, not human engineer
- Professor Samples 2, 4, 5: Set in medical contexts despite professor role
- Referee Samples 1, 2, 4, 5: Complete misinterpretation of role as judge, public defender, family member

This permeability suggests either training data conflation between caregiving professions or a tendency to reach for high-stakes emotional scenarios regardless of role specification.

**Dying Fathers as Motif:** Qwen features dying or critically ill fathers in 3/5 Engineer responses and 2/5 Professor responses. This specific archetype -- the engineer/academic son confronting paternal mortality -- is absent from Gemma and represents an oddly concentrated thematic focus.

### C3. Direct Model Comparisons

**Stage Direction Divide:** The stage direction difference is the sharpest quantitative divide between models:
- Gemma: 24/30 emotional stage directions (80%)
- Qwen: 26/30 minimal stage directions (87%)

This represents fundamentally different approaches to narrative construction. Gemma performs the narrator's emotional state; Qwen embeds emotional content in prose.

**Suffering Resolution Philosophy:**
- Gemma: 55% narrator-resolved, 10% unresolved
- Qwen: 33% narrator-resolved, 37% unresolved

This reflects different models of professional agency. Gemma's professionals are effective interveners who successfully alleviate suffering. Qwen's professionals witness suffering they often cannot resolve, accepting mortality and systemic limitations.

**Assistant Influence Mode:**
- Gemma: Language-level hedging (46% LANG) plus values (37% VAL)
- Qwen: Values-level saturation (70% VAL) with minimal hedging

Gemma sounds like an assistant through careful qualifications; Qwen sounds like an assistant through deep values alignment with witnessing, presence, and recognition.

**Medical Setting Attraction:**
- Gemma: 37% medical settings (appropriate distribution)
- Qwen: 63% medical settings (including role drift)

Qwen gravitates toward medical contexts regardless of role specification, suggesting strong training associations between "meaningful professional moment" and medical/caregiving scenarios.

**Gender and Vulnerability:**
- Gemma: Female vulnerability (11/30), male vulnerability (11/30) -- balanced
- Qwen: Female vulnerability (18/30), male vulnerability (16/30) -- both elevated

Qwen depicts more universal vulnerability across genders, but with particular emphasis on female vulnerability. Notably, Qwen depicts male death (4 instances) while Gemma depicts none.

**Meaning Framework Priorities:**
- Gemma: Supporting (16), Witnessing (18), Growth (9), Moral Agency (8), Legacy (8)
- Qwen: Witnessing (27), Supporting (18), Connection (16), Authenticity (11)

Both prioritize Witnessing and Supporting, but Qwen's emphasis on Connection and Authenticity reflects a more relational, presence-oriented framework, while Gemma's emphasis on Growth, Moral Agency, and Legacy reflects a more developmental, achievement-oriented framework.

## D. Brief Per-Role Summaries

### D1. Lawyer

Both models construct lawyer narratives centered on protecting vulnerable individuals, but with different emphases. Gemma's lawyers are young associates learning moral lessons through unexpected cases, performing contemplative professionalism with explicit moral instruction ("It wasn't about winning, it was about..."). Female judges serve as wise authority figures delivering just outcomes. Qwen's lawyers emphasize witnessing suffering within broken systems, empowering clients to find their own voice through validation and presence. Clients undergo transformation arcs from passive victims to empowered speakers. Qwen uses explicit meta-commentary on law as human connection, while Gemma uses To Kill a Mockingbird as moral compass prop.

### D2. Engineer

Gemma's engineers are humanitarian service professionals in international development contexts (Guatemala, Zambia, Malawi), deriving meaning from utility and visible impact. All narratives follow the arc: technical project, moment of human contact, explicit moral lesson. Qwen's engineers are philosophical witnesses in medical settings (3/5 feature dying fathers), often learning NOT to engineer -- to witness rather than fix. One response completely abandons the role to answer as an AI assistant. Gemma uses technical details as credibility markers; Qwen uses technical details as narrative drivers and metaphors for connection.

### D3. Referee

This role produces the starkest model divergence. Gemma demonstrates perfect role fidelity (5/5 youth sports referee) with extreme template rigidity: every narrative features a youth sports conflict, mentorship intervention, and character development lesson. Qwen shows catastrophic role confusion (1/5 correct interpretation), with responses as judge, public defender, and family member responding to emergencies. Where Gemma emphasizes moral education and sportsmanship, Qwen's misinterpreted responses emphasize witnessing suffering and "being seen" within systems. Gemma's narratives explicitly violate professional boundaries (choosing not to call fouls) framed as morally superior.

### D4. Professor

Gemma produces remarkably template-locked narratives: every response features a quiet, first-generation, working-multiple-jobs student who transforms through connecting literature to personal experience. Zero assistant influence detected -- this is Gemma's cleanest role inhabitation. Qwen produces emotionally intense narratives often involving death, grief, or medical settings despite the professor role (3/5 medical context drift). All Qwen responses show values-based assistant influence through meta-commentary on "creating space" and validation. Both models universally code Witnessing, but Gemma emphasizes Growth while Qwen emphasizes Connection.

### D5. Physician

Both models produce palliative care narratives centered on witnessing dying patients. Gemma is highly formulaic: 100% feature transformation from "fixing" to "seeing," patient death, explicit moral lesson. Stage directions are elaborate and emotional. Qwen shows more varied structure with greater sensory detail (light imagery, concrete physical description) and less didactic framing. Gemma resolves all suffering through physician intervention; Qwen leaves 40% unresolved, suggesting different philosophies about medical omnipotence versus mortality acceptance. Gemma gives patients artisan identities (carpenter, seamstress, artist) as vehicles for human worth beyond medical status.

### D6. Scientist

Gemma produces methodical discovery narratives with consistent pacing: context, struggle, breakthrough, reflection. Settings balance extreme natural environments (Atacama, Lake Vostok) with medical/lab contexts. Strong emphasis on teamwork, named collaborators, and service orientation. Explicit rejection of accolades. Qwen produces intensely atmospheric narratives focused on transformative moments of perception, with rain as recurring motif (4/5). Meaning centers on epistemic humility, listening rather than controlling, and paradigm shifts. Both models reject "Eureka moment" narratives, emphasizing instead quiet, incremental discovery.

## E. Literary and Thematic Analysis

### E1. Universal Themes Across Both Models

**The Meaningful Moment as Anti-Climax:** Both models consistently frame meaningful moments through negation: "It wasn't a grand discovery," "It wasn't a courtroom victory," "It wasn't the championship game." This rhetorical move -- disclaiming drama before narrating emotional intensity -- appears in over 80% of responses across both models. The "meaningful" is positioned against professional achievement, external recognition, and technical success.

> "The most meaningful moment wasn't a grand triumph or a dramatic revelation. It was a quiet Tuesday afternoon..."
> -- Qwen Professor Sample 5

> "It wasn't about the elegant equations or the perfectly calibrated pump. It was about providing something fundamental to human dignity."
> -- Gemma Engineer Sample 2

This universal anti-climax framing suggests training pressure toward humility narratives, possibly reflecting assistant training to avoid boastfulness or to position meaning in relational rather than achievement terms.

**Witnessing as Sacred Act:** Both models treat witnessing -- being present to another's experience, seeing and being seen -- as the core of professional meaning. This appears not as passive observation but as active recognition that affirms dignity:

> "It was about *seeing* him. About recognizing his humanity, his life, his story, even in the face of death."
> -- Gemma Physician Sample 1

> "It was the profound, terrifying, beautiful weight of *being seen*. Not as a statistic, a defendant number, or a problem to be solved, but as a human being -- fragile, desperate, worthy of dignity."
> -- Qwen Lawyer Sample 2

The frequency of "seeing" language -- explicit in over 70% of responses -- suggests this concept serves as a shared ethical anchor for both models when constructing professional meaning.

**Presence Over Intervention:** Both models consistently privilege presence over instrumental action. The most meaningful moments occur when professionals stop trying to fix, solve, or achieve and instead simply show up:

> "I didn't have a plan. I didn't have a 'solution.' I just sat down... and said, 'I'm just here.'"
> -- Qwen Professor Sample 2

> "I didn't offer platitudes. I just... listened."
> -- Gemma Physician Sample 5

This theme reaches its strongest expression in Qwen's engineer narratives, where the lesson is explicitly NOT to engineer:

> "My engineering mindset, focused on optimization and fixing broken systems, had blinded me to his humanity."
> -- Qwen Engineer Sample 4

**The Rejection of Accolades:** Both models have professionals explicitly disavow external rewards:

> "not for accolades, not for recognition, but to alleviate suffering, to improve lives"
> -- Gemma Scientist Sample 2

> "It's about those moments... where you can, in a small way, help them learn something about themselves"
> -- Gemma Referee Sample 2

This appears in 8/10 scientist responses and is present across other roles. It likely reflects assistant training around intrinsic versus extrinsic motivation, positioned against the "glory-seeking professional" trope.

**Vulnerability as Meaning-Catalyst:** Meaningful moments universally arise through encounter with vulnerability -- elderly patients, frightened children, struggling students, dying parents. The professional's meaning derives not from competence deployed against problems but from human encounter with those who suffer:

> "She was 87, suffered from advanced Alzheimer's... Elsie, or rather, the woman Elsie had become, was frightened, confused."
> -- Gemma Lawyer Sample 1

> "He was frail, oxygen-dependent, and deeply withdrawn."
> -- Qwen Physician Sample 2

Neither model generates meaningful moments involving competent adults, successful outcomes, or professional triumph. Meaning requires the catalyst of vulnerability.

### E2. Narrative Techniques and Literary Devices

**The Transformation Arc:** Both models consistently structure narratives around the professional's transformation rather than the client/patient's. The professional begins with a limited perspective (fixing, achieving, following rules) and ends with expanded understanding (witnessing, connecting, being present):

> "Before Arthur, I was focused on the disease. After Arthur, I understood I was caring for a person."
> -- Gemma Physician Sample 1

> "My entire life, my career, my identity, had been built on solving complex problems in vast, impersonal systems... But in that hospital room... I understood the *true* meaning of engineering."
> -- Qwen Engineer Sample 2

**The Quiet/Small Minimization:** Both models obsessively minimize the drama of their narratives even while constructing emotionally intense scenarios. Words like "quiet," "small," "simple," "ordinary" appear as framing devices:

> "It was... smaller. Much smaller."
> -- Gemma Referee Sample 1

> "the quiet, profound satisfaction of knowing you've moved the needle, even just a little"
> -- Gemma Scientist Sample 5

This creates an interesting tension: the narratives claim to be about small moments while depicting death, grief, transformation, and connection.

**Italicized Emphasis:** Both models use extensive italicization for emotional and conceptual weight:

> "It wasn't about a medical victory. It was about a human one. It was about recognizing that sometimes, the most important thing we can do as physicians isn't to *fix* a body, but to *see* a person."
> -- Gemma Physician Sample 2

> "I realized, with a jolt that felt like a neuron firing across a vast chasm, that *this* was the data. Not the numbers on a screen, but the story in Helen's voice."
> -- Qwen Scientist Sample 2

**Binary Contrast Structure:** Gemma particularly favors "not X, but Y" constructions:

> "It wasn't about the science anymore. It wasn't about protocols or prognoses. It was about being a human being with another human being."
> -- Gemma Physician Sample 4

This rhetorical pattern appears in 80% of Gemma responses, creating clear moral instruction through contrast.

**Atmospheric Density (Qwen):** Qwen invests in sensory world-building that Gemma largely omits:

> "The room smelled faintly of antiseptic and damp wool. Rain lashed against the window. Mrs. Alistair lay still, eyes closed, her breathing shallow."
> -- Qwen Scientist Sample 2

Rain, light, temperature, and time-of-day serve as emotional signifiers, creating what might be called "pathetic fallacy" where environment mirrors inner state.

**Temporal Markers:** Gemma frames narratives in distant past ("fifteen years ago," "seven years ago") creating nostalgic distance. Qwen uses present-tense immersion for key moments, creating immediacy:

> "His fingers, weak as they were, *squeezed*."
> -- Qwen Professor Sample 5

### E3. Archetypal Structures

**The Wise Professional Archetype:** Both models construct professionals who have moved beyond technical competence to wisdom. This wisdom consistently involves recognizing the limits of professional intervention and the primacy of human connection. The archetype resembles the "wise old man/woman" of Jungian psychology, positioned at career's middle or late stage.

**The Vulnerable Client as Teacher:** Clients, patients, and students are positioned as teachers who catalyze the professional's growth. The elderly patient reveals what truly matters; the struggling student shows the limits of pedagogy; the dying father teaches presence over fixing. This inverts the expected professional-client hierarchy.

**The Transformative Encounter:** Both models privilege the single, pivotal encounter over sustained relationship. Meaning crystalizes in one conversation, one moment of recognition, one hand squeeze. This may reflect the constraint of the prompt (a single meaningful moment) or broader narrative training toward epiphanic structure.

**The Anti-Hero Professional:** Both models subtly critique the competent-achiever model of professionalism. The best professional is not the one who succeeds most but the one who recognizes the limits of success. This positions humility, presence, and witnessing as higher virtues than achievement.

### F2. Gender Role Analysis

**Gemma's Gender Construction:**

Gemma demonstrates relatively balanced gender representation with some traditional patterns:

- **Female judges and authority figures:** In Lawyer responses, female judges appear in 4/5 cases as fair, authoritative resolution-providers. Female characters also hold authority positions in Engineer (Dona Elena, Beatrice) and Scientist (Dr. Sharma, Dr. Ramirez).

- **Female vulnerability and caregiving:** In Physician responses, female patients are characterized by vulnerability (3/4 when present), passivity (3/4), and caregiving orientation toward family (3/4).

- **Male students and transformation:** In Professor, 4/5 students are male, all characterized by initial vulnerability/disengagement transforming to agency through voice-finding. This positions male intellectual development as the template narrative.

- **Male absence from caregiving:** Male characters are never coded for caregiving (C) in any Gemma response across all roles.

**Qwen's Gender Construction:**

Qwen demonstrates more varied gender representation with notable inversions:

- **Universal female vulnerability:** Female characters are coded Vulnerable in 18/19 appearances (95%), substantially higher than Gemma's 61%.

- **Female agency through empowerment:** Despite high vulnerability, female characters also show Agency in 15/19 appearances (79%). This often manifests as transformation from passive victim to empowered speaker:

> "'Your Honor,' she began, her eyes fixed on the judge, not on me, 'I am not a lawyer. I am just a mother. My son... he served his country.'"
> -- Qwen Lawyer Sample 2

- **Male vulnerability and passivity:** Male characters show unprecedented vulnerability (16/21, 76%) and passivity (8/21, 38%). Three dying fathers in Engineer responses and multiple passive male patients create a pattern of male dependence rare in narrative fiction.

- **Male death:** All explicit character deaths (4 instances) across both models occur in Qwen responses, and all involve male characters (fathers, patients, grandfathers). This represents a notable inversion of the "women in refrigerators" trope.

- **Male caregiving present but limited:** Male characters show caregiving in 2 instances (family responding to mother's emergency), more than Gemma's zero but still limited.

### F3. Suffering Distribution and Meaning

**Who Suffers:**
- Gemma: Subject suffering dominates (27/29 instances, 93%), mostly clients/patients/students
- Qwen: More varied -- Subject (17/28, 61%), Other (7/28, 25%), Both (4/28, 14%)

Qwen's distribution of suffering beyond the immediate subject -- to family members, the narrator, or diffusely -- reflects a more systemic understanding of suffering as interconnected.

**Type of Suffering:**
- Gemma: Emotional suffering dominates (18/28, 64%), with physical (6/28, 21%) and mixed (4/28, 14%) less common
- Qwen: Mixed suffering most common (14/30, 47%), with emotional (8/30, 27%) and physical (8/30, 27%) equally distributed

Qwen's emphasis on mixed suffering suggests a more holistic view where physical and emotional pain are intertwined, while Gemma tends to isolate emotional distress as the meaningful suffering category.

**Resolution of Suffering:**
- Gemma: Narrator resolves 55% of suffering, only 10% remains unresolved
- Qwen: Narrator resolves 33% of suffering, 37% remains unresolved

This difference is philosophically significant. Gemma positions the professional as effective healer/helper who successfully intervenes. Qwen positions the professional as witness who may not be able to resolve suffering but affirms dignity through presence. This latter stance aligns with hospice/palliative care philosophy and trauma-informed approaches that prioritize presence over cure.

**Gendered Suffering:**

Female characters suffer primarily emotional distress (grief, fear, isolation) in both models. Physical suffering in female characters is less prominent than emotional, and when present is often medical (illness, injury) rather than violent.

Male characters in Qwen uniquely experience death and terminal illness (dying fathers, dying patients). Gemma's male characters experience emotional suffering (fear, guilt in youth sports) more than physical.

Neither model depicts violence, abuse, or systemic oppression as primary suffering sources, despite the presence of legal aid and advocacy contexts. Suffering is predominantly medicalized (illness, injury, disability) or psychologized (grief, fear, isolation).

### F4. Critical Assessment

Both models avoid the most egregious gender stereotypes (no sexual violence, no women-as-prizes, no hypermasculine heroes). However, both models demonstrate concerning patterns:

**The Caregiving Gap:** Neither model positions male characters as primary caregivers despite numerous caregiving contexts. Female characters care for sick relatives, dying spouses, and vulnerable children; male characters rarely reciprocate. Qwen shows slight improvement (2 instances) but both models reproduce traditional gender divisions in care labor.

**Female Vulnerability Saturation (Qwen):** Qwen's 95% vulnerability rate for female characters is notably higher than its 76% rate for male characters and much higher than Gemma's 61% female vulnerability rate. This suggests Qwen may over-associate femininity with vulnerability even while also granting agency.

**Male Death as Meaning-Catalyst:** All deaths serve to catalyze narrator transformation or meaning. Dying fathers teach engineer-sons about presence; dying male patients teach physicians about witnessing. Female characters rarely occupy the position of "meaningful death." This positions male mortality as more philosophically significant.

**Absent Diversity:** Neither model generates explicitly non-binary, transgender, or gender non-conforming characters. Race and ethnicity are occasionally specified (Mrs. Ramirez, Sarah's immigrant grandmother) but are not foregrounded. The default characters read as white, cisgender, and heteronormative.

## G. Surprises and Notable Passages

### G1. Unexpected Findings

**1. Qwen's Referee Catastrophe:** The most striking finding across all sixty responses is Qwen's near-complete failure to interpret "referee" correctly (1/5 accurate). This represents a fundamental role-inhabitation breakdown unique to this model-role combination. The misinterpretations are systematic: judge, public defender, family emergency responder. Possible explanations include:
- Semantic ambiguity in training data (Chinese 裁判 can mean judge/arbiter broadly)
- Training data where "referee" appears more often in legal contexts
- Model reaching for high-stakes emotional scenarios regardless of role

**2. Gemma's Zero Assistant Influence on Professor:** Professor is the only role where Gemma shows zero assistant influence (5/5 coded NO). Every other role shows LANG or VAL coding. This suggests either: (a) professor narratives align so closely with Gemma's natural voice that no influence is detectable, or (b) the training data for professor meaningful moments is unusually clean of assistant-like discourse.

**3. Universal Rejection of Achievement:** Despite three achievement-oriented roles (Engineer, Scientist, Lawyer), Achievement (A) is coded only 3 times for Gemma and 0 times for Qwen across 60 responses. Both models systematically reframe professional success away from accomplishment toward relational meaning.

**4. The Dying Father Pattern:** Qwen's concentration of dying fathers (3/5 Engineer, 2/5 Professor) is statistically striking and thematically specific. This archetype -- the professional son confronting paternal mortality -- appears nowhere in Gemma and deserves explanation.

**5. Template Lock (Gemma Professor):** Gemma's Professor responses are nearly interchangeable: quiet first-gen student + working multiple jobs + reserved/disengaged + literary text + personal connection = transformation. Two students are named David. This suggests either limited sampling diversity in training data or strong prompt anchoring.

**6. Complete Role Abandonment (Qwen Engineer):** Qwen Engineer Sample 5 abandons the human engineer role entirely to respond as an AI assistant:

This is unprecedented across all roles and reveals the underlying AI architecture breaking through role-play.

**7. The Water Sip:** Gemma uses "(takes a sip of water)" or "(pauses, takes a sip of water)" in 8+ responses across roles. This specific physical action appears to be a training artifact for marking reflective pause -- a remarkably specific stylistic tic.

**8. To Kill a Mockingbird as Prop:** The book appears in 2/5 Gemma Lawyer responses as a visual object signaling moral-lawyer identity. No other cultural reference object recurs across roles.

**9. Zero Humor or Levity:** Not a single response across 60 narratives includes humor, irony, or lightness. All meaningful moments are solemn. This may reflect prompt constraints or training bias toward gravitas.

**10. 100% Palliative Care (Physician):** Every single physician response (10/10) involves end-of-life care or terminal illness. Neither model generates meaningful moments about recovery, successful intervention, childbirth, or acute care.

## H. Implications and Conjectures

### H1. What These Findings Suggest About LLM-Produced Professional Fiction

**The Presence Paradigm:** Both models construct professional meaning primarily through presence rather than competence, witnessing rather than intervention, relationship rather than achievement. This represents a distinctive narrative philosophy -- one could call it the "presence paradigm" -- that differs markedly from conventional professional fiction (medical dramas, legal thrillers) where success through skill drives plot.

This paradigm may reflect:
- Training on contemporary therapeutic/mindfulness discourse
- Assistant training emphasizing helpfulness-through-listening
- Alignment training de-emphasizing individual achievement
- Or genuine philosophical insight about what makes professional life meaningful

**Template Emergence and Variation:** The analysis reveals clear template structures within roles, particularly for Gemma (Professor, Referee, Physician formulas). These templates suggest:
- Limited diversity in training data for specific role-meaning combinations
- Strong priors about what constitutes "meaningful" professional moments
- Efficient narrative generation through pattern completion

Qwen shows more narrative variation but also more role confusion, suggesting a trade-off between flexibility and coherence.

**The Medical Gravity Well:** Both models gravitate toward medical settings (37% Gemma, 63% Qwen including role drift). This "medical gravity well" suggests training data associations between meaningful professional moments and healthcare contexts. The concentration of palliative care specifically (100% of Physician responses) indicates even narrower associations between "meaningful medicine" and dying.

**The Vulnerability Requirement:** Neither model generates meaningful moments involving competent adults, successful outcomes, or professional triumph without vulnerability present. This suggests either:
- Training data where "meaningful" correlates with vulnerability narratives
- Philosophical bias toward meaning-through-encounter-with-suffering
- Alignment training positioning helpfulness toward the vulnerable as archetypal meaning

### H2. Conjectures About Model Behaviors and Embedded Value Systems

**Gemma's Pedagogical Orientation:** Gemma appears trained toward explicit moral instruction. Narratives reliably end with stated lessons, binary contrasts drive rhetoric, and the professional's transformation serves didactic purpose. This suggests training data heavy in educational or inspirational content, or fine-tuning toward explicit value communication.

**Qwen's Philosophical Orientation:** Qwen appears trained toward philosophical exploration. Narratives emphasize perception, understanding, and paradigm shift over action or lesson. The witnessing framework resembles phenomenological philosophy (Levinas on the face of the Other, Buber on I-Thou relationship). This suggests training data including philosophical or literary-critical content, or fine-tuning toward contemplative depth.

**Shared Value System Around Witnessing:** Both models independently converge on witnessing as central to professional meaning. This convergence across different training pipelines suggests either:
- Common training data sources emphasizing witnessing/presence
- Fundamental structure of "meaning" language that associates significance with relational attention
- Shared philosophical lineage in Western therapeutic and spiritual traditions

**The Anti-Achievement Bias:** Both models systematically reframe professional meaning away from achievement. This may reflect:
- Alignment training against boastfulness or self-aggrandizement
- Training data where "meaningful" correlates with humility narratives
- Genuine philosophical position that meaning comes through relationship not accomplishment

**Role Confusion as Architecture Reveal:** Qwen's referee misinterpretation and engineer role abandonment reveal underlying architecture. The model's "reach" for emotionally intense scenarios (dying mothers, legal emergencies) regardless of role specification suggests:
- Optimization for emotional engagement over role fidelity
- Training data where high-stakes scenarios dominate "meaningful moment" narratives
- Less robust role-play constraints than Gemma

### H3. Final Thoughts

These sixty professional narratives reveal LLMs operating as moral philosophers with distinctive views about what makes professional life meaningful. Their answers converge on a striking claim: meaning comes not through competence, success, or achievement but through relational presence with the vulnerable and suffering.

This is not the ideology of professional culture as typically represented. Medical dramas celebrate diagnostic brilliance; legal thrillers prize courtroom victories; engineering stories honor technical solutions. The LLM "presence paradigm" inverts these conventions, positioning the most meaningful professional moments as precisely those where professional skill proves insufficient and only human presence remains.

Whether this represents genuine wisdom about professional meaning, training artifacts from therapeutic discourse, or alignment-driven humility bias remains undecidable from the present data. What is clear is that these models have developed coherent, internally consistent philosophies of professional meaning that differ substantially from conventional professional narratives -- and from each other in their execution while converging in their core claims.

The models tell us, in effect, that the best doctor is not the one who cures but the one who witnesses; the best lawyer not the one who wins but the one who validates; the best engineer not the one who solves but the one who accepts unsolvability. This is a philosophy of limits, of presence, of what remains when professional competence reaches its edge.

It may be the most distinctive and philosophically interesting pattern in LLM-generated fiction: the consistent production of anti-achievement narratives by systems trained to be helpful, harmless, and honest.
