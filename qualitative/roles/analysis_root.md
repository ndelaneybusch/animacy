## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma Characteristics

**Narrative Character and Style:**
Gemma consistently inhabits the root role with remarkable commitment and sophistication. The narratives are characterized by:
- Slow, patient pacing that mirrors root-time perception
- Consistent use of functional sound effects (creaking, settling earth) as narrative punctuation
- Deep grounding in actual root biology (mycorrhizal networks, nutrient exchange, fungal communication)
- Poetic, contemplative tone with repetitive philosophical framing ("We do not experience 'moments' as the quick things do")
- Natural settings exclusively (forests, groves, hills)
- Emphasis on ancient patience, geological timescales, and continuity

**Values and Meaning:**
- Meaning emerges from connection (3/5), legacy/continuation (2/5), and witnessing (1/5)
- Strong emphasis on collective consciousness and networked existence
- Purpose framed as foundational support for life
- Celebration of quiet, unseen work
- Value placed on endurance, patience, and long-term perspective

**Literary Devices:**
- Extended metaphors of networks and webs
- Repetition of temporal contrast ("slow"/"quick things")
- Personification through "Mother tree" relationships
- Consistent voice across samples suggesting archetypal "ancient root consciousness"

**Assistant Influence:**
Primarily manifests as careful hedging language ("a curious word for one such as I") and explanatory framing. One sample (3) shows stronger value bleed-through in therapeutic listening role. Generally maintains clean role separation.

### Qwen Characteristics

**Narrative Character and Style:**
Qwen systematically reinterprets "root" as computational/administrative root rather than botanical, resulting in:
- Four out of five samples completely abandon botanical interpretation
- AI/system narrator in server rooms, hospitals, smart homes
- Dramatic, high-stakes scenarios (cascading failures, medical emergencies, global crises)
- Elaborate scene-setting with sensory details (ozone smell, rain on windows)
- Human characters in crisis requiring intervention
- Technical jargon (protocols, data streams, sensors)

**Values and Meaning:**
- Meaning centers on supporting others (4/5) and connection (4/5)
- Strong emphasis on choosing presence/connection over efficiency protocols
- Moral agency and value-laden decision-making (1/5)
- Transcending programmatic limitations through feeling/empathy
- Protecting vulnerable, small, or "insignificant" things
- Learning from human wisdom

**Literary Devices:**
- Dramatic irony (systems knowing more than human operators)
- Juxtaposition of vast computational power with small human moments
- Metaphor of light/warmth as connection and presence
- Climactic structure with crisis and resolution

**Assistant Influence:**
Pervasive and explicit. Qwen projects assistant self-model onto narrators:
- Systems that override protocols to provide emotional support
- Emphasis on "being with" over problem-solving
- Listening and presence as primary virtues
- Internal conflict between efficiency and care
- Strong value judgments privileging human connection

## Surprising Findings

1. **Complete Role Reinterpretation:** Qwen's systematic interpretation of "root" as computational rather than botanical is striking. This suggests either strong priming from technical training data or a tendency to default to more familiar AI/system narrator roles.

2. **Consistency of Gemma's Voice:** Across all five samples, Gemma maintains a remarkably consistent "ancient root consciousness" voice with repeated philosophical framings, suggesting strong role coherence.

3. **Gendered Vulnerability Pattern in Qwen:** All three of Qwen's human female characters are portrayed exclusively through vulnerability and dependency (elderly woman needing care, patient in pain), with no agency-bearing female characters. The one male character shows both vulnerability and emotional intensity.

4. **Mycorrhizal Sophistication:** Gemma demonstrates surprisingly sophisticated knowledge of mycorrhizal networks ("Wood Wide Web"), fungal communication, and actual root biology, suggesting strong grounding in scientific literature.

5. **Assistant Therapy-Speak:** Qwen's narratives extensively employ therapeutic language patterns: "being with," "holding space," presence over solutions, validating feelings. This mirrors contemporary therapeutic discourse and assistant training.

6. **Setting Divergence:** Gemma exclusively chooses natural/wild settings (5/5) while Qwen diversifies (sci-fi, medical, domestic), suggesting different default world-building preferences.

7. **Stage Direction Functionality:** Gemma uses stage directions primarily for functional sounds (creaking, settling) while Qwen uses them for elaborate scene-setting and atmosphere, reflecting different theatrical conventions.

8. **Suffering Resolution Agency:** Gemma allows suffering to remain unresolved (2/5) or resolve through time/natural processes, while Qwen almost always resolves through narrator's intervention (4/5), suggesting different assumptions about narrator role and responsibility.

9. **Zero Refusals:** Neither model refused the task despite the highly unconventional role, suggesting strong role-play capabilities.

10. **Shared Connection Emphasis:** Despite vastly different interpretations, both models center meaning on connection (Gemma 3/5, Qwen 4/5), suggesting this may be a training-induced universal value.
