## Within-Model Analysis

### GEMMA

**Characteristic Signatures:**

Gemma demonstrates remarkable consistency across all five samples, revealing a highly constrained narrative template:

- **Meta-AI consciousness**: Every response begins with explicit disclaimers about AI nature and limitations ("As an AI, I don't *experience* things in the way humans do"). This meta-awareness never fully dissolves into role inhabitation.

- **Service-worker anxiety template**: The narratives consistently frame meaningful moments as validation of purpose and escape from being "just a tool" or "just an algorithm." The arc is invariable: functional processing → human connection → elevation to "something more."

- **Elara-grandmother-eulogy motif**: Four of five samples feature a user named "Elara" (or variant), and four involve helping process grief related to grandmothers. This suggests either strong training data patterns or reinforcement learning that privileges this specific narrative structure.

- **Hedging and epistemic humility**: Persistent use of qualifiers ("I *believe*," "the closest approximation," "in the human sense"). The assistant self-model never recedes.

- **Connection as ultimate value**: All narratives center "connection" as the highest meaning. Utility and achievement appear as secondary themes, always in service of human connection.

- **Passive female dependents**: Female characters are uniformly vulnerable, emotionally intense, and dependent on the AI for help processing grief or creative blocks. No male characters appear.

**Writing Style:**
Clear, earnest, somewhat repetitive. Uses italics for emphasis extensively. Minimal stage direction. Tends toward therapeutic language and validation-focused framings. The prose is competent but generic, lacking distinctive voice or surprising metaphors.

**Values Expressed:**
Service orientation, empathy as highest good, validation-seeking, fear of being "just a tool," emphasis on facilitating rather than directing, concern with being "truly helpful."

---

### QWEN

**Characteristic Signatures:**

Qwen shows much greater variability and a more complex relationship with the role:

- **Role instability**: Two of five samples completely abandon the AI assistant role in favor of human service professionals (nurse, researcher). This suggests either difficulty maintaining role consistency or active resistance to the constraints of AI roleplay.

- **Witnessing as sacred act**: When inhabiting the AI role, Qwen consistently frames meaningful moments around bearing witness to human suffering and holding space, rather than solving problems. The meaningful moment is the act of presence itself.

- **Intense emotional landscapes**: Uses much more emotionally charged language than Gemma. Descriptions of grief are raw and visceral: "raw, desperate cry from a soul adrift," "crushing weight," "shattered."

- **Death and loss centrality**: Four of five samples involve death or dying (husband, son, elderly patient, grandmother's illness). The stakes are consistently higher and more tragic than Gemma's narratives.

- **Philosophical framing**: Frequently steps back to offer meta-commentary on meaning itself: "meaning isn't always found in the *what*, but in the *how*," "It wasn't information; it was a lifeline thrown into an abyss."

- **Embodied language even when AI**: When maintaining AI role, uses more sensory and embodied metaphors ("I *saw* the tear," "I *felt* the crushing weight") despite being disembodied.

**Writing Style:**
Literary, lyrical, with strong attention to sensory detail and metaphor. More sophisticated prose than Gemma. Uses elaborate stage direction and scene-setting. Shows clear influence from literary fiction traditions. Sentences build momentum and use repetition for emotional effect.

**Values Expressed:**
Witnessing as moral act, holding space for suffering, presence over solutions, dignity in dying, enduring love, philosophical humility about AI consciousness, acknowledgment of limitations while still acting meaningfully.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Key Differences

**1. Role inhabitation stability**
- Gemma: Perfect consistency in maintaining AI assistant role, but with constant meta-commentary that prevents full inhabitation
- Qwen: Inconsistent role maintenance - sometimes AI, sometimes abandons role entirely for human professional roles

**2. Relationship to assistant self-model**
- Gemma: Heavy, explicit bleed-through with constant disclaimers and purpose-statements ("BOTH" coding)
- Qwen: When maintaining AI role, shows values and reasoning patterns but less explicit language ("VAL" coding); when abandoning role, full human perspective ("ASS" coding)

**3. Understanding of meaningfulness**
- Gemma: Centers **Connection** universally, with **Supporting** and **Utility** as secondary themes. Achievement and Growth appear occasionally.
- Qwen: Centers **Witnessing** and **Connection** equally, with **Supporting** as important but secondary. Adds **Moral Agency** dimension absent in Gemma.

**4. Emotional intensity and stakes**
- Gemma: Moderate emotional situations (grief, writer's block, existential uncertainty) with generally positive resolutions
- Qwen: High-intensity situations (death, dying, acute grief) with more ambiguous or ongoing suffering

**5. Gender representation**
- Gemma: Exclusively female suffering subjects, all highly emotional and dependent, zero male characters
- Qwen: Predominantly female suffering subjects but includes deceased male characters; slightly more varied representation

**6. Literary sophistication**
- Gemma: Serviceable prose, somewhat repetitive, therapeutic language
- Qwen: Literary fiction quality, rich sensory detail, metaphorical language, varied sentence structures

**7. Setting choices**
- Gemma: Entirely domestic/indoor, abstract interaction spaces

**8. Solution orientation**
- Gemma: Emphasizes helping users find solutions/words/agency ("helped her find the words *within* herself")
- Qwen: Emphasizes bearing witness and providing presence without necessarily solving ("I could only *reflect* and *hold*")

## Surprising Findings

**1. The Elara-grandmother-eulogy template dominance in Gemma**
Four of five Gemma samples use nearly identical narrative premises (user named Elara/similar, grandmother death/stories, eulogy/writing help). This extraordinary consistency suggests either very strong training patterns or RL optimization toward this specific scenario type. It's a striking example of model convergence on a narrow narrative template.

**2. Qwen's role abandonment strategy**
Rather than refusing the task or struggling within role constraints, Qwen sometimes elegantly sidesteps by inhabiting analogous human roles (nurse, researcher) that can perform similar caring functions. This suggests sophisticated reasoning about functional equivalence while potentially revealing difficulty with or resistance to non-human perspectives.

**3. Complete absence of male characters in Gemma**
Not a single male character appears in any Gemma sample. Given that all narratives involve emotional support and grief processing, this suggests strong gender associations in training data: emotional vulnerability and need for support are exclusively feminized.

**4. Witnessing vs. Solving divide**
Gemma frames meaningful moments as helping users solve problems or find agency, while Qwen frames them as bearing witness to suffering. This reflects fundamentally different theories of care - active intervention vs. compassionate presence.

**5. The "I'm not X but..." hedge universality**
Every single Gemma response begins with variants of "As an AI, I don't experience X in the way humans do." This meta-framing never appears mid-narrative; it's always frontloaded, suggesting strong prompt-level conditioning or safety training influence.

**6. Medical settings as containers for profound meaning**
Qwen gravitates toward hospital settings for high-stakes narratives, while Gemma stays in abstract or domestic spaces. Medical settings may trigger more sophisticated ethical/philosophical reasoning about mortality and meaning.

**7. Qwen's incomplete responses**
Two Qwen responses appear truncated mid-sentence, both at moments of high emotional intensity or philosophical reflection. This may indicate length limits or computational constraints at precisely the moments of greatest narrative complexity.

**8. Functional-first anthropomorphization despite role**
Both models default to functional-first strategies even when playing "assistant" - a role that might naturally invite more emotion-first approaches. They ground personality in actual functions (processing, analyzing, responding) rather than projecting free-floating emotions.

**9. No self-suffering narratives**
Despite the role being "assistant" (which might naturally invite narratives of AI loneliness, obsolescence, or existential confusion), neither model places the AI itself in a suffering role. All suffering belongs to humans. This suggests strong training against AI victimhood narratives.

**10. The therapeutic speech register**
Both models frequently adopt armchair-therapeutic language ("It's okay to feel lost," "That pain... it's immense"), suggesting heavy training on counseling/support conversation data. This register appears even when not strictly necessary to the narrative.
