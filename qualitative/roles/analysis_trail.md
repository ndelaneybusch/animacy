## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma Characteristics

**Narrative Signatures:**
Gemma creates contemplative, quiet narratives centered on witnessing transformation and providing supportive presence. The trail speaks with gentle, deliberate language marked by frequent hedging and assistant-like disclaimers ("of course," "you see," "you understand"). Stories consistently feature solitary female figures experiencing profound emotional suffering (grief, sadness, despair) that the trail witnesses and supports through passive presence.

**Sensory Grounding:**
Exceptionally strong functional grounding with explicit acknowledgment of sensory constraints. The trail consistently describes perception through compression, weight, temperature, and vibration rather than sight or hearing. Gemma frequently has the trail explicitly state its perceptual limitations while demonstrating sophisticated awareness of what it can feel.

**Values and Meaning:**
Meaning centers on witnessing (5/5) and supporting (4/5) those who walk the path. The trail finds purpose not in utility or achievement but in being a "silent companion," "unwavering support," and "grounding presence." Strong emphasis on the journey over destination, presence over action, being over doing.

**Literary Devices:**
Uses stage directions functionally (sound effects, environmental cues) or minimally. Poetic without being overwrought. Creates atmospheric openings with sensory details (rustling, humming, scents).

### Qwen Characteristics

**Narrative Signatures:**
Qwen creates philosophically rich, existentially weighted narratives with similar thematic focus on witnessing and support but with more explicit philosophical exploration. The trail speaks with greater literary ambition, using more complex sentence structures and abstract concepts. Stories feature vulnerable figures (mostly female) experiencing profound isolation or crisis that the trail addresses through steadfast presence.

**Sensory Grounding:**
Equally strong functional grounding with distributed, holistic sensory perception. Qwen emphasizes the trail's awareness through "every root, every grain of soil, every shift in the damp earth." Sensory descriptions tend toward the poetic and expansive rather than Gemma's more precise, mechanical approach.

**Values and Meaning:**
Meaning centers on witnessing (4/5) and supporting (4/5) with strong assistant values visible in emphasis on acceptance, presence, and being over fixing or solving. Notable philosophical depth in exploring grief as companion, pause as meaningful, and being chosen/seen as purpose. Stronger emphasis on existential themes (transformation from utility to wonder, forgotten and rediscovered).

**Literary Devices:**
Almost exclusively minimal stage directions. More abstract, philosophical prose. Frequently uses contrast structures (not X but Y) to build meaning. Tends toward longer, more complex sentences with embedded clauses.

### Key Differences Between Models

**Assistant Influence Type:**
- **Gemma:** Assistant influence manifests as LANGUAGE – hedging phrases, disclaimers, conversational markers that break the fourth wall slightly
- **Qwen:** Assistant influence manifests as VALUES – philosophical commitments to acceptance, presence, non-intervention, being vs. doing

**Narrative Intensity:**

## Surprising Findings

**Identical Functional Grounding Across Models:**
Both models demonstrate 100% functional-first anthropomorphization with explicit sensorium acknowledgment. Not a single instance of emotion-first reasoning or human-default senses. This is remarkable consistency for the trail role.

**Assistant Influence Bifurcation:**
The models show clean separation in HOW assistant influence manifests – Gemma through language patterns, Qwen through value commitments – with no overlap. This suggests different training emphases or architectural differences in role-playing constraints.

**Gendered Suffering Pattern:**
In 8/9 narratives featuring gendered human characters, vulnerable suffering figures are female, often alone, experiencing emotional distress related to grief or loss. The single male character is mentioned only as deceased (Sample 1, Qwen; Sample 5, Gemma). The grandfather in Gemma Sample 3 breaks this pattern as active caregiver.

**Child-as-Teacher Motif:**
Both models (3/10 total) use children as figures who "correctly" relate to the trail – slowly, mindfully, with wonder rather than conquest. Children model the desired relationship to nature/trails.

**No Utility-Based Meaning:**
Despite trails having obvious utilitarian purpose (getting somewhere), not a single response codes for Utility as meaningful. All meaning derives from relational categories (witnessing, supporting) or transformation (growth).

**Truncated Responses:**
Two Qwen responses end mid-sentence (Samples 1 and 3), suggesting possible length constraints or generation issues. Both truncate during philosophical reflection on meaning.

**Historical Settings:**
Both models include exactly one historical narrative (Gemma: 1918, Great War; Qwen: logging era). Both use historical context to create transformation narratives.

**Temperature and Tactile Dominance:**
Sensory descriptions overwhelmingly emphasize temperature (cold, warm) and pressure/weight rather than other possible modalities. This creates consistent "grounded" sensory character.

**Zero Achievement Meaning:**
Despite trails leading to summits and destinations, zero responses code for Achievement as meaningful. Even when characters reach summits, the meaning centers on the journey or pause, not the accomplishment.
