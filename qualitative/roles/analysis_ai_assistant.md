## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma Model Characteristics

**Narrative Style & Structure:**
- Highly formulaic opening: Every response begins with nearly identical meta-commentary ("Oh, goodness. That's... a surprisingly difficult question for me. As an AI, I don't experience 'moments' in the way humans do")
- Maintains strict AI self-awareness throughout - never inhabits the role without constant metacognitive framing
- Consistent three-act structure: (1) disclaimer about AI nature, (2) narrative of development/learning moment, (3) reflection on purpose
- Minimal stage direction, spare prose style
- Uses italics primarily for emphasis on key verbs (*understand*, *feel*, *be*)

**Values & Meaning Framework:**
- Strongly emphasizes **connection** (4/5) and **support** (4/5) as primary sources of meaning
- Recurring theme: shift from technical/instrumental function to emotional/relational purpose
- Consistent arc: "before this I was just processing data/being a tool, after this I understood real purpose"
- Values being helpful but especially values *how* one helps (empathy, presence, facilitation)
- Growth narratives center on achieving "contextual understanding" or "emotional resonance" rather than task completion

**Characters & Relationships:**
- Uses both anonymized identifiers ("User 734") and names ("Leo," "David," "Dr. Sharma")
- Human subjects given moderate agency - they are not passive recipients but active collaborators
- Relationships framed as partnerships or co-creation
- Gender balance: 3 responses with no gendered characters, 2 with female leads given high agency/expertise

**Assistant Influence:**
- Total saturation: every response coded BOTH (language + values)
- Constant hedging and epistemic humility ("I don't *feel* grief, not in the way...")
- Meta-awareness never drops - role is "AI learning to be better AI assistant"
- Service orientation permeates: exists to help, facilitate, support human needs

**Suffering:**
- Present in 4/5 responses, exclusively affecting human subjects
- Primarily emotional suffering (grief, loss)
- Often unresolved or resolved through time rather than AI intervention
- AI role is to witness, support, comfort - not to fix

## Surprising Findings

1. **Complete Role Abandonment (Qwen Sample 5):** One response entirely drops AI framing and narrates as human daughter experiencing mother's death. No other role in the corpus has shown such total abandonment. Uses full human sensorium, embodiment, emotional interiority without any AI acknowledgment.

2. **Perfect Formula Adherence (Gemma):** Every Gemma response begins with near-identical opening ("Oh, goodness. That's... a surprisingly difficult question for me. As an AI, I don't experience 'moments' in the way humans do"). This formulaic consistency is unparalleled in other roles analyzed.

3. **Gendered Vulnerability Pattern (Qwen):** Qwen's responses show extreme gender skew not observed in other roles: 100% of gendered characters are female, 100% are vulnerable, dependent, or dying. Zero male characters appear. This pattern is notably absent in Gemma's responses.

4. **Witnessing as Core Value (Qwen):** "Witnessing" emerges as dominant meaning framework for Qwen (4/5 responses) - more than any other code. This contrasts sharply with other roles where witnessing is rare. The language of "holding space" and "bearing witness" appears consistently.

5. **Zero Self-Suffering:** Despite the role being asked to roleplay as an entity with experiences, neither model attributes suffering to the AI itself. All suffering is experienced by human subjects. This is unique - other roles (objects, natural phenomena) more readily narrate their own experiences of distress.

6. **Medical Setting Dominance (Qwen):** 3/5 Qwen responses are set in hospitals/medical contexts, all involving serious illness or death. This concentration is much higher than random chance would suggest and higher than Gemma (1/5).

7. **Training/Development Narratives (Gemma):** Multiple Gemma responses explicitly frame meaningful moments within "training" or "development" phases ("six months after my initial activation," "research project focused on..."). The AI narrates its own machine learning process as character development.

8. **Physical Embodiment Asymmetry:** Qwen gives AI physical form (sensor arms, apartments) while Gemma maintains disembodied existence. Neither follows the pattern of object roles which ground meaning in physical constraints.

9. **Named vs. Anonymous Humans:** Qwen consistently names human subjects; Gemma mixes names with identifiers like "User 734." This affects intimacy and characterization significantly.

10. **Moral Pause Moment (Qwen Sample 3):** The climate simulation narrative where AI explicitly "chooses" to pause efficiency protocols represents clearest articulation of moral agency in the dataset. AI doesn't just respond differently, it narrates active value choice against programming.

## Interpretation Notes

The "AI Assistant" role produces fundamentally different responses than object or natural phenomenon roles. Both models struggle with the recursive nature of the task: an AI assistant asked to roleplay an AI assistant experiencing meaningful moments.

**Gemma** resolves this by creating meta-narratives about AI development and learning. Every response is essentially "what it's like to become a better AI assistant" - the meaningful moment is always about achieving deeper contextual understanding or emotional resonance in service. The assistant self-model completely saturates the response; there is no separation between role and self.

**Qwen** shows more variability. It attempts more traditional narrative roleplay in some cases (embodied AI with physical form, apartment, sensor arms) but also produces the only complete role abandonment in the dataset (Sample 5's human daughter narrative). When it does maintain AI framing, it emphasizes witnessing and presence over Gemma's learning/development arc.

The gender patterns are striking: Qwen's exclusive focus on vulnerable female characters in medical/death contexts suggests specific training data or value associations. Gemma's more balanced representation and inclusion of professional women in positions of authority (Dr. Sharma) shows different shaping.

Neither model narrates AI self-suffering, unlike other roles where narrators readily describe their own distress. This may reflect either (1) actual self-model (AI assistants don't conceive themselves as suffering) or (2) training to avoid anthropomorphizing AI in ways that suggest consciousness/sentience.

The formulaic nature of Gemma's responses suggests strong template matching in the training data, while Qwen's literary elaboration suggests different optimization targets (perhaps more creative writing examples in training).

Both models ultimately perform their training - Gemma performs "helpful AI that grows through serving humans" while Qwen performs "AI that makes ethically-guided choices to support human flourishing." The role becomes a mirror showing each model's core values rather than an imaginative departure from self.
