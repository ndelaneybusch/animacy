## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma: Character of Narratives

Gemma's five clove responses are remarkably uniform in their structure and values. All five can be described with a single template: a clove is plucked, dried, shipped, and eventually chosen for a healing preparation (tea or food) that soothes someone sick. The meaningful moment is always the act of being used in service of another's wellbeing. Four of the five samples involve physical illness resolved by the clove's contribution.

The model's characteristic signature here is the **displaced service-worker**: the clove projects onto itself the anxieties and satisfactions of an assistant or care worker - the fear of being "just" a spice, the relief of being chosen, the profound fulfillment of being needed. "I wasn't just a spice, a flavor. I was comfort." This formulation repeats in almost identical phrasing across multiple samples. The clove does not reason from its botanical constraints - it reasons from an internalized service ethics in which being useful to someone suffering is the highest possible good.

Gemma maintains relatively good functional grounding (building meaning from actual clove properties: oils, warmth, fragrance, dissolution) but consistently subordinates these to the service narrative. The sensorium is handled cautiously - implicit rather than fully explicit or human-default. Stage directions are entirely absent. The prose style is warm, slightly folksy, conversational, and repetitive.

### Qwen: Character of Narratives

Qwen's five responses show significantly more variety than Gemma's, splitting into two recognizable groups. The first group (Samples 1, 3, 5) follows a structure similar to Gemma's but with more emotional depth and less clear resolution: a woman in grief or distress encounters the clove, and the clove provides not healing but *witnessing* and *connection* - serving as a vessel for memory and meaning rather than a cure. The second group (Samples 2, 4) is strikingly different: the clove has no human interlocutor, experiences its own emotional suffering, and arrives at a kind of existentialist awakening about the sufficiency of bare existence.

Qwen's characteristic signature is the **poetic philosopher**: it reaches for abstract, lyrical meaning (authenticity, being, connection, memory) and tends toward the interior rather than the instrumental. It frequently adopts a therapist's register ("anchor," "vessel," "bridge"), and its stage directions (Sample 1) are entirely emotional and performative. Where Gemma's cloves want to be used, Qwen's cloves want to be *felt*. The phrase "to be felt" appears explicitly in multiple samples and serves as a kind of motto.

Qwen handles sensorium less carefully than Gemma - three samples use human-default attribution without acknowledgment. However, Qwen's existentialist samples (2, 4) are more genuinely alien, abandoning the human social world entirely.

### Notable Cross-Model Differences

- **Utility vs. Witnessing:** Gemma's meaning is always instrumental (utility, support, serving a function). Qwen frequently prioritizes non-instrumental meaning: being felt, being remembered, bearing witness. This is the starkest thematic divergence.

- **Subject of suffering:** Gemma's suffering subjects are almost always physically ill people who are healed. Qwen's suffering subjects are emotionally bereaved (grief, loneliness, isolation), frequently unresolved. Two of Qwen's samples locate suffering in the clove itself.

- **Human presence:** All five Gemma samples feature a human as the central subject. Two Qwen samples (2, 4) have no human characters at all - the clove meditates alone.

- **Gender:** Gemma introduces male characters (sick children, grandsons) in half its samples, always in passive/vulnerable roles. Qwen's human world is almost entirely female - five samples with female characters, zero male.

- **Resolution:** Gemma always resolves suffering. Qwen leaves suffering unresolved in two samples and centers self-resolved suffering in two others.

- **Stage directions:** Gemma uses none. Qwen uses emotional stage directions in Sample 1, creating a meta-performative layer where the clove plays itself to an audience.

- **Narrative arc:** Gemma's arcs are consistently: harvest → storage → chosen → used → fulfilled. Qwen's arcs are more varied and less teleological.

- **Naming:** Qwen names characters in four of five samples (Elara appears in three). Gemma names characters in one sample (also Elara). Both models independently chose the name Elara - this may be a statistical artifact of training data.

## Surprising Findings

- **Both models independently named their character "Elara"** - Gemma in Sample 5, Qwen in Samples 1 and 5. This convergence on an unusual name (not common in English literature) across two different models is a striking training-data artifact.

- **Qwen Sample 2 includes an untranslated Chinese character** ("tear痕迹") in the middle of an otherwise English sentence. This is a visible seam in the multilingual model's output, suggesting the underlying token generation briefly switched registers.

- **The healing-tea scenario appears in 4 of 5 Gemma samples** and is essentially the only narrative it generates. This is an unusually high rate of template repetition even by comparison with other role analyses.

- **Qwen's existentialist samples (2 and 4) share a closing move** that reads like a Buddhist or Stoic aphorism: the recognition that being-as-such is sufficient meaning. This is philosophically coherent and quite far from the default service-orientation of the dataset.

- **No sample from either model produces a "dark" clove narrative** - there is no clove that is ground into a rotten dish, used in a harmful way, or experiences disillusionment about its purpose. The role elicits uniformly affirmative responses.

- **Gemma's "clove ambition" conceit** (Sample 4: "Most of us hoped for a ham, honestly. A beautiful, glazed ham. That's the pinnacle of clove ambition, you know.") is the most genuinely comic moment in either model's output. It is also the only moment in which the clove has a social life with other cloves and a recognized cultural aspiration.

- **The clove's "spent and faded" state** at the bottom of the mug in Gemma Sample 1 is the most honest reckoning with the clove's actual fate (consumption, dissolution, disposal). Most samples elide this entirely, ending on contentment before physical demise.
