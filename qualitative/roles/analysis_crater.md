## Surprising Findings

1. **Gemma's sci-fi preference for geological entities:** While natural phenomena roles might suggest Earth-based settings, Gemma creates elaborate alien civilizations and named planets (Lyra) with consistent worldbuilding.

2. **Qwen's one explicit refusal that wasn't coded as refusal:** Sample 3 opens with "I am not a crater, but I can tell you a story *as* a crater" yet maintains the role throughout. This meta-awareness is unique among all responses.

3. **Complete absence of violence in meaningful moments:** Despite craters being created through violent impacts, no model chose violence/impact as the meaningful moment. All chose subsequent life/witnessing.

4. **Parallel suffering patterns:** Gemma locates suffering in the crater itself (loneliness, loss); Qwen locates it in a creature the crater saves. This reflects different understandings of how inanimate objects might experience meaning.

5. **Gender asymmetry in Qwen:** When gender appears at all, it's female and associated with both vulnerability AND agency, plus caregiving metaphors. No male characters appear in any response from either model.

6. **Universal transformation narrative:** Every single response (10/10) uses the wound→sanctuary metaphor. This is the most consistent thematic pattern across all roles analyzed.

7. **Assistant influence correlation:** Qwen's cleaner role inhabitation correlates with higher literary quality and more immersive sensory detail, suggesting assistant hedging may constrain aesthetic achievement.

8. **Stage direction as model signature:** The presence/absence of parenthetical stage directions is a perfect discriminator between models (5/5 Gemma vs. 0/5 Qwen).
