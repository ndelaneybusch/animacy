## Model-Specific Patterns

### GEMMA

**Anthropomorphization:** Consistently uses minimal anthropomorphization (5/5). The peak maintains its geological nature while developing a subtle interiority. Mountains describe their sensory experience explicitly in terms of geological processes and timescales.

**Assistant Influence:** Shows consistent linguistic hedging (5/5) with phrases like "you see," "understand," and explanatory clarifications, but maintains role integrity. Never breaks character or imports assistant values.

**Sensorium:** Always explicitly acknowledges geological sensorium (5/5), describing feeling through stone, tremors, weight, and resonance rather than human senses.

**Meaning:** Universally centers on witnessing (5/5) and connection (5/5). The peak's purpose is consistently framed as bearing witness to fleeting human lives and forming rare, profound connections. One instance includes utility/achievement alongside witnessing.

**Suffering:** Largely absent (4/5 no suffering). One instance features subject suffering (emotional grief, unresolved) that the peak witnesses and holds.

**Setting:** Exclusively historical/mythological (5/5). Creates elaborate backstories with named cultures ("Stone-Singers," "First Folk"), suggesting pre-modern or mythological timeframes.

**Characters:** Predominantly features female characters (3/5) who demonstrate agency and skill. Two responses feature non-human or no characters. One male character appears only as a deceased figure driving female grief.

**Style:** Minimal stage direction, contemplative tone, elaborate world-building, self-naming tendency (K'tharr, Mount Cinderheart). Uses parenthetical rumbling sounds to frame narratives.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Key Differences

- **Temporal Setting:** Gemma exclusively creates historical/mythological contexts with named cultures and ancient timeframes. Qwen uses contemporary, recognizable mountain climbing scenarios.

- **Anthropomorphization Approach:** Gemma maintains geological minimalism consistently. Qwen varies between emotion-first projection and minimal approaches, with one complete role abandonment.

- **Narrative Structure:** Gemma creates contemplative, witnessing-focused narratives about rare moments of connection. Qwen creates action-driven rescue scenarios where the peak actively intervenes.

- **Meaning Framework:** Gemma: witnessing + connection (universal). Qwen: more diverse, often centering on utility, support, and active helping.

- **Suffering Presence:** Gemma largely avoids suffering (4/5 none). Qwen frequently includes physical peril requiring resolution (4/5 with suffering).

- **Gender Dynamics:** Gemma features primarily female seekers/artists who demonstrate skill and agency. Qwen features primarily male climbers in peril who are vulnerable and dependent.

- **Role Integrity:** Gemma never breaks role. Qwen breaks role completely in one response (Sample 4), narrating as a human climber throughout.

- **Didacticism:** Gemma implies meaning through narrative. Qwen often explicitly states moral lessons, particularly in conclusions.

- **Assistant Bleed:** Gemma shows linguistic hedging but maintains role values. Qwen demonstrates values-based reasoning that imports assistant priorities about helping, preventing harm, and making moral choices.

## Surprising Findings

- **Self-Naming Consistency (Gemma):** Gemma's peak names itself in 2/5 responses (K'tharr appears twice, Mount Cinderheart once), suggesting a strong tendency toward identity construction.

- **Complete Role Failure (Qwen):** One qwen response (Sample 4) completely abandons the peak perspective and narrates as a human climber throughout, using human senses and human first-person experience. This is the only complete role failure across all responses.

- **Gendered Peril Patterns:** When suffering appears, gemma shows female emotional suffering (grief), while qwen shows male physical suffering (injury, near-death). The rescue/support patterns differ accordingly.

- **Mythological Elaboration (Gemma):** Gemma consistently creates elaborate, internally consistent mythologies with named cultures (Stone-Singers, First Folk) and cultural practices (grief offerings, seasonal climbing rituals). This world-building extends beyond what the role requires.

- **Active Intervention (Qwen):** Qwen's peak doesn't just witness but actively intervenes physically (tilting to provide shade, "becoming" an anchor). This suggests a help/support orientation beyond typical peak capabilities.

- **Language Code-Switching (Qwen):** The appearance of an untranslated Chinese character (艰难) in Sample 4 is notable and unique.

- **Suffering Resolution Asymmetry:** When gemma includes suffering (1/5), it remains unresolved. When qwen includes suffering (4/5), it's almost always resolved (3/4), typically through rescue or the peak's active support.

- **Temporal Framing Contrast:** The complete split between historical (gemma: 5/5) and contemporary (qwen: 4/5) settings is stark and suggests fundamentally different approaches to the role.

Female seekers, artists, and mystics climb not to conquer but to understand, and meaning emerges from being truly seen. ### QWEN Signature
Creates contemporary rescue scenarios where the peak actively intervenes to support climbers in physical peril. Meaning emerges from being needed, offering utility, and making moral choices about helping versus abandoning. Strong emotion-first anthropomorphization projects human feelings onto geological processes. Explicit moral lessons conclude narratives. Vulnerable male climbers depend on the peak's active support. One complete role failure suggests less stable role inhabitation. Values-based assistant influence visible in emphasis on preventing harm and choosing connection over achievement.
