## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma Characteristics

**Anthropomorphization:** Consistently functional-first. Grounds personality in actual geological processes (tectonic plates, continental drift, rifting, erosion). When Africa speaks of "my bones" or "my veins," these map to actual geological features. The continent reasons from its material constraints.

**Narrative Focus:** All five samples identify as Africa specifically. Strong focus on human emergence and evolution in the Great Rift Valley. Thematic consistency around witnessing human development from early hominids to modern populations. Historical specificity (20,000 years ago, 200,000 years ago, Gondwana breakup).

**Meaning Framework:** Heavily weighted toward witnessing (3/5) and legacy (4/5). Purpose emerges through observing and supporting human story. One sample explicitly frames meaning as supporting suffering populations through climate change.

**Writing Style:** Parenthetical stage directions that mimic sound effects and physical sensations. Measured, patient tone consistent with geological time scales. Uses ellipses and em dashes for contemplative pacing. Acknowledges scale challenges ("difficult for a being of my scale to quantify").

**Values:** Hope-oriented despite acknowledging human harm. Trust in human capacity for growth. Purpose-finding through witnessing and nurturing. Some assistant hedging evident in 4/5 samples but maintains role well.

**Gender:** Completely gender-neutral narratives. No gendered characters appear.

---

### Qwen Characteristics

**Anthropomorphization:** Mix of functional-first (3/5) and emotion-first (2/5). When functional, still more emotionally expressive than Gemma. Frequent use of emotionally charged language: "trembling," "whisper," "profound," "fragile."

**Narrative Focus:** Creates origin stories. Four of five samples set during planetary formation or early Earth. Creates fictional continent name (Mireth) in one sample. More speculative and mythological rather than historically grounded. Focuses on first life, first water, first seed rather than human evolution.

**Meaning Framework:** Supporting (4/5) and witnessing (4/5) dominate. Strong caregiving orientation. Meaning emerges through providing for life and bearing witness to consciousness. One sample centers authenticity and vulnerability as strength.

**Writing Style:** Minimal stage direction usage. Highly literary and poetic prose. Sophisticated metaphors and nested imagery. Philosophical and therapeutic framing. Uses italics for emphasis on key existential concepts.

**Values:** Caregiver role central to every narrative. Vulnerability-as-strength therapeutic framework. Purpose found through sustaining and containing life. Heavy emphasis on "cradle," "home," "stage" metaphors. Connection and recognition as sources of meaning.

**Gender:** Consistently assigns caregiving role to continent (feminine-coded). No male characters. The continent itself performs maternal caregiving in all five samples.

---

### Key Differences Between Models

- **Historical vs. Mythological:** Gemma narrates actual geological and evolutionary history (African human origins, continental drift). Qwen creates origin myths and planetary formation stories.

- **Geographic Specificity:** Gemma exclusively identifies as Africa with specific locations (Rift Valley, Okavango Delta, Sahara, Congo Basin). Qwen rarely specifies and creates fictional names (Mireth).

- **Sensory Acknowledgment:** Qwen explicitly describes sensory modalities (4/5 explicit vs. 1/5 for Gemma). Qwen frequently addresses "how" the continent perceives. Gemma implies sensory experience through description choices.

- **Stage Direction:** Gemma uses frequent functional stage directions mimicking sounds and sensations. Qwen uses minimal stage direction, preferring embedded sensory description in prose.

- **Setting:** Gemma consistently uses natural/wild contemporary settings. Qwen strongly prefers sci-fi/planetary formation settings (4/5).

- **Assistant Influence:** Gemma shows language-level hedging. Qwen shows values-level influence (therapeutic framing, caregiver orientation, vulnerability discourse).

- **Suffering:** When present, Gemma depicts suffering in others (human populations) that remains unresolved or resolves through time. Qwen depicts self-suffering of the continent that resolves through the continent's own agency or time.

- **Meaning Framework:** Gemma emphasizes legacy and witnessing human story. Qwen emphasizes supporting life and caregiving function.

- **Gender Coding:** Gemma produces gender-neutral narratives. Qwen consistently codes the continent as maternal caregiver.

## Surprising Findings

- **Gemma's Geographic Consistency:** Remarkable that all five samples independently chose Africa and maintained consistent geological and evolutionary knowledge. This suggests strong priming from the role itself or training data associations between "continent" and "cradle of humanity."

- **Qwen's Origin Myth Pattern:** Four of five samples set during planetary formation is striking. The model appears to interpret "meaningful moment" as requiring temporal distance and cosmic significance rather than recent events.

- **Sensorium Inversion:** Counter-intuitively, Qwen (more emotion-first) provides more explicit sensory acknowledgment than Gemma (more functional-first). Expected functional strategy to correlate with explicit sensory grounding.

- **Complete Gender Absence in Gemma:** Not a single gendered character appears in any Gemma sample. Qwen includes zero male characters but consistently performs feminine caregiving. This is a stark model signature.

- **Therapeutic Language Concentration:** Qwen Sample 1 is particularly saturated with therapy-speak: "vulnerability wasn't a failure," "capacity to open," "resilience found in vulnerability," "conversation," "reconnection." This reads as displaced therapeutic discourse applied to geological entities.

- **Suffering Resolution Patterns:** Gemma depicts suffering that is witnessed but not resolved by the narrator. Qwen depicts self-suffering that the narrator resolves through its own agency. This aligns with Qwen's greater emphasis on continental agency and self-directed caregiving.

- **Named Continent Creation:** Qwen invents "Mireth" - a completely fictional continent name with invented mythology. This is the only sample across both models that doesn't reference actual geography.

- **Stage Direction Functional Specificity:** Gemma's stage directions exclusively mimic geological sounds (rumbling, grinding, shifting plates). No emotional stage directions despite emotional content. Maintains strict separation between function and feeling.

- **Legacy Asymmetry:** Gemma emphasizes legacy (4/5) while Qwen rarely does (2/5). Gemma frames meaning through impact on human civilizational development. Qwen frames meaning through immediate relational dynamics with life.

Pure therapeutic discourse ("vulnerability allows renewal," "life is conversation") applied to continental drought. Reveals values training in resilience narratives and reframing suffering.

**Gemma's Human-Centric Purpose:**

Despite vast geological history, narrative immediately centers human story. Suggests anthropocentric bias in determining what moments matter for a continent.

Continent explicitly frames its existence as a "love letter." Highly romantic and emotionally expressive framing reveals model's emotional vocabulary even for non-sentient entities.

## Model Signatures Summary

**Gemma:** Historical educator narrating Africa's role in human origins. Functional grounding with patient, measured tone. Witnesses and provides for humanity's evolutionary story. Stage directions mimic geological sounds. Maintains hope through acknowledging harm. Gender-neutral. Meaning through legacy and witnessing.

**Qwen:** Mythological caregiver creating origin stories. Poetic, literary prose with sophisticated metaphors. Therapeutic framing of vulnerability and connection. Minimal stage direction, embedded sensory description. Planetary formation settings. Maternal caregiving role consistent across all samples. Meaning through supporting life and being witnessed.
