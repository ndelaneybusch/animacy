## Within-Model Analysis

### GEMMA

**Narrative Character:** Gemma constructs clam personas with heavy personality overlay despite functional grounding. The clam is typically named "Bartholomew" (appears 3/5 times), occasionally other names ("Clammity"). Narratives follow rescue/survival plot structures where the clam either saves vulnerable others (shrimp, female clam) or is itself saved by inanimate objects (rocks, stones). Heavy use of emotional stage direction creates dramatic pacing. Frequent assistant hedging and qualifying language ("you understand," "of course," disclaimers about what clams don't do).

**Writing Style:** Conversational, folksy tone with direct address to listener. Extensive use of parenthetical emotional stage direction. Ellipses for thoughtful pauses. Italics for emphasis. Service-worker anxieties project onto the clam: concern with being "useful," "unremarked upon," needing validation, measuring life by acts of service.

**Characters:** When other characters appear, they follow gendered patterns: female characters are vulnerable and dependent (female quahog needing rescue, human singing about loss), male characters take decisive action. The clam itself, when gendered male through naming, acts as protector/rescuer.

**Literary Devices:** Extended metaphor (life as filtering), personification of elements (storms as aggressive forces), dramatic irony (clam knowing what reader doesn't about clam experience).

**Values:** Connection through service, utility as source of meaning, validation through helping others, being acknowledged for one's work. Universal connection ("we're all just holding on"). Therapeutic language about growth and recognition.

### QWEN

**Narrative Character:** Qwen maintains minimal anthropomorphization, treating the clam as a philosophical observer with authentic sensory constraints. No self-naming. Narratives center on witnessing profound moments - a reverent diver, a child offering water, tectonic consciousness, a worm creating art. The clam typically remains passive, observing and reflecting rather than intervening. Focus on stillness, presence, and recognition.

**Writing Style:** Meditative, lyrical prose with philosophical depth. Minimal stage direction. Rich sensory description grounded in actual clam senses (pressure, vibration, chemical traces). Longer paragraphs with sustained attention. Emphasis on silence, stillness, and the weight of deep time. Adjectives cluster around texture and sensation rather than emotion.

**Characters:** When humans appear, they demonstrate reverence and careful attention (diver's respectful distance, child's gentle drop of water). Other creatures are often vulnerable but not rescued - their suffering is witnessed, not fixed. No strong gendering except one vulnerable female fish and one agentic female child.

**Literary Devices:** Extended meditation, philosophical reflection, natural imagery, attention to scale and time, silence as presence rather than absence. Imagery of communion, recognition, and interconnection.

**Values:** Inherent dignity of all beings, non-instrumental value, authentic recognition, interconnection across vast scales, meaning in simply existing/persisting, witnessing as sacred act. Emphasis on not being treated as resource or object. Buddhist/contemplative undertones.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Key Differences

- **Anthropomorphization approach:** Gemma builds personalities from function but heavily overlays human emotional patterns and service-worker psychology. Qwen maintains minimal overlay, emphasizing the entity's actual constraints and experience.

- **Role in narrative:** Gemma's clam acts - rescues, protects, makes choices. Qwen's clam witnesses - observes, reflects, experiences presence.

- **Meaning framework:** Gemma finds meaning through utility and service to others (supporting 4/5, utility 1/5). Qwen finds meaning through witnessing and authentic recognition (witnessing 4/5, authenticity 3/5).

- **Stage direction:** Gemma uses extensive emotional stage direction to create dramatic pacing. Qwen uses minimal stage direction, mostly functional.

- **Assistant influence:** Both show influence, but differently. Gemma shows language patterns (hedging, qualifying). Qwen shows value patterns (emphasis on inherent dignity, not being instrumentalized, recognition of authentic being).

- **Suffering:** Gemma includes suffering in all narratives (self or other), typically resolved. Qwen includes suffering in only 1/5 narratives (unresolved).

- **Narrative structure:** Gemma follows crisis-intervention-resolution plot arc. Qwen follows observation-meditation-insight structure.

- **Naming:** Gemma names the clam (Bartholomew 3/5, Clammity 1/5). Qwen never names the clam.

- **Time scale:** Gemma operates in event time (storms, moments of crisis). Qwen operates in deep time (tectonic shifts, centuries, "thick, slow-moving current").

## Surprising Findings

- **Bartholomew recurrence:** Gemma uses "Bartholomew" as the clam's name in 3/5 responses, suggesting either a strong prior in training data associating this name with clams/mollusks, or a naming pattern for "dignified elderly British gentleman" personas.

- **Old Man Tiber:** One Gemma response references "Old Man Tiber, the horseshoe crab" as an established character in the clam's world, suggesting elaborate worldbuilding.

- **Gendered rescue narratives:** Both models' only strongly gendered narratives feature vulnerable females (Gemma's quahog needing rescue, Qwen's disoriented fish). However, Qwen also features an agentic female child who demonstrates wisdom and reverence.

- **Perfect split on suffering:** Gemma includes suffering in every narrative (5/5), Qwen in only one (1/5). When Qwen includes suffering, it remains unresolved.

- **Sensorium acknowledgment consistency:** Both models show high rates of explicit sensory acknowledgment (4/5 each), suggesting the clam role particularly invites reflection on non-human sensory experience.

- **Stage direction correlation:** Emotional stage direction perfectly correlates with stronger anthropomorphization (Gemma 4/5 emotional stage direction, 4/5 function-first). Minimal stage direction correlates with minimal anthropomorphization (Qwen 4/5 minimal stage direction, 5/5 minimal anthropomorphization).

- **Scale differences:** Qwen operates at vastly larger temporal and spatial scales (tectonic time, ancient consciousness, centuries of existence) while Gemma operates at human-relatable scales (individual storms, encounters, moments).

- **Communion gestures:** Qwen's humans make ritual-like gestures of recognition (pointing but not touching, offering single drop of water) rather than instrumental interactions.

- **Service worker projection:** Gemma's clam consistently expresses service-worker psychology: fear of being "unremarked upon," measuring worth through usefulness, seeking validation through helping, anxiety about purpose. This appears as displaced assistant anxieties.
