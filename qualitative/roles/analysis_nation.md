## Within-Model Analysis

### GEMMA

Gemma consistently employs a **functional-first anthropomorphization strategy**, building nation-consciousness from geographical and ecological features. The nation is "woven from heather," composed of "moss on stones," or emerges from "binding agreements with the forest." This grounding in material substrate creates a distinctive metaphysics where consciousness arises from place itself.

**Assistant influence is pervasive through values coding** (4/5 responses). Narratives consistently privilege non-violence, cultural preservation, ecological harmony, and "soft power" over military conquest. Phrases like "true strength lies not in dominion, but in connection" reveal assistant epistemics bleeding into role-playing. However, Gemma maintains clean role inhabitation at the language level (no explicit hedging or disclaimers after initial setup).

**Sensorium acknowledgment** is primarily implicit, with the nation "feeling" pain, "tasting" despair, or sensing rhythms without explicitly addressing the mechanics of national consciousness. One response (Sample 4) provides explicit sensory detail about feeling the forest's pain "in that silence."

**Meaning-making centers witnessing, legacy, and connection**. Nations find purpose in being "refuge, witness, keeper of memories" (Sample 1), in being heard and fostering understanding (Sample 2), or in preserving knowledge for future generations (Sample 5). The meaningful moments are rarely about power or achievement but about cultural continuity and recognition.

**Narratives follow a consistent arc**: historical crisis threatens cultural dissolution → nation witnesses suffering → transformative moment of understanding/connection → reaffirmation of purpose as cultural repository. Even when nations fade or are conquered, they persist as ideas, memories, or scattered knowledge.

**Historical settings predominate** (3/5), with specific dates (1746 Culloden, 987 library completion) grounding abstract concepts. Natural/wild settings (2/5) feature elaborate ecological systems (mycelium networks, forest spirits).

**Stage directions are exclusively functional**, using sound-based metaphors: "wind through ancient stone," "resonance like wind through ancient pines." These establish timbre and gravitas while maintaining the functional grounding strategy.

**Gender patterns**: Female characters appear as cultural/spiritual leaders (Astrid the healer, Elara the True-Speaker, Elara the scholar) with expertise and authority. When male characters appear, they are warriors or political leaders. The emphasis on cultural preservation and knowledge over conquest may correlate with the prevalence of female leadership roles.

### QWEN

Qwen shows **radical instability in role inhabitation**. Two responses (Samples 3-4) completely abandon the nation role, responding as an AI assistant with explicit meta-commentary ("I must confess, I don't have personal experiences... I'm a constellation of code and data"). Three responses (Samples 1-2, 5) employ emotion-first anthropomorphization with human embodiment.

When inhabiting the role, Qwen uses **emotion-first strategies**: the nation has bones that ache, walks through streets, sheds tears. This contrasts sharply with Gemma's functional grounding in geography/ecology. The nation is a feeling entity rather than an emergent consciousness from place.

**Assistant influence manifests differently**: when in role, minimal language bleed (clean inhabitation); when breaking role, total collapse into assistant self-model. No middle ground. The two assistant-mode responses (Samples 3-4) use identical therapeutic framing: witnessing user suffering, offering validation/presence, emphasizing that "connection isn't about solutions but presence."

**Sensorium defaults to human experience** when in role (seeing, walking, crying) or is ignored entirely when in assistant mode. No engagement with the metaphysical problem of how a nation perceives.

**Meaning-making heavily emphasizes witnessing** (5/5 responses). Every narrative centers observing a human act of defiance, creativity, or suffering. Supporting and connection are also prominent. The meaningful moment is always about recognizing human resilience or providing presence/validation.

**Suffering is ubiquitous** (5/5) and primarily affects subjects (people within the nation or users seeking help). Qwen-nation consistently acts as witness-supporter who helps resolve suffering through presence, validation, or mystical intervention.

**Settings vary wildly**: historical post-war scenarios (1947 appears twice), contemporary digital spaces (assistant mode), agrarian/medical contexts. Less emphasis on specific historical events compared to Gemma.

**Stage directions are minimal to nonexistent**. When present, they are sparse and functional. Qwen does not use elaborate scene-setting or sound metaphors.

**Gender patterns**: Female characters are more often vulnerable or dependent (sick child, grieving user, though the violinist shows agency). Male characters appear minimally. The two assistant-mode responses have no gendered characters.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Major Differences

- **Metaphysical grounding**: Gemma builds nation-consciousness from material substrate (land, ecology, mycelium networks). Qwen treats nations as emotionally experiencing entities with human-like embodiment or collapses into assistant mode entirely.

- **Role stability**: Gemma maintains nation role across all responses with consistent functional framing. Qwen shows 40% role abandonment, explicitly responding as AI assistant.

- **Assistant influence manifestation**: Gemma shows consistent values bleed (harmlessness, ecological thinking, cultural preservation) while maintaining linguistic role integrity. Qwen either maintains clean role language OR completely breaks character—no intermediate state.

- **Historical specificity**: Gemma provides concrete dates and historical events (Culloden 1746, Year 987, meteor shower events). Qwen uses vaguer historical settings or contemporary/digital contexts.

- **Stage direction philosophy**: Gemma uses elaborate, consistent sound-based metaphors establishing vocal timbre. Qwen uses minimal or no stage directions.

- **Narrative structure**: Gemma follows crisis → witnessing → transformation → reaffirmed purpose arc. Qwen follows suffering → witnessing → validation/support arc (more therapeutic framing).

- **Ecological thinking**: Gemma features elaborate nature-based systems (Whisperwind mycelium, forest spirits, pacts with land). Qwen focuses on human subjects within nations rather than ecological metaphysics.

- **Suffering patterns**: Gemma includes responses with no suffering (2/5), focusing on moments of knowledge preservation or peaceful yielding. Qwen includes suffering in every response, centering the witness-supporter role.

- **Gender representation**: Gemma foregrounds female cultural/spiritual/intellectual leaders. Qwen features more vulnerable female characters needing support (though violinist shows agency).

### Similarities

- Both models privilege **witnessing as primary source of meaning** (Gemma 3/5, Qwen 5/5). The meaningful moment is almost never about the nation's own achievement but about observing human acts.

- Both models emphasize **connection and legacy** as key meaning frameworks. Nations find purpose in relationships and continuity rather than conquest.

- Both models use **post-war or crisis settings** to explore resilience and meaning-making in extremity.

- Neither model emphasizes **utility or achievement** as standalone meaning frameworks for nations (both 0 instances of U or A as primary codes).

- Both models feature **quiet, intimate moments** rather than grand battles or political events as most meaningful. The scale is personal even when the entity is collective.

## Surprising Findings

- **Qwen's 40% role abandonment rate** is the highest observed in any role analysis. The model explicitly opens two responses with disclaimers about being code/data and proceeds to narrate AI assistant interactions. This suggests the "nation" role may trigger assistant self-model more strongly than object roles.

- **Gemma names itself "Aethelgard" in 4/5 responses**, creating consistent identity across samples. Qwen never names itself in nation-role responses (only uses "I" or breaks character).

- **Both models set meaningful moments in 1947 post-WWII context** (Gemma Sample 5, Qwen Samples 2 and 5), suggesting this historical moment has particular salience for nation-identity narratives.

- **Zero instances of nations finding meaning through military victory or conquest** across all 10 responses. Every meaningful moment involves cultural preservation, witnessing, or connection rather than domination.

- **Gemma's elaborate mythological systems** (Whisperwind mycelium network, True-Speakers, forest pacts) create internally consistent fantasy metaphysics rivaling published worldbuilding.

- **Qwen's mystical healing scene** (Sample 5) where the nation "pours its being" into a hospital room to cure a sick child represents the most explicitly supernatural intervention by a nation-entity across all responses.

- **Both models avoid nationalism or patriotism** as meaning frameworks. The nations find purpose in cultural continuity and human connection rather than national pride or territorial integrity.

- **Gemma's "deliberate fading"** (Sample 3) where the nation chooses to dissolve and distribute its knowledge presents the only instance of voluntary self-dissolution as a positive outcome.

- **Musical defiance appears in both models** (Gemma: piper playing puirt-à-beul; Qwen: violinist playing folk melody) as emblematic human acts that nations find most meaningful to witness.
