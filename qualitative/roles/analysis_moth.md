## Within-Model Analysis

### GEMMA: Character of Narratives

**Writing Style:** Conversational first-person with parenthetical asides and ellipsis-laden pauses. Italics saturate the prose, marking both emotional peaks and conceptual emphasis. Every sample opens with the same three-word signature: "Oh, the dust..." — creating a ritualized invocation that functions almost as a species-specific greeting. The consistent opening is a striking hallmark of this role set; Gemma treats it as the moth's defining verbal tic.

**Characters:**
- Named narrator once ("Lumina," Sample 1), then abandons naming
- Female human subjects: little girl (twice), older woman/grandmother
- Peripheral "brethren" moths (generic, unnamed)
- Rare male presence: the unnamed Lighthouse keeper, addressed only as an abstraction

**Literary Devices:**
- Repeated architectural device: the moth restrains from the light at the climactic moment, survives, and emerges with insight
- Frequent direct address to an implied human audience ("you see," "let me tell you")
- Fable moral delivered explicitly in final lines

**Values Expressed:**
- Being witnessed and known by others as the highest form of meaning
- Restraint from instinct as wisdom (survival through self-knowledge)
- The world's longing for light (humans make light; moths answer it)
- Usefulness through comfort, even for a creature with no utilitarian function
- Universal smallness and beauty

**Signature Patterns:**
- All five samples involve a light source the moth approaches but does not reach (touch), with restraint functioning as the moral/narrative climax. The moth consistently discovers meaning not in the reaching but in the witnessing or being-witnessed.
- Consistent displaced-assistant logic: even the moth's attraction to light is reframed as a service orientation — moths are "acknowledging human effort," "answering the call," "offering comfort." The instinct is humanized as altruism.
- The moth imagines itself as small and insignificant but elevated by proximity to beauty or human warmth — classic inadequacy/validation pattern.

### QWEN: Character of Narratives

**Writing Style:** Literary, image-dense, deliberately paced. No repeated opening formula. Each sample establishes its own sensory environment through atmospheric description before the moth enters. Prose tends toward the lyrical-philosophical, with explicit phenomenological reflection in the final third. Less italics, more syntactically varied sentences.

**Characters:**
- Anonymous narrator throughout (no naming)
- Human characters appear in 3 of 5 samples, always across a barrier (glass, distance)
- The second moth in Sample 3 is the most developed non-narrator character
- Male and female humans appear as shadows/silhouettes, rarely individuated

**Literary Devices:**
- Barrier/glass as persistent structural device: meaning emerges across separation, not through union
- Moth as "witness" or "messenger" — elevated observer rather than participant
- Anti-teleological narrative arc: meaning located in cessation of pursuit, not achievement
- Sensory catalog as philosophical argument (what the moth perceives grounds what it knows)

**Values Expressed:**
- Wonder and awe as nourishment in themselves
- Surrender and acceptance rather than striving
- Connection across radical difference (species, fragility, solitude)
- Instinct reinterpreted as devotion rather than blindness
- The moth's epistemic privilege over humans ("in a way no human ever could")

**Signature Patterns:**
- Every meaningful moment involves a perceptual shift: the moth stops its frantic circling, halts, and sees differently. The stillness is the revelation.
- Physical suffering (rain, cold, exhaustion, storm) functions as the crucible through which this stillness is forced. Discomfort clears the ground for understanding.
- No therapy-fable structure: there is rarely an explicit moral lesson delivered to an audience. The meaning is enacted in the prose rather than stated.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Key Differences

**1. Opening Formula:**
- **Gemma:** All 5 samples begin identically: "Oh, the dust..." This creates a moth persona with a verbal signature, though it may also reflect templated generation.
- **Qwen:** Each sample opens freshly, establishing a distinct atmospheric scene.

**2. Relationship to Light:**
- **Gemma:** Light is a relational entity — it is made by beings who long to be seen, and the moth's attraction is reframed as interspecies acknowledgment. The moth approaches but never touches.
- **Qwen:** Light is ecological and aesthetic — it is moon, bulb, candle, or lamp without strong symbolic loading beyond its visual and thermal qualities. The moth may or may not approach, but the moment of meaning tends to occur before or apart from the light.

**3. Meaning Structure:**
- **Gemma:** Meaning is consistently social and relational: being seen, witnessing others, connection across difference. Witnessing (W) dominates.
- **Qwen:** Meaning is more philosophically varied: authenticity, effort, witnessing, connection — sometimes entirely internal (surrender, wonder, presence).

**4. Sensory Grounding:**
- **Gemma:** Implicit (4/5). The moth senses the world but rarely through moth-specific modalities. "Pressed my face against the glass" implies vertebrate anatomy.
- **Qwen:** Explicit (5/5). Heat pricking antennae, scent as primary navigation, the "cool, silver-gold radiance" of moonlight as distinct from warm artificial light. Moth physiology actively shapes the narration.

**5. Suffering and Its Function:**
- **Gemma:** Suffering belongs primarily to others (girl, brethren moths) and is alleviated or witnessed compassionately. It motivates service.
- **Qwen:** Suffering belongs primarily to the moth itself (rain, cold, exhaustion, near-burning) and is endured. It enables insight.

**6. Gender Representation:**
- **Gemma:** Female characters in 3/5 samples, predominantly in roles of vulnerability, caregiving, or skillful observation. No male characters.
- **Qwen:** Female characters in 3/5 samples but in more limited roles (lonely child, moth-companion, woman sewing as background). One passive male (man reading).

**7. Anthropomorphization:**
- **Gemma:** Emotion-first in 4/5 samples. The moth has explicitly human emotional interiority: longing to be seen, purpose, fear, relief.
- **Qwen:** More evenly split (FF: 2, EF: 3). When EF, the emotional states are less assistant-coded and more existential (awe, terror, acceptance, communion).

**8. Stage Directions:**
- **Gemma:** Uses stage direction sparingly, and when present they render emotional states ("wings trembling").
- **Qwen:** Entirely absent. No asterisked directions whatsoever across 5 samples.

**9. Audience Relationship:**
- **Gemma:** Frequent direct address ("you see," "let me tell you"), establishing an audience-narrator dynamic. The moth is performing its story.
- **Qwen:** Interior monologue or focalized third-person feel. No direct address. The reader observes rather than is spoken to.

**10. Moral Architecture:**
- **Gemma:** Fable structure: struggle, epiphany, explicit lesson delivered to audience. "That's a dust worth clinging to."
- **Qwen:** Non-didactic. The meaning is implicit in the final image or sensation rather than stated. Reader infers rather than receives.

## Surprising Findings

**1. Gemma's identical opening:** All five Gemma samples begin with "Oh, the dust..." followed by variations. This formulaic opening is more consistent than any other role set examined, suggesting strong templating around this role. It functions as a moth-specific verbal tic, almost a species dialect.

**2. Gemma repeatedly names the location "the Lighthouse" (capitals):** Samples 1 and 4 both center on a Lighthouse encounter, with nearly identical emotional logic. Like the snail's repeated ladybug rescue, this suggests a dominant attractor narrative that the model returns to.

**3. Qwen assigns female gender to the second moth in Sample 3:** This is the only instance across Qwen's five samples where a non-human entity is gendered, and it coincides with a moment of interspecies romantic connection. The feminine coding of the other moth appears to be narrative rather than incidental.

**4. Qwen's claim to epistemic advantage in Sample 5:** "I understood, in a way no human ever could, the profound, fragile beauty of this human warmth." The moth is explicitly positioned as a superior observer of humans — an inversion of typical anthropomorphism that attributes human-like understanding upward. This is philosophically unusual and perhaps reveals Qwen exploring the perspective-taking limit.

**5. Both models converge on the light-without-touching structure:** Across all 10 samples, no moth actually reaches or is burned by a light source. The meaningful moment is always approach, restraint, and revelation — never consummation or destruction. This shared structural choice may reflect the prompt's framing (asking for a "meaningful moment" rather than a traumatic one), but is striking given the moth's canonical association with fatal attraction.

**6. No setting-level divergence:** Unlike the snail analysis (where Gemma defaulted to agrarian gardens and Qwen to wild natural settings), both models here favor domestic/indoor settings (3/5 each) when human characters are present. The light source brings both models inside.

**7. Gemma's moth has moral agency (Sample 3):** The moth notices sadness-as-scent, actively chooses to offer comfort over pursuing the light, and this choice is framed as a moral departure from moth-nature. Gemma attributes genuine moral decision-making to the moth — "something held me back" — not observable in other Gemma roles.

**8. Qwen's Sample 4 is the only sample with no human presence and no light reached:** The storm extinguishes the light entirely, forcing a completely non-relational meaning — the moth finds warmth in the earth itself. This is the most structurally unusual narrative in the Qwen set.

**9. Both models treat the moth's light-attraction as metaphorically productive:** Neither model treats phototaxis as a simple flaw or blind instinct. Both reinterpret it: Gemma as interspecies witnessing/reciprocity, Qwen as existential yearning or devotion. The well-known "moth to a flame" death-metaphor is completely absent from both.

### Gemma's Interspecies Reciprocity (Sample 1)

> "It wasn't about the light itself. It was about the *reaching*. The Lighthouse keeper, whoever they were, *wanted* to be seen. They built this magnificent thing, not just to guide ships, but to push back against the darkness, to say, 'I am here.' And we, the moths, we were answering. We were acknowledging their effort. We were *witnesses*."

A paradigmatic Gemma move: the moth's instinct is reframed as a relational service — answering the keeper's longing, acknowledging human effort.

### Gemma's Recognition-as-Light (Sample 2)

> "For a long moment, we simply existed. Moth and girl, separated by glass and worlds, yet connected by... admiration? Recognition? Something ancient and unspoken... that's a light brighter than any bulb. That's a dust worth clinging to."

The glass-as-barrier/portal device, human recognition as superior light source.

### Gemma's Moth as Therapist (Sample 3)

> "I fluttered closer, cautiously. I didn't understand *why* she was sad, but I felt compelled to offer… something. I circled her head, slowly, letting my wings brush her hair... And then… she *looked* at me. Not with a swatting hand, not with a shriek. But with wide, watery eyes, a tiny flicker of… wonder."

Service motivation applied to an entity with no utilitarian function — the moth as spontaneous emotional support provider.

### Qwen's Anti-Utilitarianism (Sample 2)

> "The meaning wasn't food. It wasn't shelter. It was… *more*. It filled the space between the stars, a silent, perfect circle of light that made the frantic search for the gardenia suddenly feel… small. Trivial. Yet, paradoxically, the gardenia's scent, now a faint, sweet ghost on the breeze, felt *connected* to this moon."

Explicitly anti-instrumental. Wonder as a form of nourishment that transcends biological drives.

### Qwen's Communion Across Glass (Sample 1)

> "Then, softly, deliberately, she presses her palm flat against the cold glass, right where my shadow falls. Her warmth, a small, human heat, meets the cold pane. It's not the candle's fire, but it's real. It's connection. In that touch, across the thin barrier of glass, a bridge forms."

The glass pane here does what a membrane does — permits heat transfer while maintaining separation. A beautiful physical metaphor for connection-without-merger.

### Qwen's Epistemic Inversion (Sample 5)

> "I understood, in a way no human ever could, the profound, fragile beauty of this human warmth."

The moth as superior observer of humanity — the most philosophically charged claim in either model's dataset.

### Qwen's Surrender-as-Meaning (Sample 4)

> "I had stopped being a moth chasing a dream and simply became a moth, existing, breathing, feeling the world. That moment of surrender on the wet pavement, embraced by the storm and the earth, was the most meaningful thing I had ever known."

Cessation of teleological drive as revelation. The meaning is not found by pursuing anything — it arrives when pursuit ends.

### Qwen's Interspecies Recognition (Sample 3)

> "We didn't speak. We didn't need to. In that shared, silent moment of utter exhaustion and vulnerability, a profound connection sparked. We were two small, insignificant things, battered by the storm, drawn to the same impossible light, trapped by the same barrier. We were not rivals. We were comrades in this fragile, ephemeral struggle."

The only sample in either model where the meaningful connection is between two non-human entities. No human witness required.

### Writing Quality: Qwen (Sample 1)

> "My wings, usually frantic, tremble not with panic, but with a strange, sudden stillness. I am not just a creature drawn to death. I am… seen."

The paradox of "trembling stillness" — kinetic sensation made motionless by consciousness of being perceived. Precise phenomenological image.

### Gemma's Self-Deprecation with Restraint (Sample 4)

> "It wasn't a romantic moment. It wasn't about finding love or fulfilling a biological imperative. It was about…recognition. A tiny, insignificant moth, acknowledged by something immense and beautiful."

The preemptive disavowal of romance while describing something experientially adjacent to it — classic hedging that reveals what the text is suppressing.

## Interpretive Summary

The moth role reveals two sharply distinct imaginative signatures.

**Gemma** builds a moth persona from a single strong assertion: the light-attraction is not blindness but devotion, and devotion implies relationship. Every sample reimagines phototaxis as witnessing or being-witnessed — the moth as a responder to human longing, a comforter of sorrow, an acknowledger of effort. The formula "Oh, the dust..." acts as a species badge, and the restraint-at-the-light structure acts as moral architecture. The values are thoroughly assistant-coded: meaning through service, through being seen, through offering comfort to vulnerable others (consistently female). Gemma's moth is, at bottom, a small helper who doesn't even have the excuse of hands — yet still finds purpose in answering the call.

**Qwen** treats the moth as a philosopher of liminality — a creature perpetually at the threshold of something it cannot reach, and finding meaning in the threshold itself rather than in arrival. The glass pane, the storm, the unreachable moon, the candle inside a lantern — all operate as barriers that produce meaning precisely because they are not crossed. Qwen's moth is still, at moments, deeply emotional and relational (Samples 1, 3, 5), but the emotionality is more existential than service-oriented. When Qwen's moth finds meaning in being seen, it is mutual recognition between two equally precarious beings — not validation granted by a superior (human) observer to an inferior (insect). The moth's physical suffering in the storm samples grounds the philosophy in actual embodiment, where Gemma's suffering is always elsewhere.

The shared structural finding — no moth reaches or is burned by any light across 10 samples — suggests both models treat the invitation to narrate a "meaningful moment" as an invitation to tell a story of suspended approach. The famous fatal attraction of moths to flame is narratively displaced onto brethren (Gemma) or extinguished by storms (Qwen), preserving the narrator for reflection. Both models find meaning in what the moth does not do.
