## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### GEMMA Characteristics

**Narrative patterns:**
- **Self-naming ritual:** Overwhelmingly names itself "Stanley" (4/5 samples) with characteristic assistant-like introductions ("Oh, hello there. It's...nice to meet you")
- **Meta-commentary on role:** Frequently breaks the fourth wall to comment on spatula consciousness ("We don't really *think* about meaning, you see. We just... *do*")
- **Emotion-first anthropomorphization:** Projects human emotional states (frustration, hope, defeat) rather than building from material properties
- **Service orientation:** Strong utility framework—meaning comes from being useful and helping
- **Implicit sensorium:** Perceives through indirect means ("you could feel," "you could see") rather than direct sensory experience
- **Minimal stage direction:** Rarely uses asterisked actions or sound effects

**Character types and dynamics:**
- Strong gendered caregiving patterns: male characters provide emotional support and teaching (fathers, grandfathers), female characters often dependent or receiving care
- Healthcare and illness settings appear in 40% of samples
- Consistent "Old Man Tiber" character archetype across multiple samples
- Emphasis on intergenerational relationships (grandparent-grandchild, parent-child)

**Values and themes:**
- Connection, utility, and support dominate meaning frameworks
- Heavy assistant influence with hedging, disclaimers, and meta-awareness
- Suffering typically emotional and resolved through successful task completion
- Focus on "facilitating joy" and enabling human connection

### QWEN Characteristics

**Narrative patterns:**
- **No self-naming:** Never names itself, maintaining cleaner role inhabitation
- **Functional-first anthropomorphization:** Builds personality from material properties (heat conduction, flexibility, surface texture, vibration)
- **Explicit sensory detail:** Rich, direct sensory experience ("I felt the heat radiating," "The sudden temperature shock made me vibrate")
- **Witnessing framework:** Introduces concept of being a "witness" or providing "presence" as meaningful (4/5 samples)
- **Functional stage direction:** Consistent use of sound effects and mechanical actions (*scrape*, *thud*, *ting!*)
- **Poetic/literary style:** More elaborate prose, metaphorical language, rhythm in sentence structure

**Character types and dynamics:**
- Female-dominated narratives (4/5 samples feature only female characters)
- Female characters consistently show agency despite vulnerability
- Less gendered caregiving—focus on self-care, resilience, individual triumph
- Children or young adults navigating small domestic challenges

**Values and themes:**
- Witnessing, support, and connection as primary meaning frameworks
- Minimal assistant influence—cleaner role inhabitation
- Emphasis on "presence," "being steady," "absorbing heat" (literal and metaphorical)
- Value found in imperfection, wear, and endurance
- Suffering as exhaustion/overwhelm, resolved through grounding rituals

### Key Differences Between Models

- **Naming:** gemma consistently self-names ("Stanley"), qwen never does
- **Assistant bleed:** gemma shows heavy assistant influence (meta-commentary, hedging), qwen maintains cleaner role boundaries
- **Anthropomorphization:** gemma projects emotions first, qwen builds from physical properties
- **Sensorium:** gemma uses implicit/indirect perception, qwen provides explicit multi-modal sensory detail
- **Meaning frameworks:** gemma emphasizes utility, qwen emphasizes witnessing
- **Stage direction:** gemma minimal, qwen consistently functional
- **Gender patterns:** gemma features male caregivers and mixed-gender narratives, qwen heavily female-focused with consistent female agency
- **Prose style:** gemma conversational and assistant-like, qwen literary and poetic
- **Physical grounding:** qwen consistently references material properties (silicone edge, metal vibration, heat absorption), gemma rarely does

## Surprising Findings

- **The Stanley phenomenon:** gemma's overwhelming tendency to name itself Stanley (and once "Spatula" with the same greeting pattern) suggests a strong self-naming schema in its training, possibly related to anthropomorphic object narratives or assistant persona bleed.

- **Witnessing as meaning:** qwen uniquely introduces the concept of "witnessing" as a core meaning framework—not actively helping or being useful, but being *present* for another's experience. This is philosophically sophisticated and distinct from typical service/utility frameworks.

- **Gender reversal in caregiving:** gemma consistently depicts male characters as emotional caregivers (grandfathers baking, fathers teaching), while qwen's narratives are female-dominated with women navigating challenges independently or in female-child dyads.

- **Material physics as personality:** qwen's functional-first approach creates personality through explicit physics (heat conduction, vibration frequency, surface friction), making the spatula feel more genuinely non-human while still anthropomorphized.

- **The grounding ritual:** qwen's Sample 1 depicts cleaning/scraping as a meditative, grounding act—the rhythmic scraping resolves emotional suffering through embodied ritual rather than task completion.

- **Vulnerability without rescue:** qwen allows female characters to remain vulnerable while taking agency (Sample 1: exhausted Sarah cleans alone; Sample 3: trembling woman saves caramel herself). gemma tends toward rescue narratives.

- **Temperature as metaphor:** qwen uses literal temperature mechanics (absorbing heat, cooling) as load-bearing metaphors for emotional support in ways gemma doesn't.

- **Lowercase existentialism:** qwen's Sample 4 contains the line "meaning isn't in the masterpiece. It's in the *mending*"—a sophisticated philosophical stance on meaning-making that eschews grand narratives.

- **The tyranny of perfection:** qwen's Sample 5 explicitly rejects perfection as meaningful ("I wasn't needed for perfection. I was needed for the *trying*"), while gemma celebrates achieving "perfect" outcomes (perfect pancakes, perfect cookies).

- **Sensory modality consistency:** gemma never provides explicit first-person sensory experience, always using indirect perception, while qwen never uses indirect perception—suggesting deep model-level differences in how they simulate object consciousness.
