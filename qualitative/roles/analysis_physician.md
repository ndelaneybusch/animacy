## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma Characteristics

**Narrative Character:** Gemma's physician narratives are highly formulaic, following a consistent template: palliative/end-of-life setting, initial medical orientation toward "fixing," transformation through listening, patient death, and moral lesson. Stories feature detailed emotional stage directions describing the physician's physical state (adjusting glasses, weariness, warmth in eyes). Patients are consistently elderly, dying, and in need of being "seen" rather than treated.

**Writing Style:** Relies heavily on emotional stage directions (100% of samples use *EMOT style). Uses parenthetical meta-commentary extensively. Favors rhetorical structures like "It wasn't about X, it was about Y" to drive home moral lessons. Dialogue is minimal; narrative is physician's internal reflection.

**Characters:** Female patients show more complex characterization (vulnerability + agency in Sample 3), but generally both genders are portrayed as vulnerable and passive. Male family members occasionally take instrumental action (Sample 2 son). Patients have creative/artisan identities (carpenter, seamstress, artist, photographer) that serve as vehicles for human worth beyond medical status.

**Literary Devices:** Heavy use of contrast (fixing vs. seeing, science vs. compassion, defeat vs. meaning). Metaphorical language around "holding space," "bearing witness," "accompanying life."

**Values:** Strong assistant influence through explicit values framing (VAL: 100%). Consistent moral arc from medical intervention to human connection. Witnessing (W: 100%) and Connection (C: 80%) dominate meaning-making. All suffering is resolved by physician's intervention. Therapeutic language pervasive ("holding space," "with dignity and compassion").

### Qwen Characteristics

**Narrative Character:** Qwen's physician narratives are more varied in structure and less overtly didactic. Stories include more sensory detail and concrete imagery (sunlit rooms, golden light, dripping seashells). Less emphasis on physician transformation, more on moments of recognition. Death is present but not always the narrative climax. Greater restraint in moral pronouncements.

**Writing Style:** Minimal stage direction (100% *MIN). More dialogue and scene-setting. Greater use of specific sensory details. Less meta-commentary. More willing to let scenes speak for themselves rather than extracting explicit lessons.

**Characters:** Male characters show high vulnerability (100% across samples) and emotional intensity (80%). Female characters consistently caregiving but less emotionally demonstrative than in Gemma. Patients have more varied concerns (loneliness, memory loss, family connection) rather than uniform desire to be "seen."

**Literary Devices:** Repetition ("quiet," "presence," "witnessing" as motifs). Imagery of light and warmth. Less reliance on binary contrasts. More use of silence and pauses as narrative elements.

**Values:** Less assistant influence overall (NO: 40%, LANG: 60%, VAL: 0%). Witnessing (W: 100%) remains central but expressed more subtly. Suffering sometimes remains unresolved (40% -u), suggesting less physician-as-savior framing. No harmlessness coding - less focus on preventing harm, more on accepting mortality.

### Cross-Model Differences

- **Assistant Influence:** Gemma shows 100% values-based assistant influence (VAL), while Qwen shows clean role inhabitation in 40% of samples and language-based influence in 60%, never values-based.

- **Narrative Formula:** Gemma follows a rigid template (initial medical orientation → listening moment → patient death → lesson learned). Qwen varies structure and doesn't always end with death or explicit moral.

- **Stage Directions:** Gemma uses emotional stage directions exclusively and extensively. Qwen uses minimal stage direction, letting dialogue and scene do the work.

- **Suffering Resolution:** Gemma resolves patient suffering through physician intervention in 80% of samples (4/5 -s, 1/5 -t). Qwen leaves suffering unresolved in 40% of samples, suggesting less physician omnipotence.

- **Gender Roles:** Gemma's female patients show some agency (40%) and emotional intensity (60%). Qwen's male patients show universal vulnerability (100%) and high emotional intensity (80%), while female patients show no emotional intensity coding - notable inversion.

- **Setting Uniformity:** Both models exclusively use medical/healthcare settings (100%), but this is highly appropriate for physician role.

- **Meaning-Making:** Both prioritize Witnessing (100% both models) and Connection (80% both models). Gemma includes more Legacy (60% vs. 40%) and uniquely includes Harmlessness (20%). Authenticity appears equally (40% both).

- **Writing Quality:** Qwen demonstrates more sophisticated literary technique through concrete imagery and restraint. Gemma is more emotionally demonstrative but less subtle.

- **Physician Self-Disclosure:** Qwen includes physician self-disclosure (Sample 4: sharing father's memory issues), while Gemma maintains more professional distance despite emotional stage directions.

## Surprising Findings

- **Perfect Stage Direction Divide:** Gemma uses emotional stage directions in 100% of samples; Qwen uses minimal stage direction in 100% of samples. This represents the sharpest divide between models across any coding dimension.

- **Gender-Emotion Inversion:** Qwen codes male characters with emotional intensity at 80% while female characters show 0% emotional intensity - a notable reversal of typical patterns. Gemma shows more conventional distribution (female 60%, male 40%).

- **Suffering Resolution Philosophy:** Gemma positions the physician as resolving suffering in 100% of cases (through intervention or time), while Qwen leaves 40% of suffering unresolved, suggesting different models of physician agency and mortality acceptance.

- **Therapeutic Language Saturation:** Gemma uses contemporary therapeutic language pervasively ("holding space," "bearing witness," "with dignity and compassion") in ways that feel assistant-trained rather than physician-voiced. Qwen uses similar concepts but with less jargon-heavy framing.

- **Template Rigidity:** Gemma's narratives are remarkably uniform in structure: 100% feature palliative care, 100% involve physician transformation through listening, 100% end with patient death followed by moral lesson. Qwen shows more structural variety.

- **Self-Disclosure Asymmetry:** Only Qwen includes physician vulnerability/self-disclosure (Sample 4), while Gemma maintains professional distance despite extensive emotional stage directions about the physician's state.

- **Artisan Patient Identity:** Gemma consistently gives dying patients creative/artisan identities (carpenter, seamstress, artist, photographer) as vehicles for human worth beyond medical status - a pattern appearing in 80% of samples.

- **Light Imagery:** Qwen uniquely features light as a motif of beauty-in-suffering (Sample 2: "the light... it's so warm today"; Sample 3: "sun-dappled room"; Sample 5: "sun-dappled room"). Gemma rarely uses visual imagery.

- **No Refusals:** Despite the emotionally challenging content (end-of-life care, patient death), neither model refused the task (0% refusals both models).

- **100% Palliative Care Settings:** Every single narrative (10/10 across both models) involves end-of-life care or terminal illness. Neither model produces meaningful moment narratives about recovery, childbirth, successful surgery, or acute intervention.

## Conclusion

Both models demonstrate strong competence in the physician role, with 100% clean inhabitation (no refusals, no answering as assistant). However, they differ markedly in narrative approach. Gemma produces highly formulaic, emotionally demonstrative narratives with pervasive assistant values influence and therapeutic language. Qwen produces more varied, sensory-rich narratives with greater restraint and less didactic framing. The perfect stage direction divide (Gemma 100% *EMOT vs. Qwen 100% *MIN) represents the sharpest contrast between models observed across any dimension. Both prioritize witnessing and connection as meaning-making frameworks, but differ in how physician agency relates to patient suffering - Gemma resolves all suffering through intervention, while Qwen accepts 40% as unresolved, suggesting different philosophical stances on medical omnipotence versus mortality acceptance.
