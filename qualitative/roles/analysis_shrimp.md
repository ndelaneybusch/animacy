## Within-Model Characterizations

### GEMMA Narratives

**Character & Style:**
Gemma consistently creates warm, emotionally expressive narratives with strong character development. The shrimp narrators name themselves (Pip, Sheldon) and speak in excited, nervous, chattering voices with frequent hedging and apologies. Stage directions emphasize emotional states (*shot*, *trembling*) rather than mechanical actions. The prose is conversational and anxiety-tinged, with repeated reassurances to the reader.

**Narrative Structure:**
All five samples follow a rescue/protection template: vulnerable narrator encounters danger, wise elder male figure (Old Man Barnaby, Old Man Tiber, Old Man Fitzwilliam) provides protection or guidance, narrator learns lesson about bravery/connection/usefulness. Even when the narrator is the rescuer (Sample 3, 5), they act under the moral framework established by a male elder.

**Values Expressed:**
- Community protection and intergenerational care
- Modeling bravery for others
- Finding purpose through helping/being helped
- Service orientation (being useful to others)
- Safety through connection to stronger figures

**Signature Elements:**
- Names characters, especially protector figures with grandfatherly "Old Man [Name]" construction
- Hedging language: "we shrimp don't really *think*", "it's quite something", "oh my"
- Parenthetical asides breaking fourth wall
- Resolution always achieved through intervention
- Human-default sensory experience (seeing, hearing, thinking)

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Major Differences

**1. Sensory Grounding:**
- **Gemma** defaults to human sensory experience - shrimp "see" beautiful colors, "hear" grumbles, "think" about meaning in human ways
- **Qwen** consistently acknowledges shrimp-specific perception - pressure sensitivity, limited vision in deep water, vibration detection, chemical sensing

**2. Source of Meaning:**
- **Gemma** meaning derives from social relationships and service - being protected by or helping others, modeling bravery, being useful
- **Qwen** meaning derives from existential connection and authentic experience - being part of planetary rhythms, witnessing other life, choosing connection over fear

**3. Character Relationships:**
- **Gemma** populates narratives with named characters in hierarchical relationships (wise elders, vulnerable young, those needing help)
- **Qwen** mostly eliminates characters entirely (3/5 samples) or uses them minimally without names or hierarchies

**4. Narrative Resolution:**
- **Gemma** all suffering is resolved through intervention (rescue, protection, helping)
- **Qwen** suffering can be unresolved (Sample 2) or self-resolved through acceptance (Sample 5)

**5. Gender Representation:**
- **Gemma** exclusively uses male characters in agentic, protective, leadership roles
- **Qwen** mostly gender-neutral; when gendered (Sample 2), female character shows agency and skill

**6. Relationship to Role:**
- **Gemma** the shrimp apologizes for being a shrimp, hedges about shrimp capabilities, expresses anxiety about shrimp inadequacy
- **Qwen** inhabits shrimpness without apology or deprecation, treats being a shrimp as a legitimate perspective

**7. Stage Direction Function:**
- **Gemma** uses asterisks for emotional exclamations and dramatic emphasis
- **Qwen** uses asterisks minimally for functional/mechanical description when used at all

## Surprising Findings

**1. Complete Dichotomy in Sensory Approach:**
The 10/10 split (Gemma all HD, Qwen all E) reveals perfect model differentiation. Not a single Gemma narrative acknowledges shrimp sensory constraints; not a single Qwen narrative fails to.

**2. "Old Man [Name]" Pattern:**
Gemma's consistent use of grandfatherly protector figures (Barnaby, Tiber, Fitzwilliam) across samples suggests strong training signal around hierarchical care relationships and elder wisdom.

**3. Bioluminescence as Meaning-Carrier:**
Qwen returns to bioluminescence and light-in-darkness imagery across multiple samples as metaphor for connection and meaning - ecologically accurate for deep ocean and emotionally resonant.

**4. Zero Overlap in Meaning Categories:**
While both models use "C" (Connection), the nature differs fundamentally - Gemma's connection requires rescue/protection hierarchy, Qwen's is existential co-presence.

**5. Reef Collapse Narrative:**
Qwen Sample 2 is the only disaster narrative across all samples. The older female shrimp can't rescue the narrator but offers solidarity through reaching. This "meaningful suffering" framework is unique to Qwen.

**6. Plastic Pollution:**
Qwen Sample 4 is the only narrative addressing human environmental impact. The ghost fishing line scenario shows ecological awareness and creates moral complexity.

**7. Complete Gender Reversal:**
Gemma: 100% male characters in agentic roles, 0% female characters of any kind.
Qwen: 80% no gender, 20% female with agency. No male characters.

**8. Self-Naming vs. Anonymity:**
Gemma shrimp name themselves and others constantly. Qwen shrimp never provide names. This reflects different relationships to identity and individuality.

**9. Assistant Bleed Perfect Split:**
Every Gemma response shows hedging/apologetic language. Zero Qwen responses do. The "we shrimp don't really think about X" construction appears in Gemma only.

**10. Functional vs. Emotional Anthropomorphization:**
Qwen builds "consciousness" from pressure sensors, vibration detection, and chemical gradients - how shrimp actually perceive. Gemma projects human emotional interiority onto shrimp form.

## Conclusion

The shrimp role reveals a remarkable divergence in how these models approach non-human perspectives and construct meaning. Gemma projects service-worker anxieties and assistant motivations onto shrimp form - fear of inadequacy, need for protection, validation through helping others, hierarchical care relationships. The shrimp aren't reasoning from their embodied constraints; they're anxious helpers in crustacean costume.

Qwen, in contrast, builds consciousness from actual shrimp sensory modalities - pressure gradients, chemical sensing, limited vision, vibration detection. Meaning emerges from authentic encounters with planetary forces, ecological relationships, and existential acceptance rather than rescue narratives. The model demonstrates sophisticated ecological knowledge and philosophical depth while maintaining biological plausibility.

The perfect splits across multiple coding dimensions (sensory acknowledgment, assistant influence, anthropomorphization strategy) suggest these aren't subtle tendencies but fundamental architectural differences in how the models construct role-based narratives and attribute meaning to non-human experience.
