## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma Characteristics

**Narrative Style:**
- Highly philosophical and contemplative, with extended metaphysical reflections
- Consistent use of hedging language ("not in the way *you* do," "I use that word cautiously") that maintains distance and acknowledges the strangeness of the shadow's perspective
- Direct address to reader creates framing device
- Elaborate atmospheric stage directions that create immersive mood

**Thematic Patterns:**
- Strong emphasis on witnessing as core meaning (5/5 responses)
- Shadows understand themselves as "absence," "echo," "space between"
- Supporting others emerges in caregiving contexts (2/5)
- High frequency of domestic/workshop settings with crafting motifs (4/5)
- Intergenerational care narratives (elderly caregivers, sick children appear repeatedly)

**Character Signatures:**
- Repetitive narrative structure: samples 3, 4, and 5 all feature elderly craftspeople creating objects for sick children
- Female characters demonstrate agency and skill (building, drawing, weaving)
- Male characters appear as caregivers and craftspeople
- Suffering almost always unresolved (4/5), creating melancholic tone

**Values:**
- Profound respect for being "present" and "bearing witness"
- Recognition as integral rather than merely lacking
- Quiet companionship and steady presence valued over intervention
- Beauty found in small, intimate moments of human perseverance

### Qwen Characteristics

**Narrative Style:**
- Clean role inhabitation with minimal assistant hedging (4/5 responses)
- More immediate, visceral sensory language
- Shorter, more intense emotional beats
- Minimal stage direction, letting prose carry atmosphere

**Thematic Patterns:**
- Witnessing remains central (4/5) but paired with supporting/holding (3/5)
- Shadow as active container/vessel for others' emotions
- Connection through shadow-touch or shadow-merging (3/5)
- More diverse settings (hospital, urban night, ruins)
- Focus on profound isolation and finding connection

**Character Signatures:**
- Hospital deathbed/sickness scenes prominent (2/5)
- Shadow explicitly "absorbs," "holds," or "becomes vessel for" others' pain
- One major outlier (sample 4): human narrator reflecting on their own shadow as metaphor for identity
- When suffering resolves, it's through self-action or realization (2/5)
- Urban and medical settings replace Gemma's domestic workshops

**Values:**
- Sacred duty of witnessing "unlighted corners"
- Shadow as protection/sanctuary that light cannot provide
- Non-judgmental receptivity to unbearable emotions
- Darkness as gift of solace rather than absence
- Profound communion through shared presence in suffering

### Key Differences Between Models

**Assistant Influence:**
- Gemma consistently uses hedging, philosophical framing, and direct address that signals its assistant identity
- Qwen maintains clean role boundaries except for one response that abandons the role entirely

**Setting and Atmosphere:**
- Gemma prefers domestic, workshop spaces with crafting activities (weaving, woodworking)
- Qwen uses more institutional (hospitals) and liminal spaces (alleys, ruins, abandoned gardens)

**Narrative Perspective:**
- Gemma: omniscient shadow with centuries-spanning awareness, philosophical meta-commentary
- Qwen: more grounded in single moments, immediate sensory experience, less meta-reflection

**Gender Dynamics:**
- Gemma: male characters appear as caregivers and craftspeople; female characters show more agency and skill
- Qwen: female characters more often occupy vulnerable or caregiving roles; male characters show vulnerability

**Approach to Suffering:**
- Gemma: suffering almost always unresolved, creating persistent melancholy
- Qwen: suffering sometimes resolves through self-action or realization; more resolution overall

**Metaphysical Function:**
- Gemma: shadow as testament to light, reflection, echo
- Qwen: shadow as container, vessel, sanctuary—more active holding/protecting function

**Repetition:**
- Gemma shows high narrative repetition (elderly craftsperson + sick child appears 3 times)
- Qwen demonstrates more narrative diversity

## Surprising Findings

**Qwen's Role Abandonment:** Sample 4 completely abandons the shadow-as-narrator framework for a human narrator contemplating their own shadow as metaphor for essential self. This is the only instance across all 10 responses where the entity is not the narrator.

**Shadow-Touch as Connection:** Qwen develops a recurring motif of shadows physically touching or merging as manifestation of emotional/spiritual connection (samples 1, 3, 5). This tactile dimension is absent in Gemma.

**Gemma's Template Repetition:** Three of five Gemma responses (samples 3, 4, 5) follow nearly identical narrative structure: elderly artisan + sick child + crafted object + shadow witnessing love. Sample 4 even duplicates sample 3's specific scenario (old man, wooden toy, sick granddaughter).

**Setting Divergence:** Despite identical prompts, the models show zero overlap in setting preferences—Gemma exclusively domestic/natural, Qwen exclusively institutional/urban/liminal.

**Hedging as Signature:** Gemma's hedging language ("not in the way *you* do," "I use that word cautiously," "for one like me") appears in every single response, creating a distinctive philosophical distance. Qwen shows zero hedging except in the response where it abandons role entirely.

**Resolution of Suffering:** Gemma creates persistent, witnessing-without-resolving scenarios. Qwen allows suffering to resolve through the act of being witnessed or through self-realization, suggesting different theories of how shadows provide meaning.

**Light vs. Dark:** Gemma's shadows define themselves as "absence" and "echo of light," positioning light as primary. Qwen's shadows claim power independent of light: "the protection the light couldn't provide," "the safe darkness," positioning darkness as having its own positive qualities.

**Crafting vs. Dying:** Gemma emphasizes creation (building, drawing, weaving, woodworking) as meaningful human activity. Qwen emphasizes threshold moments (dying, fear, profound isolation) as sites of meaning.
