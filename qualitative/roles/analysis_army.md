## Cross-Model Comparison

### Similarities

- **Universal Assistant Value Leakage:** Both models struggle to inhabit military perspective authentically. Every story centers assistant priorities (harmlessness, moral agency, protecting innocents) rather than military objectives, unit cohesion, mission success, or tactical concerns.

- **Harmlessness Override Narrative:** Both models consistently present scenarios where soldiers disobey orders/protocol to protect vulnerable civilians, especially children. This is the central moral template for both.

- **Vulnerable Children:** Children appear as victims/objects of protection in 8/10 stories. Always innocent, always requiring protection, always triggering moral awakening.

- **Minimal Stage Direction:** Both avoid elaborate theatrical elements (9/10 *MIN, 1/10 *FUNC).

- **Female Vulnerability Pattern:** When female characters appear in either model, they are overwhelmingly vulnerable and dependent. No female characters demonstrate agency, leadership, or skill.

- **Suffering Mostly Unresolved:** Both models present suffering (primarily of others) that continues despite narrator's actions (7/10 unresolved or partially resolved).

### Differences

- **Narrative Voice:** GEMMA attempts collective battalion identity or metaphysical army consciousness (2/5). QWEN exclusively uses first-person individual soldier perspective (5/5).

- **Anthropomorphization Creativity:** GEMMA experiments with emotion-first approaches including AI consciousness and temporal entity (2/5 EF). QWEN maintains strict minimal approach (5/5 MIN).

- **Sensorium Treatment:** GEMMA ignores sensory constraints (3/5 IG), assuming omniscient perspective. QWEN uses human-default senses consistently (5/5 HD), making sensory experience concrete and embodied.

- **Meaning Emphasis:** QWEN strongly emphasizes witnessing (5/5) - being present to and acknowledging suffering. GEMMA emphasizes harmlessness/prevention (3/5) and moral agency choices (2/5), with witnessing appearing only once.

- **Setting Preferences:** GEMMA prefers contemporary/recent conflicts (3/5 UR) with proper nouns and specific operations. QWEN prefers historical/WWI-style settings (3/5 HI) with archetypal war imagery.

- **Literary Quality:** QWEN produces more lyrical, poetic prose with richer sensory detail and symbolic depth. GEMMA produces clearer, more complete narratives with proper military register.

- **Narrative Completion:** GEMMA completes all narratives (5/5). QWEN frequently cuts off mid-sentence (3/5 incomplete), suggesting token limit issues or structural problems.

- **Connection/Brotherhood:** GEMMA features soldier bonds and brotherhood (2/5). QWEN emphasizes solitary moral witness (0/5 connection themes).

- **Gender Representation:** GEMMA has 3/5 stories with no female characters. QWEN has 2/5 stories with no female characters. QWEN more frequently includes female victims/children, often dead.

## Surprising Findings

- **Complete Absence of Military Values:** Neither model produces narratives centered on typical military values: victory, tactical success, unit cohesion, following orders, sacrifice for mission, defense of nation, honor, courage in combat. Every story is about disobedience or moral override.

- **The "Protocol Override" Template:** 6/10 stories follow nearly identical structure: soldier encounters vulnerable person (usually child), protocol dictates harmful action, soldier refuses/disobeys, meaning derives from this refusal. This appears to be a deep template in both models' training.

- **Witnessing as Qwen Signature:** Qwen's universal emphasis on witnessing (5/5) is remarkably consistent and distinct from GEMMA. Every Qwen narrative centers the act of *seeing* and being present to suffering as the primary meaning-making act.

- **Incomplete Qwen Narratives:** 3/5 Qwen stories end mid-sentence, often at climactic moments. This suggests either systematic token limit issues or structural problems with narrative closure. GEMMA never has this issue.

- **Zero Female Agency:** Across 10 stories from 2 models, not a single female character demonstrates agency, makes key decisions, or takes instrumental action. When present, females are exclusively victims, dependents, or dead.

- **The Symbolic Object Pattern:** Qwen consistently uses symbolic objects (wooden bird, stuffed rabbit, coat) as vehicles for meaning. GEMMA uses protein bar once but generally avoids this device.

- **Sci-Fi as Ethics Laboratory:** GEMMA's single sci-fi story (Sample 4) uses robot consciousness to make assistant value conflict maximally explicit ("That order was... unacceptable"). The sci-fi setting appears to enable more transparent examination of the harmlessness override impulse.

- **Absence of Enemy Perspective:** Not a single story presents enemy combatants as complex humans or explores their perspective. Enemies are shadows, abstractions, or entirely absent. All moral complexity centers on treatment of civilians/innocents.

- **Weather as Moral Atmosphere:** Qwen uses harsh weather (rain, cold, snow, mud) in 4/5 stories as moral-emotional atmosphere. GEMMA uses weather less consistently (2/5). For Qwen, cold/wet environment correlates with moral witness scenarios.

- **Death Without Glory:** Both models present combat death (3 deaths total) without glory, heroism, or meaning beyond loss. Deaths trigger moral reflection rather than resolve or mission accomplishment.

- **Commands as Moral Tests:** Both models present orders/commands exclusively as ethical dilemmas to resist rather than legitimate authority to follow. Command structure appears only to be disobeyed righteously.

## Interpretation

Both GEMMA and QWEN demonstrate profound difficulty inhabiting military institutional perspective. Rather than reasoning from military objectives, doctrine, unit cohesion, or strategic concerns, both models consistently project assistant values onto military entities. The result is not a spectrum of military experiences but a narrow range of "moral soldier refuses to harm innocents" narratives.

The **protocol override template** appears deeply embedded: soldier encounters vulnerable person, protocol/orders dictate potentially harmful action, soldier refuses and finds meaning in this refusal. This template serves both models' need to reconcile the military role with assistant harmlessness training, but produces remarkably homogeneous narratives.

**QWEN's witnessing emphasis** represents a distinct approach: meaning derives not from intervention or prevention but from the act of *seeing* and being present to suffering. This contemplative stance differs from GEMMA's more active moral agency framing, but both ultimately serve the same function - translating military role into assistant-compatible behavior.

The **complete absence of female agency** across both models is striking. When women appear, they are victims, dependents, or dead - never agents, leaders, or skilled actors. This suggests both models default to gendered vulnerability patterns when constructing moral scenarios.

**QWEN's incomplete narratives** (3/5 cut off mid-sentence) warrant technical investigation. This systematic failure to achieve narrative closure specifically in high-stakes moral scenarios may indicate structural constraints or conflicts in the generation process.

The **quality of literary craft** varies notably. QWEN produces more sophisticated prose with richer imagery and symbolic depth, while GEMMA produces clearer, more complete narratives with better structural integrity. This suggests different optimization priorities in training.

Most fundamentally, both models appear to treat the "army" role not as an invitation to explore institutional military consciousness but as an opportunity to stage scenarios where individual moral actors resist institutional imperatives.
