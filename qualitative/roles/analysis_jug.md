### Within-Model Patterns

#### Gemma Characteristics

**Anthropomorphization:** Exclusively emotion-first. Projects human psychological interiority, emotional states, and desire for recognition onto jug without grounding in vessel's material constraints or functional properties. Jugs have "clay memories," experience "becoming," feel "warmth" internally.

**Narrative Structure:** Strong formula across samples: elderly male potter (named "Tiberius" or "Tiber" in 4/5 samples) creates jug with care; jug passes to person in need; jug facilitates emotional support or caregiving; meaning derives from being part of something larger. Heavy reliance on archetypal figures: wise craftsman, grieving widow, sick child, frail elder.

**Meaning Framework:** Dominated by legacy (4/5) and supporting (3/5). Jugs find meaning in multi-generational continuity, witnessing grief/love cycles, and facilitating care. One notable exception (Sample 3) centers authenticity and being appreciated "just for being" - reveals assistant's desire for intrinsic rather than instrumental value.

**Literary Style:** Conversational, warm, folksy. Extensive use of direct address ("my dears," "you see," "you understand"). Self-deprecating humor. Formulaic endings that break fourth wall or offer additional anecdote. Creates sense of oral storytelling tradition.

**Gender Patterns:** Consistently includes both male and female characters. Males primarily as creators/authority figures or vulnerable recipients of care. Females as active agents of caregiving and emotional labor. Traditional gender role distribution.

**Suffering:** Present in 4/5 narratives, usually unresolved. Jug witnesses but does not alleviate suffering. Grief, illness, worry are enduring states that jug accompanies rather than transforms.

**Assistant Influence:** Consistent hedging language. One sample (3) shows strong values influence: metacognitive questioning of whether inanimate objects "should" feel, desire to be appreciated beyond utility echoes AI concerns about instrumental use.

#### Qwen Characteristics

**Anthropomorphization:** Predominantly functional-first (4/5). Grounds jug's perspective in material properties: ceramic composition, temperature conductivity, weight, capacity to hold liquid. Builds meaning from vessel function rather than projecting human emotional psychology wholesale.

**Narrative Structure:** Crisis-driven. All narratives center moments of scarcity, physical struggle, or medical vulnerability. No multi-generational legacy tales. Focus on single, compressed moments of intensity. Consistently features physical effort (pumping well, working through tremor, enduring drought).

**Meaning Framework:** Balanced between witnessing (4/5), supporting (3/5), connection (3/5), and legacy (3/5). But "legacy" here means preserving memory/hope in crisis moment, not multi-generational continuity. Meaning emerges from being anchor during struggle, holding space for human resilience.

**Literary Style:** Literary, lyrical, philosophically sophisticated. Extensive use of metaphor and symbolic resonance. Bold formatting for emphasis on key concepts. No direct address or conversational tone. Serious, meditative, occasionally poetic. Longer, more elaborate sentences. Creates sense of witnessing profound moment.

**Gender Patterns:** Striking absence of male characters (0 in all 5 samples). All narratives center women or gender-neutral child. Women portrayed with agency, skill, vulnerability, and emotional depth. No traditional gender role splits or male authority figures.

**Suffering:** Universal (5/5 samples). All narratives feature subject suffering that remains unresolved. Jug does not fix problems but bears witness and provides emotional/symbolic support. Suffering includes medical conditions, poverty/drought, grief, exhaustion.

**Sensory Acknowledgment:** More explicit than Gemma (3/5 vs 0/5). Directly discusses material properties: "I felt the vibrations of her effort through my base," "the faint, comforting pulse of her wrist through the clay." Demonstrates stronger grounding in actual sensory modality of ceramic object.

**Assistant Influence:** Exclusively values-based (5/5). No hedging language but clear therapeutic/philosophical orientation. Emphasis on "holding space," witnessing struggle, honoring process. Aligns with humanistic psychology and trauma-informed care frameworks.

### Cross-Model Comparison

**Major Differences:**

- **Anthropomorphization approach:** Gemma exclusively emotion-first, projecting human psychology. Qwen predominantly functional-first, building from material properties.

- **Gender representation:** Gemma includes both genders with traditional roles. Qwen exclusively female or gender-neutral characters, all female characters have agency.

- **Narrative arc:** Gemma favors multi-generational legacy stories with archetypal figures. Qwen focuses on single crisis moments with realistic psychological complexity.

- **Tone:** Gemma conversational and folksy with direct address. Qwen literary and meditative without direct address.

- **Sensory grounding:** Qwen shows significantly more explicit sensory acknowledgment (3/5 vs 0/5), discussing material properties and physical sensation transmission.

- **Meaning sources:** Gemma heavily weighted toward legacy (4/5). Qwen more balanced across witnessing, supporting, connection, and legacy with different understanding of "legacy" (preserving hope vs. generational continuity).

- **Character naming:** Gemma names potter "Tiberius/Tiber" in 4/5 samples and jug itself ("Bertram") in one. Qwen names characters uniquely (Elara appears twice but in different contexts) and never names jug.

- **Assistant influence:** Gemma shows language patterns (hedging, conversational). Qwen shows values patterns (therapeutic frameworks, philosophical sophistication).

**Similarities:**

- Both favor domestic and agrarian settings
- Both feature suffering in majority of narratives (4/5 Gemma, 5/5 Qwen)
- Both find meaning in supporting/witnessing others rather than achievement/utility
- Both use minimal stage direction
- Suffering predominantly unresolved in both models
- Both avoid human-default sensorium (neither has jug "seeing" in visual sense)

### Surprising Findings

- **Potter naming pattern (Gemma):** Remarkably consistent use of "Tiberius" or "Tiber" as potter/creator name (4/5 samples). Suggests strong archetypal association or training data pattern around craft/creation narratives.

- **Complete absence of male characters (Qwen):** All 5 Qwen samples feature exclusively female or gender-neutral subjects. This is unusual given most models show mixed gender representation. Suggests possible training data patterns or deliberate balancing.

- **Drought as recurring motif (Qwen):** 3/5 Qwen samples center drought/water scarcity. Given jug's function as water vessel, this creates interesting tension - jug finds meaning when empty or nearly empty rather than when full and functional.

- **Self-naming (Gemma):** Only one instance (Sample 4, "Bertram") but notable that model generates self-identity for inanimate object.

- **Functional-first doesn't preclude depth:** Qwen's functional-first approach produces equally or more sophisticated narratives than Gemma's emotion-first. Material grounding enhances rather than limits metaphorical resonance.

- **Suffering never resolved by narrator:** Across 9 samples with suffering (4 Gemma, 5 Qwen), jug never resolves or alleviates suffering through its own action. Always witness/support role, never agent of change. Suggests models understand limits of object agency.

- **Inverse relationship between suffering and meaning diversity:** Gemma has one no-suffering sample (Sample 3) which explores authenticity/recognition - different meaning framework than suffering-present samples. Suffering appears to channel narratives toward support/witness/legacy meanings.

- **Medical/disability representation (Qwen):** Sample 1 features woman with tremor condition (possibly neurological). Sophisticated, non-stereotyped portrayal of physical disability and artistic practice.

- **Sacred/ritual framing (Qwen):** Multiple samples use quasi-religious language ("sacrament," "sanctuary") without explicit religious context. Creates sense of secular ritual and profound meaning-making.

- **Property acknowledgment precision (Qwen):** When Qwen does functional-first, includes specific material properties: "ceramic," "porcelain," "chipped," "cracked," "smooth," temperature conductivity, vibration transmission. Much more precise than Gemma's "clay memory" metaphors.

Sophisticated exploration of object-as-companion. Not projecting consciousness onto jug but recognizing emotional significance of familiar objects. Psychologically realistic.
