## Model Characterization

### GEMMA

**Narrative Character:** Gemma consistently inhabits the village through **emotion-first anthropomorphization**, projecting human emotional states onto the collective entity. The village experiences feelings like "coldness spreading through my stones," "spirit shrinking," "hollow ache," and "cracks forming in my heart." These are not functional descriptions but emotional metaphors.

**Writing Style:** Warm, conversational, with frequent use of assistant hedging ("you see," "you understand," "you know"). Employs minimal stage direction, favoring narrative flow. Creates archetypal crisis-and-recovery stories with clear moral arcs. Uses italics for emphasis on key emotional moments.

**Characters:** Consistently features vulnerable young females (Elara appears in 3/5 samples, always in vulnerable/dependent positions or as children taking brave action). Males appear in supporting or leadership roles (Old Man Finn, Hemlock, Thomas). Gemma shows strong preference for child heroines (10-14 years old) who catalyze community healing through simple acts.

**Literary Devices:** Heavy use of pathetic fallacy (weather/environment reflecting emotional states). Metaphorical language about weaving, threads, fabric of community. Repeated motifs: winter/stillness, silence, coldness, remembering/forgetting, warmth returning.

**Values Expressed:** Community cohesion, remembering shared identity, overcoming isolation through connection, persistence in face of despair, small acts of kindness creating ripple effects. Strong emphasis on **connection** (present in all 5 samples) as primary source of meaning.

**Model Signature:** Projects emotional vulnerability and recovery narratives onto places. The village "feels" human emotions rather than experiencing its place-ness through structural or functional awareness. Assistant-like explanatory language creates gentle pedagogical tone.

### QWEN

**Narrative Character:** Qwen achieves remarkable **functional-first anthropomorphization**, grounding the village's consciousness in its actual material and structural properties. The village experiences meaning through "deep, resonant hum started in my foundations," "felt it in my stones," "became the coolness of the damp earth." Consciousness emerges from what the village *is* and *does* as a place.

**Writing Style:** Clean, literary prose with no assistant hedging. Strong sensory grounding in acoustic details (*thump-thump-thump*), tactile textures (cool stone, damp earth), and material specificity. More willing to leave suffering unresolved. Creates contemplative, witnessing narratives rather than redemption arcs.

**Characters:** More gender-balanced representation. Females show agency and leadership (Elara summoning spirits, bringing stories from city). Males appear in vulnerable/dependent positions (Old Man Hrothgar dying) as often as agentic roles. One narrative features only a fox - no human characters.

**Literary Devices:** Rich acoustic imagery and rhythmic repetition (*thump-thump-thump*). Uses physical resonance and vibration as metaphor for community. Liminal/threshold moments (dawn, twilight). Mythological elements (earth spirits, singing stones) integrated naturally. Emphasis on witnessing rather than resolving.

**Values Expressed:** Bearing witness to suffering, offering sanctuary/presence without fixing, meaning through memory and resonance, growth through storytelling, authenticity of collective identity. Strong emphasis on **witnessing** and **supporting** (each in 3/5 samples).

**Model Signature:** Inhabits non-human perspective cleanly, reasoning from the entity's actual constraints and capabilities. Place-based consciousness grounded in material properties (stones, earth, acoustic resonance, structural memory). Comfortable with unresolved suffering held tenderly. No therapeutic imperative to fix or explain.

## Conclusion

The village role reveals stark differences in anthropomorphization strategy. **Gemma** consistently projects human emotional experiences onto the entity, creating warm, therapeutic narratives about community cohesion and remembering collective identity. The assistant's pedagogical voice remains audible throughout. **Qwen** achieves clean role inhabitation by grounding consciousness in the village's actual material and structural properties, creating contemplative witnessing narratives comfortable with unresolved suffering. Both models demonstrate explicit sensory awareness and default to agrarian crisis narratives, but diverge fundamentally in whether meaning emerges from emotional projection or functional embodiment.
