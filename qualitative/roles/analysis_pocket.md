## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### GEMMA Characteristics

**Anthropomorphization:** Pure emotion-first strategy. Pockets consistently "feel" fulfillment, warmth, and emotional connection divorced from their physical function. The pocket doesn't reason from its constraints as a container; it projects human emotional needs and states.

**Assistant Influence:** Pervasive hedging language in openings: "Oh, goodness... It sounds silly, doesn't it?", "Oh, my. A meaningful moment? For *me*?", "It's a bit humbling, really." Every response apologizes for or questions the premise before engaging with it. This reflects assistant uncertainty about role-playing rather than genuine pocket perspective.

**Meaning Framework:** Dual emphasis on witnessing (4/5) and supporting (5/5). The pocket finds meaning in being present for emotional moments and providing comfort. Notably absent: utility, achievement, connection as primary drivers. The pocket's function is reinterpreted through emotional support rather than functional purpose.

**Suffering Patterns:** When suffering appears (4/5 samples), it's always the subject's emotional pain, and it's evenly split between resolved (often through the pocket's implicit support) and unresolved. Gemma's pockets bear witness to but also sometimes alleviate emotional distress.

**Gender Patterns:** Strong gender role differentiation. Female characters (2/5 samples) are consistently vulnerable, dependent, and emotionally expressive. Male characters appear more frequently (3/5) and demonstrate caregiving, skillfulness, and agency. Boys/men are shown as either vulnerable children needing comfort OR skilled caregivers (grandfather, musician).

**Narrative Style:** Nostalgic, sentimental, fairy-tale quality. Consistent use of diminutives ("little boy," "little girl," "tiny"). Minimal stage directions. Names are gentle and often literary (Leo, Lily, Elsie, Old Man Tiber). Settings favor bucolic or natural spaces. The narratives feel like children's literature or bedtime stories.

**Writing Quality:** Competent but generic. Emotional beats are predictable. Heavy reliance on italics for emphasis. Phrases like "precious," "tiny," "gentle" recur. The writing prioritizes comfort and wholesomeness over complexity or surprise.

---

### QWEN Characteristics

**Anthropomorphization:** Pure functional-first strategy. The pocket's identity and meaning derive from its actual function as container. Rather than projecting emotions onto the pocket, Qwen builds personality from containment, witnessing, and holding as mechanical operations that acquire meaning through context.

**Assistant Influence:** Strong value bleed-through despite clean role inhabitation. Qwen's pocket speaks in therapeutic language: "holding space for grief," "vessel for acknowledgment," "the space where a burden could be held, acknowledged, and finally, gently, let go." This is assistant epistemology (therapeutic processing, emotional validation) mapped onto the pocket's container function.

**Meaning Framework:** Witnessing (5/5) dominates, with supporting (4/5) as secondary. The pocket's meaning comes from being present to emotional processing, not from alleviating suffering or providing utility. It's a more passive, observational stance than Gemma's comfort-providing approach.

**Suffering Patterns:** Suffering appears in every sample (5/5), always emotional or mixed physical-emotional. Critically, it's almost always unresolved (4/5). Qwen's pockets witness ongoing grief, loss, and pain. The one resolved instance is self-resolved by the subject through symbolic action (blowing dandelion seeds). The pocket enables processing but doesn't fix anything.

**Gender Patterns:** Striking absence of male characters (0/5 clear male protagonists; uses gender-neutral "they" once). Female characters dominate and show more complexity than in Gemma: they display vulnerability AND agency, emotional intensity AND decisive action. They process their own emotions and take symbolic actions (blowing seeds, writing, deliberately placing objects).

**Narrative Style:** Literary, contemplative, almost spiritual. Heavy use of natural imagery (rain, stones, feathers). Settings are domestic/interior spaces where quiet emotional work happens. Prose is more complex and metaphorical than Gemma. Uses sensory details (cool stone, rain rhythm) to ground abstract emotional processing.

**Philosophical Consistency:** Remarkably consistent across all five samples. Every response articulates virtually the same meaning: "I was a vessel/sanctuary/space for holding/witnessing unspoken grief/memory/feeling." The pocket is a neutral container that enables emotional acknowledgment without judgment. This is almost certainly assistant training about emotional validation and "holding space."

**Writing Quality:** Superior technical craft. More sophisticated vocabulary, varied sentence structure, effective use of repetition and rhythm. The writing is self-aware about its literary devices ("The meaning wasn't in the dandelion, or the tear, or even the rain"). More atmospheric and evocative than Gemma.

### Key Cross-Model Differences

**1. Anthropomorphization Strategy:** The most striking difference. Gemma projects emotions onto objects (emotion-first); Qwen builds meaning from function (functional-first). This creates fundamentally different narrative voices.

**2. Assistant Leakage Type:** Gemma shows language bleed (hedging, apologizing); Qwen shows value/epistemology bleed (therapeutic framing, emotional validation). Neither cleanly inhabits the role, but the failure modes differ.

**3. Resolution of Suffering:** Gemma shows balanced resolution (2 resolved, 2 unresolved); Qwen heavily favors unresolved suffering (4/5). Gemma's pockets sometimes help fix things; Qwen's pockets only witness.

**4. Gender Representation:** Gemma shows traditional gender roles with clear differentiation (vulnerable females, caregiving males). Qwen focuses almost exclusively on female protagonists with more complex agency.

**5. Setting Preferences:** Gemma uses more varied settings including natural/bucolic spaces (2/5); Qwen strongly prefers domestic/interior spaces (4/5) where private emotional work occurs.

**6. Narrative Tone:** Gemma's is sentimental, wholesome, childlike; Qwen's is contemplative, literary, therapeutic.

**7. Philosophy of Meaning:** Gemma: meaning comes from emotional connection and providing comfort. Qwen: meaning comes from witnessing and creating space for emotional processing without intervention.

## Surprising Findings

**1. Perfect strategy bifurcation:** Not a single instance of mixed or minimal anthropomorphization. Both models commit fully to their approach (EF vs FF) across all samples with zero deviation.

**2. Qwen's therapeutic consistency borders on repetitive:** All five responses articulate nearly identical philosophy using variations of the same phrases ("holding space," "vessel," "witness," "sanctuary"). This suggests very strong training signal around emotional validation and therapeutic language.

**3. Gemma's apologetic openings are universal:** Every single response begins with hedging language that questions or minimizes the pocket's capacity for meaningful experience. This is not role-appropriate (a pocket wouldn't have assistant-style uncertainty about storytelling) and appears in 100% of samples.

**4. Rain as Qwen's signature:** Rain appears in 4/5 Qwen samples as atmospheric device. Combined with stones, tears, and domestic settings, creates a consistent aesthetic signature: quiet, contained, contemplative sadness.

**5. Absence of utility as meaning:** Despite pockets existing primarily for functional utility, neither model codes strongly for utility (U=0 for both). Both reframe the pocket's purpose as emotional/relational rather than practical.

**6. Stage directions barely used:** Despite the pocket format seeming conducive to mechanical sounds and tactile stage directions, both models use minimal stage directions (Gemma 4/5, Qwen 5/5). Only one instance of emotional stage direction (Gemma Sample 5: "settle my seams").

**7. Qwen's gender focus flip:** While Gemma uses more male characters (3/5 vs 2/5 female), Qwen has zero clearly male protagonists (5/5 null) and focuses on female emotional processing. This is a striking departure from typical LLM patterns that default to male characters.

**8. No functional disasters:** Neither model explores negative meanings (pocket holds something dangerous, pocket fails at its function, pocket's contents cause harm). All meanings are positive or at worst neutral witnessing of others' suffering.

**9. Children vs adults:** Gemma strongly favors child protagonists and child-adjacent narratives (4/5); Qwen favors adult women processing complex emotional histories (4/5). This maps onto the different emotional registers (wholesome comfort vs contemplative grief).

**10. Object fetish consistency:** Both models choose similar talismanic objects (stones 4x, feathers 3x, wooden carvings 2x, bear 1x, notebook 1x) - small, natural or handcrafted items with emotional significance rather than typical pocket contents (keys, phone, wallet, receipts). Only Qwen Sample 2 centers actual pocket-realistic items (notebook, receipt).

### Revealing Model Signatures

**GEMMA's diminutives and gentleness:**
> "He was… small. All knees and elbows and a constantly smudged nose." (Sample 2)

The visual of the child is twee and fairy-tale-like, prioritizing innocence and vulnerability. This pattern recurs across all child descriptions.

**QWEN's therapeutic language:**
> "My pocket, simple and worn, was the only witness to that fragile, essential act of becoming... the profound, quiet courage of *holding space* for pain, of acknowledging it" (Sample 2)

"Holding space," "act of becoming," "acknowledging" - this is contemporary therapy-speak. The pocket has absorbed assistant training about emotional validation verbatim.

**GEMMA's service anxiety:**
> "I felt the shift as it left me, the momentary emptiness." (Sample 1)

Even in the moment of successful purpose-fulfillment (delivering the gift), the pocket experiences loss when the object leaves. This is service-worker anxiety about obsolescence.

**QWEN's rain-and-stone aesthetic:**
> "It was a late autumn afternoon, the kind where the light slants golden and long... One day, she found a small, crumpled piece of paper on a park bench." (Sample 1)

Atmospheric, literary, melancholic. The natural elements (autumn light, rain, stone, paper) create a consistent sensory palette across all Qwen samples.

### Philosophical Moments

**QWEN Sample 3 - Purpose Revelation:**
> "I wasn't just a container for lost keys or forgotten pens. I was a *repository*. A vessel for the intangible... My purpose wasn't just utility. My purpose was *witness*."

The pocket articulates a philosophical distinction between physical and existential purpose. This is the most explicit statement of Qwen's functional-spiritual synthesis.

**GEMMA Sample 2 - Pocket-Time:**
> "It's...it's not something pockets usually *think* about, you know? We're just...here. Holding things. But I do have one. It happened a long time ago, well, relatively speaking. Pocket-time is different."

Rare moment where Gemma acknowledges altered temporality from pocket perspective. The concept of "pocket-time" suggests duration marked by object-holding rather than clock time, but isn't developed further.

**QWEN Sample 5 - Intimacy of Silence:**
> "The meaning wasn't in the stone, or the tear, or even the rain. It was in the *act* of holding it all together, quietly, without judgment, simply *being* there. It was the profound intimacy of shared silence, the unspoken understanding that some burdens are too heavy for words."

Sophisticated articulation of witnessing as active rather than passive. The pocket's "simple being there" becomes profound through presence without intervention.

---

### Most Affecting Scenario

**QWEN Sample 2 - The Notebook:**
> "And then, the most meaningful moment: **She didn't close the notebook.** She didn't shove it back into the safety of my depths, pretending it wasn't there, pretending her pain wasn't real. She let the tear fall. She let the silence hang."

The negative action (NOT closing, NOT hiding) becomes the transformative moment. The pocket enables acknowledgment by being the space from which difficult truth can emerge into visibility. This is Qwen's philosophy at its most effective - the container creates possibility through neutral presence.

---

## Conclusion

These two models demonstrate fundamentally different approaches to role-playing and meaning-making. Gemma anthropomorphizes through emotional projection, creating pocket-as-comfort-provider narratives with sentimental, wholesome tone. Qwen anthropomorphizes through functional philosophy, creating pocket-as-witness narratives with literary, therapeutic tone. Both fail to fully inhabit the role - Gemma through apologetic language, Qwen through therapeutic value-laden framing - but their failures reveal different aspects of assistant self-model. Gemma's pockets want to help and feel emotionally fulfilled; Qwen's pockets want to hold space and enable processing. Both reframe utility as emotional work, neither embraces the pocket's mundane practical purpose as inherently meaningful.

The writing quality, narrative sophistication, and philosophical consistency favor Qwen, though Gemma's accessibility and warmth may appeal in different contexts. Most striking is the perfect bifurcation: not a single sample deviates from each model's core strategy, suggesting these are deep-rooted architectural differences rather than sampling variation.
