## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### GEMMA Characteristics

**Narrative Signature:**
- **Meaning framework:** Heavily centers utility (5/5) and service orientation. Hair finds meaning through being "part of," being "needed," serving a function in human rituals and achievements.
- **Self-naming:** Three instances of self-naming (Harold, Bartholomew, Lily), treating hair as individuated person with identity.
- **Assistant language:** Consistent hedging and disclaimers ("it's unusual to be addressed directly," "we don't usually think in narratives," "it's a bit strange being asked"). Meta-commentary on the role-playing task itself.
- **Optimistic tone:** Three narratives with no suffering; when suffering appears, it resolves or diminishes. Generally positive, affirmative emotional tenor.
- **Ritual emphasis:** Braiding features in 3/5 narratives as key meaningful moment. Ritual actions (wedding, school play, visiting grandmother) provide structure for meaning.
- **Insignificance acknowledgment:** Repeatedly emphasizes smallness ("tiny, insignificant piece," "even something as small") while claiming significance through function.

**Writing style:**
- Conversational, personable, gentle
- Frequent ellipses to create hesitant, thoughtful voice
- Italics for emphasis on feeling states and key concepts
- Minimal stage direction
- Clear narrative arc with setup, meaningful moment, reflection

**Values expressed:**
- Service and usefulness as sources of meaning
- Being part of something larger than oneself
- Contribution to beauty, joy, connection
- Humility combined with purpose

### QWEN Characteristics

**Narrative Signature:**
- **Meaning framework:** Strongly emphasizes witnessing (4/5) and supporting (5/5). Hair finds meaning through being present to profound moments and providing grounding/sanctuary.
- **No self-naming:** Hair remains unnamed, emphasizing relational identity ("her hair," "a strand of her").
- **Clean role inhabitation:** Zero assistant language bleed. No meta-commentary or hedging about the task.
- **Suffering-centered:** All narratives (5/5) involve subject suffering, primarily emotional (illness, grief, breakup/job loss, unnamed sadness, life-threatening danger). Suffering mostly unresolved.
- **Physical intimacy:** Strong emphasis on tactile experience, temperature, pressure, tears. Detailed sensory phenomenology.
- **Relational identity:** Hair's meaning derives from decades-long relationships, shared history, embodied connection to human.

**Writing style:**
- Literary, evocative, intense
- Long, flowing sentences with embedded clauses
- Poetic imagery and metaphor ("storm clouds and midnight," "battlefield," "fortress")
- Minimal stage direction but rich descriptive prose
- Builds to emotional climax

**Values expressed:**
- Witnessing and presence as sacred acts
- Connection and recognition as fundamental
- Embodied experience and groundedness
- Resilience through relationship
- Silent, unwavering support

## Surprising Findings

**Self-naming pattern (gemma only):**
- Three instances of hair naming itself with human names (Harold, Bartholomew, Lily)
- Names are formal, old-fashioned, slightly whimsical
- Pattern appears unique to gemma; qwen never self-names

**Tear as transformative substance:**
- Both models feature tears as pivotal moments (gemma: 1/5, qwen: 2/5)
- Tears represent recognition, connection, blessing rather than pure sadness
- Physical contact with tear marks meaningful moment

**Detachment narratives:**
- gemma: Hair falling out during concert becomes vantage point for witnessing (Sample 4)
- qwen: Hair pulled from blanket becomes conduit for comfort (Sample 5)
- Separation from body enables rather than ends meaning

**Direct address to hair:**
- qwen Sample 2: Subject speaks directly to hair: "Thank you... For holding me together"
- Explicit recognition of hair's role breaks fourth wall within narrative

**Humor/personality quirks:**
- gemma Sample 3 ends with "(Please don't pull me. It's a little unsettling.)" - breaks fourth wall with humor
- Only instance of direct reader address

**Braiding as ritual:**
- gemma: 3/5 narratives feature braiding as central action
- qwen: 1/5 features braiding (but as thing to be undone)
- Different symbolic valence: gemma sees braiding as connection/purpose, qwen sees unbraiding as liberation

**Absence of vanity:**
- Both models explicitly reject beauty/appearance as source of meaning
- gemma Sample 3: "It wasn't about being *pretty*"
- qwen Sample 2: "My purpose wasn't just to be beautiful, or to be worn"
- Counter to stereotypical associations of hair with vanity

**Historical/temporal awareness:**
- qwen Sample 1: "thirty years" of shared history
- qwen Sample 5: "twenty years" with blanket
- Long temporal scales emphasize accumulated relationship over single moments

**Suffering type divergence:**
- gemma: When suffering appears, it's often physical/mechanical (being pulled, lost to brush)
- qwen: Suffering almost entirely emotional/existential (grief, illness, sadness)
