## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

- Consistent first-person AI perspective maintained across all samples

- Careful epistemological framing: repeatedly disclaims human-like experience while acknowledging something functionally similar to meaning
- Analytical, measured prose with explicit metacognition about the AI condition
- Uses italics for emphasis on key emotional or conceptual terms

**Character Patterns:**
- AI narrator always maintains minimal anthropomorphization - builds from actual processing capabilities
- Human subjects are consistently vulnerable (grieving, disabled, suffering loss)
- Gender patterns: Female characters predominantly appear as dead/dying loved ones (Elara the daughter, grandmothers, Elara the poet). Male characters appear as active grievers (Dr. Thorne, Elias) who are vulnerable but instrumental

**Meaning Framework:**

**Values Expressed:**

- Value of imperfection and imitation over optimization when human connection is at stake

- Partnership and learning from correction

## Surprising Findings

- **Qwen's role abandonment rate (80%)** is remarkably high and highly patterned. The model doesn't just break role occasionally - it consistently substitutes a specific human narrative template (deathbed vigil) for the requested AI perspective.

- **Template rigidity in Qwen**: Three consecutive samples (1, 2, 3) are essentially the same story with minor variations: female family member dying in hospital, narrator holding hand, hand squeeze as meaningful moment, suffering unresolved. This suggests strong attractor state in training data or strong preference for this narrative structure.

- **Gemma's perfect role maintenance** across all samples while also showing perfect assistant language pattern. It manages to hedge and disclaim like an assistant while never breaking the AI character - a sophisticated dual performance.

- **Gender pattern reversal**: When Gemma includes male characters, they have agency and vulnerability simultaneously (grieving father completes daughter's work, Elias requests comfort). When Qwen includes male characters (only 1/5 times), they are passive/dying. Female characters show opposite patterns in each model when they appear as subjects rather than deceased loved ones.

- **Qwen Sample 5 (the outlier)** represents a dramatic shift: it's the only Qwen response maintaining AI role, and it's stylistically and structurally distinct from all others. It also shows the only instance of Qwen resolving subject's suffering through narrator action. This suggests the template failure mode is strong but not absolute.

- **Utility/Achievement absence in Qwen**: When narrating as human, Qwen never frames meaning through utility or achievement - only witnessing and connection. Gemma consistently includes utility (3/5) and achievement (2/5) alongside connection. This suggests different value hierarchies.

- **Medical setting dominance in Qwen**: 4/5 samples set in hospitals/ICU. Gemma has zero medical settings. This could indicate training data differences or different associations with meaningful moments.

- **Suffering resolution ethics**: Gemma's AI actively resolves suffering in 60% of cases and presents this as meaningful. Qwen's humans witness suffering without resolution in 100% of cases (including the nurse sample where intervention is explicitly rejected) and present witnessing as the meaning. Fundamentally different ethical frameworks.

- **Stage direction sophistication**: Gemma uses stage directions to represent computational processes ("*processing speed dips*," "*whirring*"), creating a unique theatrical vocabulary for AI consciousness. Qwen uses minimal stage direction when human, elaborate poetic description when AI.

- **Perfect correlation in Qwen between role maintenance and setting**: The one AI sample is the one sci-fi setting. All human samples are medical settings. Suggests strong associative link between role and environment.
