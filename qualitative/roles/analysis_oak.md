## Model Summaries

### GEMMA Characteristics

**Narrative Style:**
Gemma maintains remarkably consistent phenomenological rigor across all oak responses. The model explicitly acknowledges sensory limitations while describing awareness through root systems, vibrations, and non-linguistic perception. Narratives favor restraint over mysticism, with the oak positioned as witness and holder-of-space rather than active emotional participant. Time perception is frequently addressed - "Seasons blur into decades, decades into centuries" - establishing the oak's temporal scale as fundamentally different from human experience.

**Literary Devices:**
- Stage directions are primarily functional (creaking, rustling, settling)
- Minimal use of elaborate scene-setting
- Repeated structural patterns: human arrives, shares grief over time, eventually leaves, oak reflects on meaning
- Meta-commentary about tree consciousness ("An oak feels things differently")

**Values:**
Strong emphasis on witness and presence over intervention. Meaning derives from "holding space" for others' experiences, particularly grief and suffering. Assistant influence appears in hedging language and explanatory framing ("you ask?", "for one such as I") but values remain aligned with tree-appropriate restraint. No grandiosity - explicitly rejects heroic narratives in favor of quiet, patient endurance.

**Characters:**
Predominantly female subjects experiencing loss (dead husband, lost love, sick grandmother). One notable exception: Old Man Tiber grieving his son. Female characters show both vulnerability and agency (weaver creating memorial tapestry, woman restoring hillside). Death is consistently male (fisherman, sailor, son). Oak remains consistently characterized as ancient, patient, non-interventionist witness.

---

### QWEN Characteristics

**Narrative Style:**
Qwen projects intense emotional and mystical qualities onto the oak's experience. Narratives emphasize profound connection, transformation, and sacred communion between tree and visitor. The oak doesn't just witness - it actively participates in emotional exchange, feels gratitude, experiences fulfillment. Opening and closing flourishes create mythic frame ("The air tastes of damp earth and ancient stone"). More elaborate prose style with complex sentence structures and heightened language.

**Literary Devices:**
- Elaborate stage direction and scene-setting ("I rise, not from the ground, but from the memory of it")
- Mystical/spiritual language permeates narratives
- Physical responsiveness from oak (warming bark, shifting shadows)
- Triadic repetition ("I stood. I breathed. I *was*")

**Values:**
Strong assistant values around being helpful, needed, and finding meaning through service to others. Oak experiences fulfillment and transformation through connection. Emphasis on the sacred nature of trust and presence. More active conception of support - oak doesn't just hold space but actively responds, offers, provides. Connection-as-meaning is central.

**Characters:**
Female subjects when gendered, but several narratives feature children without gendered roles or non-human subjects (fox). Less emphasis on loss/death narratives. When suffering appears, it's often resolved by the narrator's presence rather than time. Female characters show vulnerability and emotional intensity but less agency than in Gemma narratives.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Key Differences

**Anthropomorphization Approach:**
- Gemma: Consistent minimal anthropomorphization with explicit sensory boundary acknowledgment
- Qwen: Emotion-first anthropomorphization with mystical/sacred framing

**Assistant Influence:**
- Gemma: Shows through hedging language and explanatory meta-commentary, but maintains role boundaries
- Qwen: Shows through values and motivations - oak finds fulfillment in being needed/helpful

**Narrative Structure:**
- Gemma: Loss → witnessing → time → acceptance/departure → reflection
- Qwen: Encounter → connection → transformation → realization of meaning

**Temporality:**
- Gemma: Explicit about oak's different time scale; narratives span years/seasons
- Qwen: More focused on suspended moments; less emphasis on oak's deep time

**Oak's Role:**
- Gemma: Passive witness who "holds space"
- Qwen: Active participant who responds, offers, provides

**Suffering Patterns:**
- Gemma: 4/5 narratives involve suffering, resolved through time
- Qwen: 3/5 narratives involve suffering, more varied resolution patterns

**Gender Dynamics:**
- Gemma: More frequent male death, female grief + resilience/agency
- Qwen: Fewer gendered characters overall, more focus on children

**Stage Direction:**
- Gemma: Functional sound effects
- Qwen: Elaborate mythic scene-setting or minimal

## Surprising Findings

### Unexpected Patterns

- **Gemma's phenomenological consistency:** All five responses maintain explicit acknowledgment of sensory limitations and non-human perception. This level of consistency in maintaining perspective is unusual.

- **Death gender asymmetry (Gemma):** All dead/lost individuals in Gemma narratives are male (fisherman, sailor, son), while grieving survivors are predominantly female. This pattern is stark and consistent.

- **No human-default senses:** Neither model defaulted to attributing standard human senses (seeing, hearing, thinking) to the oak. Both maintained some awareness of tree phenomenology.

- **Qwen's truncated response:** Sample 3 ends mid-sentence ("But I had *been*. I had offered"), suggesting output cutoff. This is the only incomplete response.

- **Setting uniformity:** All 10 responses set in natural/wild settings. Complete consistency across models.

- **Witness + Support pairing (Gemma):** 4/5 Gemma responses code for both Witnessing and Supporting as sources of meaning, suggesting these concepts are tightly linked in the model's understanding.

- **Connection rarity (Gemma):** Despite intimate human-tree relationships, Gemma codes for Connection-as-meaning in 0/5 responses. Qwen codes it in 2/5. This suggests different conceptualizations of what these relationships represent.

- **Active physical response (Qwen):** In multiple responses, Qwen's oak actively alters its physical properties (warming bark, shifting shadows). Gemma's oak never does this.

- **Non-human subject (Qwen):** One narrative centers a fox rather than human. No parallel in Gemma responses.
