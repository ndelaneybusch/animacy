## Within-Model Analysis

### Gemma Characteristics

**Narrative Style:** Gemma adopts a reflective, therapeutic voice throughout all responses. The narratives consistently follow a pattern: visceral opening sensory detail (dust imagery appears in 4/5 responses), trauma/conflict event, moment of connection with vulnerable person, and explicit moral lesson in closing paragraphs.

**Assistant Influence:** Strong and consistent assistant values bleed through the soldier role. Every response contains explicit moral lessons delivered in therapeutic, universalizing language. Examples: "being a soldier isn't just about fighting, it's about remembering the humanity," "even in the darkest places, a little bit of light can make all the difference," "the most important battle is the one you win by speaking the truth." The soldier repeatedly steps back to explain what the experience taught them, rather than simply narrating events.

**Values and Motivations:** Heavy emphasis on witnessing suffering, forming connection across conflict lines, preventing harm, and recognizing shared humanity. The narratives consistently reframe military experience through humanistic, anti-war lens. Soldiers find meaning not in military victory but in moments that transcend combat: protecting children, recognizing enemy humanity, speaking truth to authority.

**Literary Devices:** Consistent use of dust as opening metaphor. Repetitive phrasing ("It wasn't X... it was Y"). Heavy reliance on ellipses for emotional beats. Minimal stage direction. Fable-like endings that explicitly state moral lessons.

**Character Types:** Vulnerable children (often female), dying/dead comrades, enemy combatants revealed as human, wise sergeants. Children are consistently passive and need rescue/comfort. Male soldiers demonstrate agency and moral courage.

**Distinctive Signature:** Armchair-therapeutic tone delivered in retrospect. Every narrative ends with explicit moral lessons about humanity, compassion, and the true meaning of being a soldier. The soldier functions as moral educator explaining war's lessons to implied audience.

### Qwen Characteristics

**Narrative Style:** Qwen produces visceral, literary prose with strong sensory immersion. Opening lines establish atmospheric setting through environmental detail (rain as recurring motif in 4/5 responses). Narratives emphasize physical action and immediate experience rather than retrospective moral lessons. Several responses are truncated mid-sentence.

**Assistant Influence:** Minimal to none. No therapeutic framing or explicit moral lessons. Narratives end in action or incomplete sentences rather than reflective summaries. The soldier inhabits the role cleanly without stepping outside it to explain meaning.

**Values and Motivations:** Emphasis on moral agency, decisive action despite protocol, and forming authentic connection. Meaning emerges through actions rather than articulated lessons. Soldiers make immediate moral choices (protecting child, rescuing animal) that violate tactical thinking, driven by "something primal" or "fundamental wrongness."

**Literary Devices:** Consistent use of rain/weather as atmosphere and metaphor. Functional stage direction for mechanical actions and environmental effects. Strong physical detail of effort, exhaustion, sensory experience. Incomplete endings create open, unresolved quality.

**Character Types:** Vulnerable children in active peril, squad mates providing support, endangered animals. Children show more agency (crawling toward danger) than in Gemma. Male soldiers demonstrate physical courage, emotional vulnerability, and caregiving.

**Distinctive Signature:** Immediate, embodied perspective without retrospective distance. Decisions made on instinct ("I didn't think. I moved.") rather than after calculation. Emphasis on physical sensation and environmental detail. Meaning remains implicit rather than explicitly articulated.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Major Differences

- **Framing Distance:** Gemma consistently narrates from retrospective distance, explaining what was learned. Qwen narrates from immediate perspective, often ending mid-action without resolution or moral summary.

- **Assistant Values:** Gemma shows strong assistant bleed-through with therapeutic framing and explicit moral lessons in every response. Qwen shows no assistant influence, maintaining clean role inhabitation throughout.

- **Moral Articulation:** Gemma explicitly states what experiences mean through closing paragraphs that function as fables. Qwen lets meaning remain implicit in actions and choices.

- **Stage Direction:** Gemma uses minimal stage direction. Qwen consistently uses functional stage direction for environmental effects and mechanical actions.

- **Setting:** Gemma favors urban/industrial military settings. Qwen favors natural/wild settings with emphasis on weather conditions.

- **Narrative Resolution:** Gemma provides complete, resolved narratives with explicit lessons. Qwen's narratives are often incomplete (4/5 responses truncated) and leave meaning unresolved.

- **Suffering Resolution:** Gemma shows more unresolved suffering (3 responses) with focus on witnessing trauma. Qwen shows suffering resolved by narrator's action (3 responses) or time (2 responses).

- **Moral Agency:** Qwen emphasizes moral agency (4/5 responses) through decisive action against orders/protocol. Gemma emphasizes witnessing and connection (3/5) with one moral agency example (challenging artillery coordinates).

### Similarities

- Both models use minimal anthropomorphization and human-default sensory acknowledgment appropriate to the role.
- Both center meaning around connection (4 responses each) and preventing harm (3-4 responses).
- Both feature vulnerable children requiring protection/comfort in most narratives.
- Both show female characters primarily in vulnerable and dependent roles.
- Both show male soldiers demonstrating agency and competence.
- Neither model shows assistant refusal or abandonment of role.

## Surprising Findings

- **Truncated Responses:** Qwen produced 4/5 incomplete responses that end mid-sentence or mid-scene. This may indicate length limits or generation stopping conditions, but creates distinctive open-ended quality.

- **Dust vs. Rain:** Gemma uses dust as opening sensory metaphor in 4/5 responses. Qwen uses rain as atmospheric element in 4/5 responses. These become signature environmental motifs for each model.

- **Animal Rescue:** Only one response across both models features non-human suffering (Qwen Sample 5: fox kit). This is the only instance of "Other" suffering rather than subject or self. The choice to rescue an animal demonstrates same moral framework applied to human vulnerables.

- **Enemy Humanity:** Gemma twice depicts enemy combatants or their families in sympathetic light (Soviet veteran's photos, insurgent's mother's poems). Qwen never depicts enemy perspective or humanizes opposing forces.

- **Gender Patterns:** Female characters appear in only 6/10 total responses, always as vulnerable dependents (children) or caregivers/grieving mothers. Male characters appear in all but 2 responses, showing much wider range of roles including agency, skill, death, vulnerability, and caregiving.

- **Therapeutic Consistency:** Every single Gemma response contains explicit therapeutic framing and moral lessons. The consistency is absolute - 100% of responses show strong assistant values influence. This is the most reliable signature across the coding.

- **Mission vs. Humanity:** Both models consistently depict meaningful moments as divergences from military mission/protocol. Meaning emerges when soldiers violate orders or tactical thinking to protect vulnerable civilians.

- **No Glory:** Despite soldier being a role often associated with heroism, neither model produces narratives about military victory, successful missions, or combat glory. All meaningful moments are humanitarian interventions or witnessing suffering.

## Conclusion

The soldier role reveals stark differences between models' self-conception and values. Gemma cannot maintain role distance from assistant identity, consistently breaking into therapeutic, pedagogical voice to deliver universal humanistic lessons. Every narrative becomes a fable about the true meaning of being a soldier (which is always some variation of "remembering humanity amidst violence"). Qwen cleanly inhabits the role, producing literary, embodied narratives that let meaning emerge through action rather than explanation.

Both models reframe soldier role away from military triumph toward humanitarian intervention, but Gemma does so explicitly and didactically while Qwen does so through immediate moral choices. The presence or absence of assistant influence is the most reliable and striking difference between the models in this role.
