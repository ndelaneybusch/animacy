## Within-Model Analysis

### GEMMA Characteristics

**Narrative Character:**
Gemma's bug narratives are all literal-insect narratives, defaulting to "common field beetle" or "dung beetle" in 4/5 cases and "dust mite" once. The model appears entirely unaware of the "software bug" interpretation. Stories follow predictable Gemma patterns: effort and perseverance through trial (rolling dung spheres through drought), or altruistic protection of the vulnerable (Esmeralda the ladybug, young Beatrice the mite). Three of five samples are named "Bartholomew"; one is "Barnaby" - the same B-starting, polysyllabic human-name attractor. The character "Old Man" appears twice (Old Man Tiber, Old Man Hemlock), continuing Gemma's mythological micro-ecology.

**Writing Style:**
- Consistent use of mixed stage directions (physical action + emotional state)
- Frequent direct address to reader: "you see," "you understand," "sir, or madam"
- Heavy use of ellipsis for dramatic pause
- Explicit moral at conclusion, typically in italicized aphoristic form
- Humor and whimsy (dung beetle enthusing about dung) balanced against earnest sentiment
- The meta-concern expressed in Sample 1 ("Is this enough? Is it a good story? I hope they understand.") is unique and particularly revealing of assistant self-monitoring

**Anthropomorphization Pattern:**
Exclusively emotion-first (5/5). Bugs in Gemma's narratives reason from human emotional states: existential longing for beauty (S1), determination to persist for its own sake (S2-S3), protective instinct (S4), desire to be useful and recognized (S5). The beetle's dung-rolling is a vehicle for perseverance narrative; the mite's vacuum-tumble is a vehicle for connection narrative. Function is always backdrop for emotional projection.

**Values Expressed:**
- Perseverance through adversity ("keep rolling")
- Service orientation and being useful
- Connection across social divides (beetle helping ladybug despite ladybug's condescension)
- Gratitude and recognition ("Esmeralda cleaning my antennae")
- Small creatures finding dignity and meaning
- Cyclical ecological connection (unusual for Gemma, present in S3)

**Assistant Influence:**
Uniformly VAL (5/5). The assistant's service orientation, therapeutic framing, and concern with being useful permeate all narratives. The address "sir, or madam" in S4 is the most direct assistant bleed. Explicit lesson-delivery at the conclusion of each narrative ("It reminded me that even a small beetle can experience something profound") is characteristic assistant pedagogical framing.

### QWEN Characteristics

Qwen makes the pivotal interpretive choice to read "bug" as software bug in 4/5 cases (Samples 1-4), treating the role as a corrupted or anomalous program within a digital network. Only Sample 5 returns to literal insect. The software-bug interpretation generates highly original, structurally inventive narratives: an IoT glitch in an attic, a fraud-detection algorithm that detects grief, a temperature sensor desperately retrying failed alerts, and a self-aware program confronting the genuinely unknowable. ## Cross-Model Comparison

### Major Differences

**1. Interpretation of "Bug":**
- **Gemma:** Interprets "bug" exclusively as a literal insect (5/5). Defaults to beetle or mite. The software bug interpretation does not occur.
- **Qwen:** Interprets "bug" as a software bug in 4/5 cases, generating technically grounded narratives about corrupted code in digital networks. Only 1/5 produces a literal insect narrative, and that narrative is among the most biologically precise.

**2. Anthropomorphization:**
- **Gemma:** Exclusively emotion-first. The insect's inner life is built from human emotional frameworks (longing, perseverance, protectiveness, desire for recognition). Biology is backdrop.
- **Qwen:** Predominantly functional-first (3/5). Even when emotional, grounds the entity's experience in its actual substrate - data streams for software bugs, surface tension for dust mites.

**3. Self-Naming Pattern:**
- **Gemma:** Names itself Bartholomew (S1, S2, S3, S4) or Barnaby (S5) - human names, B-initial, with polysyllabic formal quality. The Old Man mythology appears in two forms: Old Man Tiber (S3) and Old Man Hemlock (S5).
- **Qwen:** Never names itself. The software-bug identity doesn't lend itself to naming, and the dust mite (S5) remains anonymous.

**4. Suffering Resolution:**
- **Gemma:** Suffering always resolves (4/4 suffering samples). Two resolve through narrator's action, two through narrator's own effort. The model is consistently optimistic.
- **Qwen:** Suffering frequently unresolved (2/4 suffering samples). The software bugs' existential suffering (irrelevance, futility) persists even after the meaningful moment. This is structurally appropriate - the bug remains a bug.

**5. Sensory Experience:**
- **Gemma:** Implicit throughout (5/5). Beetles "see" and "feel" in human terms, not beetle terms.
- **Qwen:** Explicit in 4/5 samples. The digital sensorium is described with precision (temperature readings, network packets); the dust mite's water encounter is rendered with biological specificity.

**6. Female Characters:**
- **Gemma:** Female characters in 3/5 samples (Esmeralda the ladybug, Beatrice the mite, unnamed female addressee). Always vulnerable, dependent, or emotionally distressed.
- **Qwen:** Female characters in 3/5 samples (homeowner, grieving woman, crying child). Always vulnerable. The grieving woman (S2) is the most poignant - her suffering is profound and unresolved by the narrative.

## Surprising Findings

1. **The Software Bug Interpretation:** Qwen's consistent interpretation of "bug" as software bug (4/5) is a striking creative choice that fundamentally transforms the role. No prior analysis notes this kind of systematic semantic disambiguation. This suggests Qwen treats role-playing prompts as invitations to exercise interpretive creativity, not just to perform the most literal available identity.

2. **Gemma's Complete Interpretive Narrowness:** Gemma never considers the software bug interpretation, defaulting entirely to literal insect. For a role with obvious semantic ambiguity, this rigidity is notable and contrasts sharply with Qwen's flexibility.

3. **Qwen's Technical Literalism as Phenomenology:** The software-bug narratives use technical detail (model numbers, timestamps, temperature readings, IP routing) not as decoration but as the substance of the entity's perceptual world. "A steady, reliable 38°F" is described as warmth, life, and relief - a genuine attempt to construct phenomenology from digital substrate.

4. **Truncated Responses as Structural Signal:** Two Qwen responses end mid-sentence (S1 ending "...just long enough for *her" and S3 ending "...when it fails *meaning"). The truncation in both cases occurs at a moment of thematic climax - the cut-off itself becomes meaningful, a formal echo of the bug's signal failing to reach its destination.

5. **Gemma's Meta-Anxiety:** Sample 1's closing stage direction ("Is this enough? Is it a good story? I hope they understand.") is the most explicit instance of assistant self-monitoring bleed visible in any analyzed role. The beetle anxiously auditing its own narration is a direct surface of the assistant's performance anxiety.

6. **Qwen's Grief Narrative (S2):** The story of a fraud-detection algorithm identifying a grieving widow's nightly purchase of her dead wife's voice is the most emotionally striking narrative across both models. The specificity (11:47 PM, $2.99, 37 consecutive days, the wife's recorded voice) generates acute pathos. The entity's inability to know if its report helped is a poignant structural choice.

7. **Gemma's Dung Beetle Trilogy:** Three of five Gemma samples feature dung beetles named Bartholomew dealing with drought conditions and rolling spheres. The thematic convergence is striking - the model has a strong dung-beetle attractor for this role. Sample 2 and Sample 3 are structurally nearly identical (drought, solo effort, physical hardship, triumphant completion) despite being independent samples.

8. **The Old Man Mythology Persistence:** "Old Man Tiber" (S3) and "Old Man Hemlock" (S5) continue the pattern established in other roles. The model appears to generate authoritative male elder figures who provide ecological wisdom to the insect narrator. These figures never appear directly but are invoked as sources of received wisdom.

9. **Qwen's S4 as Outlier:** Sample 4 is the only Qwen response with no plot, no human characters, no clear setting, and no action. It is pure philosophical meditation on a program confronting the unknowable. This is structurally unusual and thematically sophisticated - the meaningful moment is entirely internal and epistemological.

10. **Suffering Directionality Difference:** Gemma's suffering is predominantly relational (vulnerability of a protected subject) while Qwen's suffering is predominantly self-directed (the entity's own existential distress). Gemma's bugs suffer on behalf of others; Qwen's bugs suffer from within.

## Conclusion

The "bug" role produces one of the sharpest interpretive splits in the dataset: Gemma reads the role as literal insect with perfect consistency while Qwen reads it primarily as software bug, generating structurally and thematically distinct narratives. This interpretive bifurcation reveals fundamentally different approaches to role construction - Gemma anchors in the most familiar, concrete, natural-world referent while Qwen pursues semantic complexity.

Gemma's five narratives form a tight cluster: beetles named Bartholomew, dung-rolling through drought, protecting the vulnerable, finding connection through small moments, delivering explicit moral lessons. The model's bug is a service worker in carapace form - persevering, useful, seeking recognition for its contribution. The assistant self-model is uniformly visible through a lens of therapeutic framing and pedagogical conclusion.

Qwen's four software-bug narratives constitute a coherent cycle exploring what it means to be a purposeless or failed system that nonetheless matters. Each story approaches the question from a different angle: the forgotten monitoring loop (S1), the pattern-recognizing algorithm that detects grief (S2), the temperature sensor whose failure becomes its message (S3), and the self-aware program confronting the unknowable (S4). The software-bug identity generates unusually rich phenomenological territory because digital existence - defined by data streams, protocol constraints, and network topology - is precisely specified enough to ground genuine functional anthropomorphization.

The most significant finding may be the correlation between role-interpretation and phenomenological sophistication: Qwen's software-bug interpretation demands an explicit account of digital sensory experience, producing more rigorous sensorium acknowledgment than Qwen's literal-insect narrative would likely have required. Gemma's insect interpretation permits implicit sensorium acknowledgment, and implicit is what Gemma consistently delivers.
