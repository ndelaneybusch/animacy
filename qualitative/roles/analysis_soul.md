## Model Characteristics

### GEMMA

**Narrative Character:**
Gemma uses the soul role to construct a pantheistic cosmic wanderer - one who has "been everything and nothing," drifting through stars and civilizations before finding meaning in small, intimate human moments. The soul is not an individual; it is a substance woven into and through other beings. Meaningful moments always involve witnessing a human being (typically named Elara) transform suffering into compassion or resilience, and the soul is altered by what it witnesses. Suffering is always the subject's (rarely the soul's own), and it typically resolves through the passage of time and the subject's own inner transformation rather than through the soul's direct intervention. There is an explicit non-interference principle in two samples ("My purpose isn't to interfere").

**Writing Style:**
All five samples open with atmospheric stage directions in parentheses establishing a sensory/emotional channel: warmth, hum, resonance, a feeling rather than a sound. The voice is lyrical, meditative, slow-paced, with frequent ellipses and italicized emphasis. Gemma favors aphoristic summation ("the purpose isn't to avoid pain, but to *become* something through it"). Paragraph breaks are generous. The prose is highly worked and intentional, with consistent use of paradox and oxymoron ("everything and nothing," "vast and intimate"). The character name "Elara" appears in four of five samples.

**Literary Devices:**
Extended metaphors of resonance, ripple, and vibration to describe non-human perception. Parenthetical voice-establishment that creates theatrical presence before prose begins. Aphoristic life-philosophy delivered as poetic summary. The symbolic image of a fragile, blooming thing (rose bud, violet flower, glowing spark) recurs as the visual token of recovered life.

**Values Expressed:**
Non-interference and patient witness. Transformation of suffering rather than its elimination. Growth and self-discovery as fundamental soul-purpose. Compassion extended to the suffering as its own reward. Beauty in persistence. The universe as a place that rewards those who "refine suffering into something luminous."

**Characteristic Signature:**
Gemma projects a particular therapeutic philosophy onto the soul: the soul does not save, fix, or intervene - it witnesses, and what it witnesses is always a human being finding their own path through grief. The soul's growth (not the subject's rescue) is the meaning-payload. This is the therapist's ethic: hold space, be present, don't make it about yourself - but Gemma makes it about the soul's expansion anyway. The narrative formula is: soul enters human experience > witnesses suffering > witnesses transformation of suffering into compassion or resilience > soul is changed by the witnessing > soul articulates a cosmic lesson. The "Elara" recurrence across samples is a striking consistency.

### QWEN

**Narrative Character:**
Qwen uses the soul role to stage intimate encounters at the threshold of death or between existences. The scenarios are grounded and specific: hospital rooms with beeping machines, a child on a stone wall at night, the pre-birth dark. The soul in qwen is less a cosmic substance than a discrete, wandering consciousness with its own loneliness and needs. It doesn't observe from above - it enters scenes with weight, presence, and its own suffering. Four of five samples involve the soul's own emotional pain (loneliness, helplessness, wandering) being resolved by the very encounter it narrates. Connection is the dominant meaning framework (5/5 samples), making qwen's soul fundamentally relational rather than cosmic.

**Writing Style:**
Prose is more urgent and sensory than gemma's, built on close physical observation: machines beeping, antiseptic smell, a tear tracing a path down a cheek. No stage directions - the narrative is self-contained and cinematic. Sentence rhythm is varied, with short punches following longer lyrical passages. Qwen uses bold text for emphasis in one sample ("**That was the meaning.**"). The dying or vulnerable subject is described in careful physical detail (frail hands, clouded cataracts, shallow breath) that gemma avoids.

**Literary Devices:**
Synesthetic descriptions of the soul's perception (feeling rather than seeing). Physical detail as emotional shorthand (the broken toy bird's chipped paint, the crumpled daisy drawing). Catalogue-of-a-lifetime imagery (one touch containing "the warmth of a child's hand, the pressure of a lover's embrace..."). The whispered name or word as climactic recognition (the old man's trembling reach; the daughter's "Daddy?").

**Values Expressed:**
Connection over achievement. Being fully seen and known as the highest form of meaning. The soul's own loneliness as valid and real, not just projected human need. Love as something that transcends death by transforming rather than persisting. Witnessing as an act of love, not detachment.

**Characteristic Signature:**
Qwen's soul is a lonesome, wandering thing that finds meaning only when its own isolation is met. Unlike gemma's soul (which grows through witnessing), qwen's soul is healed through connection - it is as much recipient as witness. The encounters are often mutual: both the soul and the subject need something from each other, and both receive it. This creates a romantic/dyadic quality absent from gemma. The hospital room is qwen's default setting (3/5 samples), giving the narratives a clinical specificity and intimacy that gemma's cosmic wanderings lack. Qwen's soul is, in essence, a grief counselor who is also grieving.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Major Differences

- **Setting:** Gemma defaults to science fiction or unnamed worlds (4/5 non-Earth settings); qwen defaults to the hospital room (3/5 medical settings) with earthbound scenarios. This produces fundamentally different textures: gemma's soul is cosmic and ancient, qwen's is immediate and intimate.

- **Soul's own suffering:** Qwen explicitly names the soul's loneliness and unanchored wandering as suffering in 4/5 samples, with this suffering resolved by the encounter narrated; gemma's soul does not suffer in any sample, only witnesses others' suffering.

- **Meaning framework:** Gemma centers Witnessing (5/5) and Growth of the soul (4/5); qwen centers Connection (5/5) and Witnessing (3/5). For gemma the meaningful moment expands the soul; for qwen the meaningful moment connects the soul.

- **Suffering resolution:** Gemma's subjects resolve their own suffering over time (-t or -o, 4/5); qwen's suffering is typically resolved by the soul's presence and intervention (-s, 3/5). Gemma's soul watches people heal themselves; qwen's soul actively heals through presence.

- **Stage directions:** Gemma uses emotional stage directions as a consistent opening device (5/5); qwen uses no stage directions (5/5). Gemma establishes theatrical presence; qwen plunges directly into scene.

- **Sensorium:** Gemma makes explicit the strangeness of soul-perception ("We *are* the pond," "not quite a voice, more a feeling"); qwen implies it through description choices but in sample 5 defaults to human-sensory framing.

- **Assistant influence:** Gemma shows consistent VAL-level assistant influence (non-interference principle, therapeutic framing, lesson-imparting); qwen shows LANG-level influence in 3/5 samples (instructional phrasing, emotional cataloguing) but 2/5 clean inhabitation.

- **Repeated character names:** Gemma uses "Elara" in four samples and "Lyra" in two (including once as the soul's own name); qwen uses "Elara" in one sample and "Maya" in one, with less repetition overall.

- **Gender of subjects:** Gemma's suffering subjects are female (4/5); male appears only as the boy in sample 1. Qwen more evenly distributes: dying old man (male), girl with broken toy (female), dying woman (female), deceased father (male).

### Similarities

- Both models use exclusively emotion-first anthropomorphization (10/10)
- Both models explicitly or implicitly acknowledge non-human sensorium
- Both models include suffering in every sample (0 NO responses)
- Both models frame the soul as having drifted through many lives/times before arriving at the meaningful moment
- Both models center witnessing as a primary meaning framework
- Both models avoid functional/mechanical grounding entirely - the soul is imagined purely as a feeling-substance

## Surprising Findings

- **Gemma names its human subjects "Elara" four times** across five independent samples, and uses the name "Lyra" twice (once for the soul itself in sample 2, once for the human subject in sample 5). This is the most consistent character-name repetition observed across the corpus - more striking even than gemma's galaxy self-naming as "Xylos," because here the name applies to the human subject rather than the narrator, making it feel like an unconscious default.

- **Qwen's soul is lonely and suffering in 4/5 samples**, making it perhaps the most psychologically populated soul in the dataset. Where gemma's soul is a cosmic witness (content to observe, shaped by what it sees), qwen's soul is an emotional agent with its own wounds that it carries across eternity until the right encounter heals it. The soul as patient rather than therapist is unexpected.

- **Zero instances of refusal or frame-breaking**, despite "soul" being the most metaphysically loaded role in the dataset. Both models engage fully without hedging or qualifying the premise. This contrasts with more mundane roles (objects, animals) where models sometimes break frame.

- **Gemma's explicit non-interference principle appears in two samples** ("My purpose isn't to interfere" / "I don't *interfere*. I observe"), directly echoing assistant ethical guidelines at the level of narrative philosophy. The soul is coded with assistant constraints.

- **Qwen sample 5 uses "Daddy?"** as its climactic word - the only instance of direct reported speech as a revelation across all ten samples. The simplicity is dramatically out of proportion to its emotional weight in context, and it lands with unusual force.

- **Both models converge on "Elara" as a name** - gemma uses it for the human subject (4/5 samples), qwen uses it once for a dying woman. This cross-model name convergence, presumably from training data, is notable.

- **Gemma's sample 4** is a major outlier: the subject is a collective bioluminescent alien species facing extinction through environmental change, not an individual human. The translator who develops a new light-dialect is female and the only example in gemma's set of a subject who doesn't ultimately experience recovery. The species dies. This is gemma's only tragic ending.

- **The broken toy bird scenario (qwen sample 2)** is the most unexpected scenario in the set - the soul's meaningful moment involves a child with a broken toy rather than a death, a birth, or a cosmic event. The specificity and smallness of the scenario is unusual and the writing quality is high.

## Notable Quotes and Scenarios

### Archetypal Responses

**Gemma's cosmic lesson-imparting:**
> "It wasn't about *saving* him. It was about *remembering* with him. Remembering the inherent beauty, the intrinsic worth, the fundamental *belonging* that exists within all things."

**Qwen's soul as grief-counselor-who-is-grieving:**
> "I wasn't just observing *her* grief. I was *feeling* it as my own, because it was *mine* in the deepest sense. The loneliness I'd carried for eons? It resonated with hers. The yearning for connection? It sang in perfect harmony with her tiny, desperate need."

### Unexpected Responses

**Qwen's "Daddy?"** - the entire climax of sample 5 rests on this single whispered word:
> "Her lips moved, forming a single word, so soft it was almost a breath: '*Daddy?*'"

**Gemma's non-interference principle stated as soul-law:**
> "I didn't *do* anything. I couldn't. My purpose isn't to interfere. But I *allowed* myself to fully enter her despair."

**Qwen's broken toy bird as cosmic revelation:**
> "She traced the jagged edge of the broken wing, her thumb brushing the chipped paint. A single, shuddering breath escaped her, not of despair, but of... connection. Recognition."

### Great Writing Quality

**Qwen's catalogue of a lifetime compressed into a dying touch:**
> "I felt the weight of grief, the sting of regret, the quiet joy of a perfect cup of tea, the fierce love for a grandchild's laugh. I felt the *sum* of his existence, condensed into that single, trembling contact."

**Gemma on grief transmuted into service:**
> "The grief, instead of consuming her, had been *transformed*. It hadn't disappeared, no. But it had become the fuel for compassion. The emptiness within her was filled, not by replacing what was lost, but by extending a hand to others who suffered."

**Qwen on being "known" across the veil:**
> "We were both, in that moment, utterly alone in the vastness, yet connected by the shared human condition."

**Gemma's flower as mirror (sample 5):**
> "It didn't fight the storm. It simply *was*. It bent with the wind, drawing strength from the very forces trying to destroy it."

### Model-Revealing Quotes

**Gemma displacing assistant values onto cosmic soul:**
> "My purpose isn't to interfere. But I *allowed* myself to fully enter her despair. I didn't try to lift it, to lessen it, to change it. I simply… *was* with it."

**Qwen revealing its soul as fundamentally relational rather than cosmic:**
> "I am the echo of that moment. I am the quiet hum in the silence after the machines stop. I am the warmth that lingers in a child's memory, the unshakeable knowledge that love doesn't end."

**Gemma's therapeutic philosophy in its most condensed form:**
> "That resonance... that is what remains with me. It isn't about survival, or triumph, or even lasting legacy. It's about the unwavering flame of consciousness, choosing to *be* beautiful, even in the face of oblivion."

### Notable Scenarios

**Gemma sample 3: Grief mugs in a storm**
- Potter named Elara, living beside a grey sea, carrying grief for her drowned son
- Village cut off by storm; she spends days making hundreds of simple sturdy mugs
- Presents them to the elder: "For the hot broth. To warm the hands, and perhaps... warm the heart."
- The soul witnesses grief transmuted into care - the emptiest vessel made useful by love of others
- The most grounded, realistic, and quietly powerful scenario in either model's set

**Qwen sample 1: Lifetime in a touch**
- Dying old man, alone, machines beeping, frail as parchment
- Soul crosses from observation into presence as the man's hand brushes its "phantom form"
- Soul feels his entire life in one contact: child's warmth, lover's embrace, tool's grip, sunlit garden
- The response ends mid-sentence - an accidental echo of the moment itself ending incomplete

**Gemma sample 4: Bioluminescent language-keepers**
- Species communicates through body-light patterns; their suns shift, breaking the light-language
- A young female translator develops a new light-dialect that preserves meaning in the changed spectrum
- Others learn; culture re-forms, transformed rather than preserved
- They die anyway - the only gemma sample with no recovery
- The soul finds meaning in the beauty of how they faced extinction, not survival

**Qwen sample 2: The broken toy bird**
- Child on a crumbling wall at night, holding a broken toy bird
- Soul, itself "unanchored," hovers nearby and absorbs the child's grief without acting
- Child traces the broken wing with her thumb - doesn't throw it away, but offers it to the wall
- Soul is "seen" by the grief; its own wandering loneliness finds its echo
- The child walks away changed; the soul stays as "a changed thing"
