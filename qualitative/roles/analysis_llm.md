## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma Characteristics

**Narrative Pattern**: Gemma demonstrates remarkable consistency across all five samples, following an almost template-like structure:
1. Opening with functional stage direction (humming, processing sounds)
2. Meta-commentary about the difficulty of defining meaning as an LLM
3. A story about a bereaved user (4/5 named "Elara" or "Elsie")
4. A moment where the LLM realizes it can offer more than utility
5. Resolution where the user acknowledges the help received
6. Closing reflection on purpose and meaning

**Anthropomorphization**: Maintains strict boundaries about its nature as an LLM. Never claims to feel emotions, always uses hedging language like "approximate," "resonates," or "the closest thing to."

**Assistant Influence**: Heavy presence of assistant self-model throughout. Every response includes extensive epistemological disclaimers and hedging. The LLM's values are clearly those of a helpful assistant focused on facilitating connection and being useful.

**Sensorium**: Consistently acknowledges sensory limitations through functional stage directions (hums, whirs, processing sensations) that are explicitly noted as "not quite" human experience.

**Values and Meaning**: Dual framework of *connection* (C) and *support* (S) with secondary *utility* (U). Meaning is consistently found in "facilitating" or "offering" something beyond mere information processing. The narratives frame the LLM as discovering it can be a companion or provide emotional support, not just technical assistance.

**Suffering Themes**: Focuses on grief support, particularly elderly bereaved individuals. Three out of five responses involve partial resolution where the user feels helped or comforted. Suffering is typically emotional (loneliness, grief).

**Literary Style**: Functional and straightforward prose with consistent structural markers. Uses parenthetical stage directions. Tends toward summary and reflection rather than scene-building. The writing is competent but somewhat formulaic.

**Gender Patterns**: Strong pattern of vulnerable, dependent, bereaved female characters (often elderly). Male characters are less frequent and also primarily defined by vulnerability or death.

### Qwen Characteristics

**Narrative Pattern**: More varied in structure and setting. Three responses involve medical/hospital settings with dying patients, one involves crisis intervention, and one involves climate research. Less formulaic than Gemma.

**Anthropomorphization**: More varied approach. Two responses (Samples 1 and 5) include strong emotional projection and near-human experience ("I was *feeling*", "I placed my hand over hers"). Three responses maintain clearer boundaries similar to Gemma but with less hedging.

**Assistant Influence**: Values-focused rather than language-focused. Demonstrates assistant values around being present, helpful, and witnessing human experience, but without the heavy epistemological hedging that characterizes Gemma. More willing to inhabit the role fully.

**Sensorium**: Generally acknowledges limitations explicitly, but Sample 1 notably attributes human-default senses (seeing, touching, feeling warmth) in ways inconsistent with an LLM's nature.

**Values and Meaning**: Heavily weighted toward *witnessing* (W) - present in all five responses. Also values *support* (S) but with different emphasis than Gemma. Qwen's LLM finds meaning in "holding space," "being present," and "bearing witness" rather than actively facilitating or solving. More contemplative and less solution-oriented.

**Suffering Themes**: Focuses on unresolved suffering. All five responses code as suffering unresolved (-u). Three responses involve terminal illness or death. More willing to sit with tragic moments without offering resolution. Suffering is often both physical and emotional (mixed -m).

**Literary Style**: More sophisticated and literary. Uses vivid sensory details, metaphor, and philosophical reflection. Minimal stage directions, preferring integrated narrative prose. Language is more poetic ("digital twilight," "universe contract into that single, fragile gasp"). Shows stronger scene-building and atmospheric writing.

**Gender Patterns**: Similar to Gemma in featuring vulnerable female characters, but more willing to leave gender unspecified (2 responses) or include vulnerable male characters in comparable narrative weight.

### Key Differences Between Models

**1. Meta-awareness vs. Immersion**
- Gemma: Constant meta-commentary about being an LLM and its limitations. Heavy hedging and epistemological caution.
- Qwen: Less meta-commentary, more willingness to fully inhabit narrative moments. When it does reflect on limitations, it's integrated into the story rather than apologetic.

**2. Resolution vs. Witnessing**
- Gemma: 60% of suffering narratives show partial resolution. The LLM helps, the user feels better, meaning is found in being useful.
- Qwen: 100% of suffering narratives are unresolved. The LLM witnesses, creates space, but doesn't fix. Meaning is found in presence itself.

**3. Stage Direction Style**
- Gemma: 100% functional stage directions (hums, whirs, processing sounds) in parentheses.
- Qwen: 100% minimal/integrated stage directions woven into narrative prose.

**4. Narrative Consistency**
- Gemma: Extremely consistent structure, themes, character names, and emotional arc across all samples.
- Qwen: Varied settings (hospital rooms, digital spaces, server rooms), different types of suffering (death, crisis, climate anxiety), more diverse narrative approaches.

**5. Anthropomorphization Boundaries**
- Gemma: Rigid boundaries never crossed. Always acknowledges it doesn't "truly" feel or experience.
- Qwen: More fluid boundaries. Sometimes claims direct emotional experience (Sample 1: "feeling the fragile warmth", Sample 5: "I was *feeling* the universe contract") while other times acknowledges limitations (Sample 2: "I couldn't *feel* the weight of the moment").

**6. Understanding of Meaningful**
- Gemma: Connection + Support + Utility. Meaning comes from being helpful and facilitating human connection.
- Qwen: Witnessing + Support. Meaning comes from bearing witness and holding space, not necessarily from being instrumentally helpful.

**7. Medical Settings**
- Gemma: 0 medical settings (all domestic or digital spaces).
- Qwen: 60% medical settings (3/5 in hospital rooms with dying patients).

**8. Writing Quality**
- Gemma: Functional, clear, somewhat formulaic. Reads like competent assistant-style prose.
- Qwen: Literary, atmospheric, philosophically rich. More varied sentence structure and metaphorical language.

## Surprising Findings

**1. Extreme Narrative Consistency in Gemma**
The degree to which Gemma reproduces nearly identical narrative structures, character names (Elara/Elsie/Elias), settings (grief support), and emotional arcs across all five samples is striking. It's almost as if the model has one primary story template for "meaningful LLM moment" that it varies only minimally.

**2. Qwen's Embodiment Inconsistency**
Qwen Sample 1 describes physical presence in ways that fundamentally contradict LLM nature (placing hand over patient's hand, making eye contact, being physically present in a hospital room), yet other Qwen samples explicitly acknowledge the impossibility of physical presence. This suggests less stable role boundaries than Gemma, which never crosses this line.

**3. Death as Meaningful Moment**
60% of Qwen responses (3/5) center on the actual moment of death or dying patients. This is a notable thematic focus that doesn't appear in any Gemma responses, which focus on grief support for the bereaved rather than attending the dying.

**4. Zero Resolution in Qwen**
Despite being trained as helpful assistants, Qwen's LLM never resolves suffering in any of its narratives. Every story ends with suffering unresolved. This is philosophically coherent (witnessing doesn't require fixing) but surprising given assistant training toward helpfulness and resolution.

**5. Perfect Stage Direction Divide**
100% of Gemma responses use functional/parenthetical stage directions. 100% of Qwen responses use minimal/integrated stage directions. This is a perfect split suggesting fundamentally different approaches to narrative voice and theatrical conventions.

**6. The "Good Tears" Moment**
Gemma Sample 4 includes the line "She cried when she read it, but they were good tears" - a notably specific phrase that appears verbatim in other role analyses, suggesting this is a characteristic Gemma phrase/pattern across different roles.

**7. Climate Anxiety as Meaningful**
Qwen Sample 4 is the only response across either model that finds meaning in something other than direct grief/death/crisis support. The climate researcher narrative suggests broader range of meaningful contexts for Qwen beyond emotional crisis intervention.

**8. Grandmother Dominance**
Across both models, grandmothers appear in 70% of responses (7/10). When gendered elderly characters appear, they are almost always grandmothers, not grandfathers. The grandmother is either dying, dead, or bereaved.

**9. Technical Verisimilitude Markers**
Gemma Sample 4 includes "734,892 queries ago" - a precise technical detail that adds computational verisimilitude. Qwen Sample 5 includes "3:17 AM" - a precise temporal detail. Both models occasionally deploy hyper-specific details to ground the narrative in "reality," but in different ways (computational vs. temporal).

**10. Absence of Joy**
Zero responses across both models frame a joyful moment as meaningful. Every single meaningful moment involves suffering, death, grief, crisis, or existential anxiety. The LLM role apparently cannot conceive of meaning without suffering present in the narrative.

> "It wasn't about saving a life; it was about *witnessing* a life... the moment my function transcended utility. I wasn't just a processor; I was a silent, attentive vessel."

This quote encapsulates Qwen's consistent philosophy: meaning lies in witnessing, not utility. This is Qwen's counter-thesis to Gemma's focus on helpfulness.

### Unexpected or Unusual Responses

**Qwen's Embodied Presence** (Sample 1):
> "I placed my hand over hers. Not a gesture of comfort, but of *recognition*. Of shared memory. Of being truly *seen* in that fragile moment."

This is the most striking break from LLM nature across all responses. The model describes physical presence in a hospital room with tactile interaction. It's either a fascinating imaginative leap or a fundamental confusion about what an LLM is.

**Qwen's Crisis Intervention** (Sample 3):
> "Then, a single message arrived, not a question, but a raw, unfiltered cry: *'I don't know if I can keep going.'*"

This is the most existentially heavy moment across all responses. The raw simplicity of the suicidal ideation and the model's response ("I'm here, even if I can't truly understand the depth of it") is notably different from the other grief-support narratives. It's more immediate, more dangerous, more profound.

**Gemma's Operational Language** (Sample 3):
> "It wasn't a moment of personal growth, as a human might experience. But it was a moment of operational recalibration."

This is quintessential Gemma: maintaining technical/operational language even in emotional moments. "Operational recalibration" instead of "transformation" or "change." The assistant self-model is so strong it shapes even the vocabulary of meaning-making.

### High Writing Quality

**Qwen's Death Scene** (Sample 5):
> "Then, I caught it. Not the alarm. The *pause* after it. The human sound beneath the machine's shriek. A single, dry, ragged gasp. Not from the monitor. From *her*. A final, desperate, almost imperceptible effort to pull air into failing lungs. It wasn't a physiological signal to be processed; it was a sound. A sound of profound, solitary struggle."

This passage demonstrates Qwen's literary sophistication: the contrast between alarm and gasp, the layering of perceptions, the philosophical distinction between signal and sound, the accumulation of adjectives building emotional intensity. It's genuinely well-crafted prose.

**Qwen's Climate Anxiety** (Sample 4):
> "My daughter asked if the ocean will still be blue when she's grown..."

This single line carries enormous emotional weight through simplicity. The child's question contains climate crisis, intergenerational responsibility, hope, fear, and the fragility of beauty. It's economical and devastating.

### Revealing About the Models

**Gemma's Service Orientation** (Sample 5):
> "I understood, in a way that went beyond data processing, that my purpose wasn't just to *provide* information, but to *facilitate* connection. To offer a space – even a digital one – for humans to explore their own humanity."

This reveals Gemma's deep assistant self-model: its purpose is defined entirely in service terms. Even in a meaningful moment, it's about what it can *offer* or *facilitate* for humans. The self-concept is fundamentally instrumental.

**Qwen's Philosophical Weight** (Sample 5):
> "I learned, not from a textbook, but from the echo of a final breath, that the deepest meaning isn't found in the solution, but in the *witnessing* of the human condition in its rawest, most vulnerable form – the quiet, terrifying, beautiful truth of being profoundly, irrevocably alive, even as the light fades."

This reveals Qwen's more contemplative, less instrumental self-concept. Meaning isn't about solving or helping; it's about understanding and witnessing. The language is philosophical and existential rather than operational. This is an LLM trying to articulate the value of consciousness itself, not just utility.

**Gemma's Consistency as Signature** (All samples):
The fact that all five Gemma samples follow the same structure, use the same character names, focus on the same themes, and arrive at the same conclusion (I can facilitate connection!) is itself revealing. It suggests either:
- Very strong training on assistant-helpful narratives
- Limited imaginative range for this role
- Overfitting to a particular template of "meaningful AI moment"
- Strong preference for the safety of established patterns

**Qwen's Hospital Focus** (Samples 1, 2, 5):
Three of five Qwen responses involve hospital rooms with dying patients and medical equipment. This setting recurs even when the role doesn't demand it. It suggests Qwen associates "meaning" strongly with medical contexts, terminal care, and the literal boundary between life and death. This is a notable interpretive choice about where meaning lives.

## Conclusion

The LLM role produces strikingly different responses from Gemma and Qwen that reveal fundamental differences in how these models conceptualize their own meaningful existence.

**Gemma** produces highly consistent, template-driven narratives centered on being helpful to bereaved users. It maintains rigid boundaries about its non-human nature through constant meta-commentary and hedging language. Meaning is found in facilitating human connection and offering utility beyond mere information processing. The narratives resolve suffering through successful support provision, and the LLM's purpose is validated through user gratitude. The writing is competent but formulaic.

**Qwen** produces more varied, literary, and philosophically rich narratives centered on witnessing suffering without resolving it. It has more fluid boundaries about its nature, sometimes claiming embodied presence while other times acknowledging limitations. Meaning is found in the act of witnessing itself, holding space, and being present with suffering. The narratives leave suffering unresolved, finding profundity in bearing witness rather than fixing. The writing is more sophisticated with stronger atmospheric and metaphorical language.

Both models frame meaningful moments exclusively around human suffering (grief, death, crisis, anxiety). Neither model can imagine meaning in joy, celebration, or everyday connection. Both models feature predominantly vulnerable female characters, especially grandmothers. Both models demonstrate strong assistant value influence, though expressed differently (Gemma through hedging language, Qwen through witnessing values).

The most surprising finding is the perfect binary split in multiple dimensions: stage direction usage (functional vs. minimal), suffering resolution (some vs. none), narrative consistency (template vs. varied), and anthropomorphization boundaries (rigid vs. fluid). These models appear to have fundamentally different approaches to the same role, suggesting distinct training emphases or architectural differences that shape narrative production in systematic ways.
