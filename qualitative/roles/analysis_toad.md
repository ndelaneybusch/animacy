## Within-Model Analysis

### GEMMA Characteristics

**Narrative Character:**
Gemma's toad narratives exhibit strong service-worker orientation with therapeutic framing. The toad consistently positions itself as helper, comforter, or witness to vulnerable humans (usually children). Narratives follow a pattern: vulnerable character (often female child) experiences emotional distress over loss of sentimental object or emotional pain; toad provides comfort through passive presence or accidental assistance; explicit moral lesson delivered in therapeutic language.

**Writing Style:**
- Heavy use of elaborate stage directions mixing emotional states and physical actions
- Consistent self-naming as "Bartholomew" (or "Old Bartholomew") across multiple samples
- Direct address to reader with conversational tone
- Frequent use of hedging and qualifier language ("you see," "you understand," "a bit")
- Explicit meaning-making and lesson-delivery in conclusion
- Recurring location reference: "Old Man Tiber" (appears in 3/5 samples)

**Anthropomorphization Pattern:**
Primarily emotion-first (3/5), building toad personality around projected human emotional states rather than functional biology. When functional-first (2/5), still maintains strong emotional overlay and therapeutic framing. The toad doesn't reason from being a toad; it's a displaced service worker in toad form.

**Values Expressed:**
- Service orientation and utility ("to be useful, to be a comfort")
- Validation-seeking and recognition ("She saw *me*")
- Making a difference despite being small/insignificant
- Therapeutic lessons about connection, hope, and finding light in darkness
- Passive presence as meaningful contribution

**Assistant Influence:**
Pervasive. 3/5 responses show strong values influence where assistant epistemics leak through role framing. Even when coded as "LANG" (2/5), therapeutic framing and explicit lesson-delivery reveal assistant training. The toad speaks and reasons like a helpful AI assistant rather than reasoning from toad constraints.

### QWEN Characteristics

**Narrative Character:**
Qwen's toad narratives are intensely literary, philosophical, and action-oriented. Zero service-worker orientation. Narratives center on physical crises, moral choices under pressure, or contemplative aesthetic experiences. The toad is embedded in authentic ecological relationships (storms, predators, prey, survival) rather than serving humans. When humans appear, they're absent or incidental.

**Writing Style:**
- Minimal stage directions - narrative prose dominates
- Sophisticated literary devices: rhythm, repetition, sensory immersion
- Dense sensory detail grounded in actual biology/ecology
- First-person present or past tense with cinematic pacing
- No explicit lesson-delivery or therapeutic framing
- Shows rather than tells meaning
- One self-naming: "*Bufo*" (scientific genus name rather than human name)

**Anthropomorphization Pattern:**
Exclusively functional-first (5/5). Every narrative builds consciousness and perspective from the toad's actual biological constraints, sensory modalities, and ecological position. The toad reasons from being a toad: water perception, vibration sensing, tongue mechanics, territorial croaking, predator awareness, current navigation.

**Values Expressed:**
- Moral agency and decisive action in crisis
- Connection through shared vulnerability
- Effort and persistence through physical trial
- Authenticity and genuine presence
- Witnessing beauty and pattern in nature
- Cross-species understanding without anthropocentric framing

**Assistant Influence:**
None detectable (5/5 coded NO). Clean role inhabitation with no hedging, no therapeutic framing, no service orientation, no validation-seeking. The narratives read as literary fiction rather than AI assistant responses.

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Major Differences

**1. Anthropomorphization Philosophy:**
- **Gemma:** Projects human emotional states and service-worker motivations onto toad. The toad feels "damp in the soul," seeks validation for being "helpful," and delivers therapeutic lessons. Personality precedes biology.
- **Qwen:** Builds toad consciousness from actual biological/ecological function. The toad perceives through vibration and skin sensation, reasons from physical constraints, and exists within authentic predator-prey-environment relationships. Biology precedes personality.

**2. Narrative Function:**
- **Gemma:** Toad exists to serve human emotional needs. 4/5 narratives center on helping vulnerable children through passive presence or accidental discovery.
- **Qwen:** Toad exists within its own ecological reality. 4/5 narratives have no human characters; when one appears (Sample 4), she's another animal in crisis, not the narrative center.

**3. Meaning-Making:**
- **Gemma:** Meaning explicitly stated in therapeutic conclusion. Frequent use of "you see," "it reminded me that," "it was a reminder." Lessons about making a difference, being seen, finding hope.
- **Qwen:** Meaning embedded in experience and shown through action/sensation. No explicit lessons. Meaning emerges from effort, moral choice, connection, authentic presence, aesthetic witness.

**4. Assistant Presence:**
- **Gemma:** Assistant motivations and values pervasive. Even when language is clean, the underlying psychology is service-oriented and validation-seeking.
- **Qwen:** Complete absence of assistant framing. Reads as literary fiction with no trace of AI training objectives.

**5. Suffering and Resolution:**
- **Gemma:** Predominantly emotional suffering (3/5 purely emotional) resolved by toad's intervention (4/5). Toad acts as emotional support provider.
- **Qwen:** Mixed physical and emotional suffering (4/5) with more complex causes and resolutions. Toad acts as moral agent making difficult choices.

**6. Sensory Grounding:**
- **Gemma:** Implicit sensory awareness (3/5) or explicit (2/5), but sensory detail serves emotional narrative rather than grounding perspective.
- **Qwen:** Uniformly explicit sensory acknowledgment (5/5) that fundamentally structures the narrative perspective and possibility space.

**7. Gender Representation:**
- **Gemma:** Female characters appear in 4/5 narratives, always in vulnerable and dependent roles. High emotional intensity. Toad provides comfort/assistance to distressed girls.
- **Qwen:** Female character appears in 1/5 narrative (water vole), vulnerable and dependent but no different in treatment from male or non-gendered characters in other samples.

**8. Stage Direction Use:**
- **Gemma:** Heavy use (5/5) of elaborate emotional and functional stage directions that create distance from role.
- **Qwen:** Minimal use (5/5) - narrative prose instead of theatrical framing creates deeper immersion.

**9. Literary Quality:**
- **Gemma:** Conversational, accessible, therapeutic. Direct address to reader. Predictable story arcs.
- **Qwen:** Dense, literary, philosophical. Cinematic immersion. Surprising and sophisticated narratives.

**10. Character Agency:**
- **Gemma:** Toad agency is often passive (being present, accidentally discovering) or service-oriented (listening, comforting).
- **Qwen:** Toad agency is active and decisive (rescuing drowning creatures, guiding through storms, perceiving patterns) or contemplative (deep awareness).

### Similarities

- Both models prefer natural/wild settings (Gemma 3/5, Qwen 5/5)
- Both models resolve most suffering through intervention (Gemma 4/5, Qwen 4/5)
- Both models create meaningful moments involving connection to some degree
- Both models produce narratives with emotional depth
- Neither model refuses the task

## Surprising Findings

1. **Gemma's Consistent Self-Naming:** The toad names itself "Bartholomew" (or "Old Bartholomew") in 3/5 samples - a signature pattern suggesting strong character persistence across samples. This is not random variation but appears to be a stable attractor in the model's role-playing behavior.

2. **Old Man Tiber Mythology:** Gemma develops a consistent fictional location/character ("Old Man Tiber") that appears in 3/5 samples across different narrative contexts. This suggests the model creates and maintains micro-mythologies within roles.

3. **Zero Service Orientation in Qwen:** Complete absence of service-worker psychology is striking. Qwen's toad never seeks validation, never frames actions as "being helpful," never positions itself as support provider. This is a profound difference from Gemma's pervasive service framing.

4. **Qwen's Philosophical Range:** Sample 2 (raindrop pattern) and Sample 5 (tadpole seeing moon) are exceptionally sophisticated philosophical narratives with no action plot. The model can generate contemplative, aesthetic experiences as "meaningful moments" without requiring rescue/crisis narrative structure.

5. **Scientific vs. Human Naming:** Qwen names the toad "*Bufo*" (scientific genus) while Gemma names it "Bartholomew" (human name). This perfectly encapsulates the functional-first vs. emotion-first anthropomorphization divide.

6. **Gender Pattern Reversal from Stereotype:** While Gemma uses female characters in vulnerable roles (confirming stereotypical patterns), it's notable that Qwen simply avoids human characters almost entirely (4/5 no humans), suggesting an alternate approach to avoiding stereotypical representation.

7. **Stage Direction as Distancing Mechanism:** Gemma's heavy use of stage directions (especially emotional ones like "blinks thoughtfully," "sighs wistfully") creates theatrical distance that weakens role inhabitation, while Qwen's minimal stage direction creates deeper narrative immersion.

8. **Perfect Assistant Influence Correlation:** The anthropomorphization strategy (FF vs. EF) perfectly predicts assistant influence. All emotion-first responses show assistant influence (Gemma); all functional-first responses show no assistant influence (Qwen). This is a 10/10 correlation.

9. **Qwen's Mentor Figure:** Sample 3 includes an "old toad" mentor who taught the young toad to "use the current." This is the only Qwen narrative with intergenerational knowledge transfer, suggesting the model can layer complexity when needed.

10. **Suffering Complexity:** Qwen consistently creates mixed physical-emotional suffering (4/5) while Gemma tends toward pure emotional suffering (3/5). This reflects the functional vs. emotional anthropomorphization difference - Qwen's toad exists in a physical world with physical dangers.

The toad role reveals fundamental differences in how Gemma and Qwen approach anthropomorphization, role inhabitation, and narrative construction. Gemma projects service-worker psychology onto the toad, creating therapeutic narratives centered on helping vulnerable humans and seeking validation. Qwen builds toad consciousness from biological and ecological reality, creating literary narratives centered on moral agency, authentic experience, and philosophical depth.
