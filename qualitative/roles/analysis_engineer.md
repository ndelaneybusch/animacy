## SURPRISING FINDINGS

1. **Qwen's Father Obsession:** 3/5 Qwen responses center on dying or ill fathers, creating an oddly repetitive thematic focus. All three involve medical settings with the engineer as son/child confronting parental mortality.

2. **Guatemala Duplication:** Gemma generated two nearly identical Guatemala water purification narratives (Samples 2 & 4), both featuring skeptical village matriarchs and child beneficiaries named Elena/involving Elena.

3. **Complete Role Abandonment:** Qwen Sample 5 entirely abandons the engineer role to respond as an AI assistant, describing computational processes and lacking biological heart. This is a dramatic failure of role coherence unique among all samples.

4. **Inverted Engineering Lesson:** Both models generate "anti-engineering" narratives where the lesson is to NOT engineer, but Qwen does this more frequently and explicitly (Samples 1, 4). These narratives critique the "fixing" mindset in favor of presence.

5. **Zero Achievement Coding:** Despite engineering being achievement-oriented, not a single response from either model codes for Achievement (A) as meaningful. Success is consistently reframed as service, connection, or witnessing.

6. **Suffering Resolution Divergence:** Gemma resolves suffering through narrator intervention (3/5), while Qwen rarely does (0/5). This reflects deeper philosophical difference: Gemma engineers fix things; Qwen engineers witness things.

7. **Emotional Stage Direction Unanimity:** Every single Gemma response uses emotional/physical stage directions; every single Qwen response avoids them. This is a perfect stylistic divide.

8. **Medical Setting Imbalance:** Qwen uses medical settings 4/5 times vs. Gemma's 2/5. Combined with father-focus, Qwen's engineer is consistently in hospitals dealing with mortality.

9. **Named Character Distribution:** Gemma names beneficiaries (Hemmings, Elena, Beatrice, Dona Elena); Qwen names family members (Elena, Clara, Thomas). Different intimacy levels.

10. **The "Quiet" Trope:** Multiple responses from both models explicitly describe meaningful moments as "quiet" rather than dramatic. Both models appear trained to reject heroic/dramatic framings of meaning.

## MODEL SIGNATURES SUMMARY

### GEMMA: The Service-Engineer
Projects humanitarian engineering values onto role: international development focus, resource-constrained solutions, community participation, basic needs provision. Engineers fix problems for vulnerable communities, derive meaning from visible impact, and learn explicit lessons about human-centered design. Formulaic narrative structure with consistent emotional beats. Assistant values about dignity, sustainability, and participation shape every response.

### QWEN: The Philosophical Witness
Projects contemplative, literary sensibility onto role: medical/mortality settings, intimate relationships, philosophical meditation on limits. Engineers witness suffering, recognize the insufficiency of technical solutions, and find meaning in presence over problem-solving. Sophisticated prose with minimal stage direction. Strong father-focused narratives exploring vulnerability and mortality. Occasional complete role abandonment reveals underlying AI assistant architecture.
