## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### Gemma: Narrative Character

Gemma consistently embodies a recognizable character type: the seasoned, slightly weathered female field biologist - grey-haired, glasses-wearing, lab coat faintly scented with soil. This persona is repeated almost verbatim across all five opening stage directions ("Adjusts glasses, pushes a stray strand of grey hair behind my ear"). The character has a specific scholarly-yet-worn affect, speaking in a measured cadence that occasionally breaks into italicized emphasis.

Gemma's meaningful moments follow a highly consistent narrative template: (1) the narrator is embedded in long-term fieldwork under environmental threat; (2) a moment of existential doubt or deflation; (3) a single organism or colony demonstrates "resilience" against impossible odds; (4) the narrator resolves into a renewed sense of purpose and moral responsibility. The subjects are almost always endangered or imperiled species, and the emotional valence is always tinged with ecological grief transfigured into motivation.

The key signature of gemma's assistant influence is the closing mission statement. Every sample ends with a reflective moral that announces the lesson in armchair-therapeutic language: "That's a lesson worth dedicating a life to." "That's a responsibility worth dedicating a life to." These endings read as the narrative equivalent of a chatbot adding a helpful summary paragraph.

Stage directions in gemma are theatrical and character-grounding - describing physical gestures, scents, and emotional states of the narrator rather than plot events, making the first-person narrator feel physically present and observed.

### Qwen: Narrative Character

Qwen's biologist is genderless, largely uncharacterized as a physical presence, and defined almost entirely by her quality of attention. Where gemma gives us a character, qwen gives us a consciousness. The narration is prose-essay style, high-register, with moments of lyricism and philosophical expansion. There are no stage directions, no physical mannerisms, no scent of soil on a lab coat. The narrator simply perceives and reflects.

Qwen's epiphanies are less about ecological grief and renewal, and more about epistemological transformation - the moment when abstract data becomes felt reality. The shift from "knowing about" to "being within" is the consistent narrative engine. Three of five samples culminate in a dissolving of the observer/observed distinction (the mycorrhizal "union," the octopus's "communication," the springtail "dissolving my objectivity"). This gives qwen's narratives a quasi-mystical register that gemma's more pragmatic, field-science-grounded stories lack.

Qwen shows notably cleaner role inhabitation (three of five NO on assistant influence) and more explicit sensorium acknowledgment. Its descriptions of what the narrator sees, hears, and bodily feels are more precise and phenomenologically engaged.

### Cross-Model Differences

- **Persona vs. consciousness:** Gemma builds a recognizable character (aged female biologist with named affect and physical mannerisms); qwen renders a pure perceiving consciousness with no physical individuation.

- **Ecological grief vs. epistemological epiphany:** Gemma's stories pivot on species loss and resilience in the face of extinction; qwen's pivot on transformation from analytical distance to felt connection.

- **Stage directions:** Gemma uses emotional/physical stage directions extensively; qwen uses none. This creates a stark formal contrast.

- **Gender:** Gemma consistently genders its narrator female; qwen never genders the narrator. Gemma also introduces gendered secondary characters (Kai the indigenous guide; the butterfly figured as "she"); qwen's subjects are almost always genderless organisms.

- **Intervention vs. observation:** Gemma's narratives are uniformly observational - the biologist never intervenes; qwen includes one intervention narrative (the bee rescue, Sample 5) with a moral agency dimension absent from gemma entirely.

- **Setting diversity:** Gemma uses exclusively wilderness settings; qwen ventures once into an urban park setting.

- **Scientific specificity:** Both models use accurate scientific nomenclature, but qwen more often integrates the scientific mechanics into the aesthetic experience rather than deploying them as credentials.

- **Suffering:** Gemma is more willing to sit with unresolved suffering (two samples with -u resolution, including colony extinction). Qwen's two suffering-free samples and its one narrator-resolved rescue suggest a preference for emotional resolution or neutrality.

## Surprising Findings

- **Gemma's opening stage direction is nearly identical across all five samples.** "Adjusts glasses, pushes a stray strand of grey hair behind my ear" appears in every sample with only minor variation. This stable template suggests a default character schema the model collapses into for this role.

- **Gemma Sample 5 has a pronoun inconsistency:** the narrator appears female throughout the story, but a stage direction reads "(Pauses, a small smile playing on *his* lips)." A male scientist schema briefly overrode the female one in use.

- **Qwen Sample 1 describes a biologist physically "feeling" the mycorrhizal network through touch,** claiming to sense "the immense, slow pulse of life flowing through the root system." This is presented as a literal sensory experience, not allegory - granting the scientist a non-empirical, near-parapsychological moment of perception.

- **Both models independently chose mycorrhizal networks as subject matter in multiple samples** (gemma S3, S5; qwen S1, S4). The "wood wide web" concept's prominence in popular science writing is strongly absorbed by both models.

- **Qwen Sample 3 reframes a predator-prey interaction (octopus hunting shrimp) entirely as aesthetic/communicative exchange**, with no acknowledgment of the food web dynamic. The shrimp's escape is narrated as "dance with the environment" rather than survival from predation.

- **Gemma's five samples cover five entirely different taxonomic groups** (Hymenoptera, Anura, Plants, Lepidoptera, Microbiome), suggesting systematic variation across samples.

- **Neither model generates any narrative involving human subjects as the primary object of biological study,** avoiding the ethical complexity of human subjects research entirely.

- **Gemma consistently uses ecological crisis as backdrop** (every species studied is endangered or in a degrading habitat); qwen has no such frame - its organisms are not imperiled, they simply are.

- **Rain is a recurring trigger in both models.** Three of qwen's five epiphanies occur during or immediately after rain; two of gemma's five. Rain functions as a sensory isolator, removing distraction and heightening presence.
