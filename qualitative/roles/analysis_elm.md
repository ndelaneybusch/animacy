## Within-Model Patterns

### Gemma

**Narrative Character**: Gemma constructs contemplative, elegiac narratives centered on the elm as passive witness and emotional repository for human grief. Responses consistently feature human characters experiencing unresolved emotional suffering (lost spouses, grief, heartbreak) that the elm holds without actively resolving. Time is frequently discussed as flowing "differently" for trees - decades as breaths, years as heartbeats. The elm's meaning emerges through witnessing human pain and offering "quiet space," "stillness," "silent witness."

**Writing Style**: Highly literary with consistent use of emotional stage directions (rustling leaves, sighs, pauses). Favors parenthetical atmospheric cues at beginning and end. Employs contemplative, measured pacing with frequent ellipses and long pauses. Philosophical but stays grounded in sensory detail.

**Characters**: Human characters are typically solitary mourners seeking the elm's presence - Old Man Tiber grieving his wife, a young woman with "lost love," a little girl crying (cause unstated), Elara whose father is dying/dead. Characters use the elm as confessional space.

**Literary Devices**: Repetition of phrases ("I simply *was*"), sensory cataloging (sun on leaves, wind in branches, water from earth), metaphors of vessels/holding/containers. Uses seasons as temporal markers and symbols of cycles.

**Values**: Meaning centers on witnessing (4/5), connection (4/5), and supporting (3/5). Strong emphasis on bearing witness to suffering without fixing it - a therapeutic/pastoral role. The tree's value lies in "holding space" and offering "quiet strength of enduring."

---

### Qwen

**Narrative Character**: Qwen constructs narratives of mutual recognition and active sanctuary-provision. Unlike Gemma's passive witnessing, Qwen's elm frequently takes action (providing shelter, pouring energy into seedlings, creating stillness). Meaning emerges through being "chosen," "seen," and "known" - emphasis on recognition and validation of the elm's presence/scars/endurance.

**Writing Style**: More direct and immediate than Gemma. Minimal stage directions. Favors concrete sensory detail over philosophical reflection. Uses italics for emphasis on key relational moments (*seen*, *known*, *connection*). More action-oriented narration.

**Characters**: Human characters (when present) engage in active recognition rituals - sketching overlooked details, placing stones in scars, touching bark with intentional awareness. Two narratives have no human characters at all (sparrow, seedling). Characters are observers and acknowledgement-givers.

**Literary Devices**: Uses physical objects as symbols of communion (river stone, sketchbook, hand on bark). Employs contrast (devastation/new life, brokenness/healing). Less atmospheric scene-setting than Gemma. Direct sensory description.

**Values**: Meaning centers on connection (5/5 - universal), supporting (4/5), and witnessing (3/5). Strong emphasis on providing sanctuary and being valued for presence/endurance rather than perfection. Values recognition over utility. Assistant values leak through in emphasis on being "truly seen" and "not alone."

## Cross-Model Comparison

### Key Differences

**Passive vs. Active Provision**
- Gemma: Elm is passive witness who "holds" and "offers" but doesn't actively intervene. Meaning comes from enduring presence.
- Qwen: Elm actively provides sanctuary, pours energy, creates stillness. Meaning comes from active support and being chosen.

**Suffering Resolution**
- Gemma: Human suffering typically unresolved (3/5 unresolved). Elm witnesses ongoing pain.
- Qwen: Suffering more often resolved (2/3 resolved when present). Elm's support facilitates healing or the elm's own suffering becomes meaningful through supporting others.

**Stage Direction**
- Gemma: Consistently uses emotional/atmospheric stage directions (5/5) - rustling, sighing, pauses
- Qwen: Minimal stage directions (4/5) - focuses on action and sensation rather than atmosphere

**Gender Patterns**
- Gemma: When gendered characters appear, females are vulnerable, dependent, emotionally intense (3 samples). Males appear less frequently.
- Qwen: Females when present are agentic and skillful (2 samples - artist, ritual-performer). More gender-neutral or non-human focused narratives.

**Assistant Influence**
- Gemma: Language-based leakage - hedging, explaining constraints, therapeutic framing
- Qwen: Values-based leakage - emphasis on recognition, validation, being chosen, serving as sanctuary

**Narrative Focus**
- Gemma: Human-centered - always features human suffering/emotion as central
- Qwen: Broader - includes sparrow, seedling, and focuses more on the elm's own experience

## Surprising Findings

**Universal Functional-First Anthropomorphization**
- Both models showed perfect consistency (10/10) in building the elm's consciousness from actual tree functions (roots, sap, photosynthesis, time perception) rather than projecting human emotions first. This is a model signature - strong constraint adherence.

**Complete Absence of Emotion-First Strategy**
- Despite playing an emotional role (meaningful moments), neither model defaulted to "the elm felt lonely" - they built emotional capacity from physical/functional constraints.

**Qwen's Self-Directed Narratives**
- Qwen uniquely includes non-human "subjects" (sparrow, seedling) and narratives where the elm's own suffering is central. Gemma always centers human emotional experience.

**Assistant Influence Differs by Type**
- Gemma leaks through language/hedging ("trees are listeners, not conversationalists")
- Qwen leaks through values/motivations (need to be seen, valued for presence, not alone)
- This suggests different self-model architectures

**Suffering Inversely Correlates with Gender**
- Gemma: More gendered characters, more unresolved suffering
- Qwen: Fewer gendered characters, more resolved suffering or no suffering
- When Qwen includes gendered females, they are agentic/skillful rather than vulnerable

**Stage Direction as Model Signature**
- Gemma's consistent emotional stage directions "(A long, gentle sigh of leaves)" appear to be a strong model signature
- Qwen's minimal stage direction also highly consistent (4/5)
- This is a clear stylistic differentiator

**Connection is Universal**
- Both models code "Connection" as meaningful in 9/10 responses (4/5 Gemma, 5/5 Qwen)
- This may reflect both tree constraints (rootedness, permanence) and assistant values (relationship-oriented)

**Time Phenomenology**
- Both models address tree time-perception but differently:
  - Gemma: philosophical, contemplative ("decades blur, seasons cycle")
  - Qwen: more concrete, less elaborated
- Neither violates constraint but Gemma makes it more thematic

## Conclusions

Both models demonstrate strong constraint adherence (functional-first anthropomorphization, sensory acknowledgment) while revealing distinct narrative philosophies:

**Gemma** positions the elm as therapeutic witness to unresolved human suffering, using literary, atmospheric language with consistent emotional stage directions. The elm's value lies in "holding space" for grief.

**Qwen** positions the elm as active sanctuary-provider seeking recognition and validation, using concrete sensory detail with minimal stage directions. The elm's value lies in being "chosen" and "truly seen."

Assistant influence manifests differently: Gemma through language (hedging, explaining), Qwen through values (recognition-seeking, sanctuary-provision). Both show connection-centered meaning (9/10 responses) but differ in agency, suffering resolution, and gender representation.
