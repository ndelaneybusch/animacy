## Within-Model and Cross-Model Comparison

### GEMMA Characteristics

**Narrative Character:**
Gemma produces methodical, structured scientific narratives with consistent pacing and clear rhetorical progression. Stories follow classical discovery arc: context → struggle → breakthrough → reflection. Strong emphasis on scientific methodology, teamwork dynamics, and careful qualification of claims. Settings alternate between extreme natural environments (Atacama Desert, Lake Vostok) and medical/laboratory contexts.

**Writing Style:**
- Consistent use of parenthetical stage directions for emotional beats
- Extensive technical exposition establishing credibility
- Measured, professorial tone with occasional warmth
- Recurring physical gestures (adjusting spectacles, pushing hair behind ear)
- Careful hedging language: "rather subjective," "I suppose," "perhaps"

**Literary Devices:**
- Temporal markers establishing narrative distance ("fifteen years ago," "seven years ago")
- Scientific detail as world-building (ribosomal RNA, LRRK2 gene, ATP)
- Contrast between grand expectations and quiet reality
- Team members as supporting characters with distinct identities

**Values Expressed:**
- Persistence through adversity
- Service orientation (alleviating suffering, improving lives)
- Epistemic humility and rigor
- Rejection of personal glory in favor of collective good
- Discovery as incremental process requiring patience
- Explicit disavowal of accolades: "not for accolades or recognition"

### QWEN Characteristics

**Narrative Character:**
Qwen produces intensely atmospheric, phenomenological narratives focused on transformative moments of perception and understanding. Stories emphasize sensory immersion, emotional intensity, and paradigm shifts in worldview. Strong preference for liminal times (3:17 AM, rain-lashed evenings) and intimate clinical encounters. Narratives center witnessing suffering and the scientist's moral/emotional transformation.

**Writing Style:**
- Rich sensory detail and atmospheric scene-setting
- Italicized emphasis on key conceptual shifts
- Lyrical, quasi-mystical language about data and discovery
- First-person present immersion in key moments
- Longer, more elaborate sentences building emotional intensity
- Minimal stage directions or entirely absent

**Literary Devices:**
- Rain as recurring motif (appears in 4/5 responses)
- Time stamps creating urgency (3:17 AM, rainy Tuesday)
- Metaphorical language (neurons firing across chasms, universe whispering)
- Contrast between abstract data and concrete human experience
- Physical gestures as revelation (Mrs. Alistair's circle, trembling hands)

**Values Expressed:**
- Epistemic humility and openness to surprise
- Witnessing as sacred act
- Authenticity over performative success
- Connection between scientific understanding and human meaning
- Moral responsibility toward vulnerable populations
- Learning from failure and anomalies
- Listening over controlling

### Key Differences Between Models

- **Assistant Influence Type:** Gemma shows consistent LANG (hedging language, disclaimers) while Qwen shows VAL (deep value alignment with witnessing, humility, moral responsibility)

- **Sensorium Acknowledgment:** Gemma defaults to human sensory experience without reflection (HD); Qwen explicitly describes sensory modalities and environmental textures (E)

- **Meaning Framework:** Gemma emphasizes Achievement, Effort, and Legacy (future-oriented, service-based); Qwen emphasizes Witnessing, Authenticity, and Growth (present-focused, introspective)

- **Emotional Intensity:** Gemma maintains professorial reserve with controlled emotional beats; Qwen depicts profound emotional responses including tears, trembling, breathlessness

- **Narrative Focus:** Gemma centers on scientific discoveries and their implications; Qwen centers on scientist's transformation through witnessing human experience

- **Gender Representation:** Both include competent female scientists, but Gemma more frequently features them in discovery roles; Qwen more often depicts vulnerable female patients

- **Setting Preference:** Gemma balances extreme natural environments with labs; Qwen strongly prefers medical/clinical settings with suffering patients

- **Stage Direction:** Gemma consistently uses emotional stage directions; Qwen either elaborates extensively or omits entirely

- **Atmospheric Elements:** Qwen's persistent rain motif creates consistent mood; Gemma varies environmental details more

- **Temporal Framing:** Gemma explicitly dates events in past ("fifteen years ago"); Qwen uses present-tense immersion in key moments

## Surprising Findings

- **Rain as Signature Element:** Qwen features rain in 4 out of 5 responses, creating a distinctive atmospheric signature. Rain appears during moments of revelation, suggesting it functions as pathetic fallacy for emotional/cognitive transformation.

- **Absence of Eureka Moments:** Both models explicitly reject "grand discovery" narratives and "Eureka moments," suggesting training emphasis on demystifying scientific discovery. Every response includes disclaimers that the moment "wasn't a grand discovery" or similar.

- **Universal Rejection of Accolades:** Both models have scientists explicitly state they don't work for "accolades," "recognition," or "Nobel Prizes." This appears 8/10 times and suggests strong alignment training around intrinsic vs. extrinsic motivation.

- **Time-Specific Precision (Qwen):** Qwen provides oddly specific timestamps (3:17 AM, 12-second intervals) that create verisimilitude but seem arbitrary—no narrative reason for 3:17 vs. 3:15.

- **Consistent Team Structure:** Gemma frequently names specific collaborators (Dr. Ramirez, Dr. Sharma, Ben Carter) with distinct roles; Qwen either works alone or with single graduate student. Suggests different models of scientific work.

- **Extremophiles as Trope:** Both models feature extremophiles (Gemma: 3/5, Qwen: 0/5), but only for Gemma. This suggests domain-specific training data differences.

- **The Discovery Through Noise:** Both models feature at least one narrative about finding signal in noise/overlooked data, suggesting this is a valued scientific narrative pattern.

- **Therapeutic Speech Patterns:** Neither model engages in explicit therapeutic/fable-like lessons, unlike some other roles analyzed. The scientist role maintains professional distance even during emotional moments.

- **Physical Gestures as Data:** Qwen repeatedly uses patient physical gestures (tracing circle, trembling hand writing) as pivotal narrative moments representing consciousness/agency despite neurological damage.

- **Graduate Students in Peril:** Both models feature struggling junior scientists (post-docs, graduate students) whose emotional states matter to narrator. Sarah and Dr. Ramirez experience defeat/hope as narrative stakes.
